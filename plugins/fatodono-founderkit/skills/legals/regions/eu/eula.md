## Document

- **Purpose:** Patterns of the root End User License Agreement that EU law makes invalid. This block adds no text to
  the document; its counts are the root's.

## Avoid on root sections

### telemetry

- **Avoid:** framing telemetry as the user's consent: 4/12. The EDPB Guidelines 05/2020 on
  consent: consent inside a licence is neither separate nor freely given. Reading data from the
  device needs valid consent unless it is strictly necessary for the service (ePrivacy Directive
  Art. 5(3)).

### software-changes

- **Avoid:** changing a digital product for consumers without telling them: part of the about
  7/13 that change or stop the software at any time without notice or liability. Directive (EU)
  2019/770 Art. 19: a change needs a reason the contract states, no cost to the consumer and
  clear information, and a change with a negative impact needs notice in advance on a durable
  medium and a right to end the contract; Art. 22 makes terms that depart from this non-binding
  on the consumer. For business users it stays a pattern.
