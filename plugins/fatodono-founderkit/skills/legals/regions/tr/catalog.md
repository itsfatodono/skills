# tr catalog

Items only the `tr` region needs, in the row format of `helpers/catalog.md`.

| Id | Title | Kind | Family | Trigger question | Skeleton |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tr-distance-sales | Mesafeli Satış Sözleşmesi (distance sales contract) | document | commerce | Do you sell goods, digital content or services to consumers in Turkey through your own website or app (not only through a marketplace)? | `regions/tr/tr-distance-sales.md` |
| tr-pre-contract-info | Ön Bilgilendirme Formu (pre-contract information form) | document | commerce | Do you sell goods, digital content or services to consumers in Turkey through your own website or app (not only through a marketplace)? The same question as `tr-distance-sales`: the buyer confirms this form before that contract, so a yes needs both. | `regions/tr/tr-pre-contract-info.md` |
| tr-kvkk-notice | KVKK Aydınlatma Metni (privacy notice under KVKK Art. 10) | document | privacy-notices | Do you collect personal data from customers, users or visitors in Turkey (accounts, orders, contact forms, analytics, app identifiers)? KVKK Art. 10 requires the notice whenever personal data is collected, so for a product used in Turkey the answer is almost always yes. It is a separate text from the privacy policy, and consent texts are a separate item. | `regions/tr/tr-kvkk-notice.md` |
| tr-explicit-consent | KVKK Açık Rıza Metni (explicit consent text under KVKK Art. 5(1), 6(3)(a), 9(6)(a)) | document | privacy-notices | Do you process personal data of customers or users in Turkey for something no other KVKK condition covers: marketing messages or profiling, ad tracking, special-category data without another legal condition, or a one-off transfer abroad? Each such purpose needs explicit consent, asked in a text separate from the KVKK notice. Regular transfers abroad and processing for the service itself never need it. | `regions/tr/tr-explicit-consent.md` |
