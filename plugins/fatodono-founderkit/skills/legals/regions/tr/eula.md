## Document

- **Purpose:** Patterns of the root End User License Agreement that Turkish law makes invalid. This block adds no text to
  the document; its counts are the root's.

## Avoid on root sections

### telemetry

- **Avoid:** framing telemetry as the user's consent: 4/12. The KVKK Board's decision "Hizmetin
  Açık Rıza Şartına Bağlanması" holds that consent made a condition of a service is not freely
  given, and its guide "Açık Rıza" (KVKK Yayınları No: 98, June 2025, p. 10) asks for consent per
  purpose.
