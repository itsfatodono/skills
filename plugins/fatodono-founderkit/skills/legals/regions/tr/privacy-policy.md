## Document

- **Purpose:** Patterns of the root Privacy Policy that Turkish law makes invalid. This block adds no text to
  the document; its counts are the root's.

## Avoid on root sections

### intro-scope

- **Avoid:** treating use of the product as consent to the policy: 6/32. KVKK Art. 3(1)(a)
  defines explicit consent as specific, informed and given with free will, and the Aydınlatma
  Yükümlülüğü Tebliğ (Art. 5(1)(f)) keeps informing apart from taking consent.

### sensitive

- **Avoid:** tying sensitive data to consent given by choosing to provide it: 5/9. KVKK Art. 6
  rests special-category data on explicit consent or another condition it lists, and Art.
  3(1)(a) makes explicit consent a statement about a specific subject; providing the data is not
  one.
