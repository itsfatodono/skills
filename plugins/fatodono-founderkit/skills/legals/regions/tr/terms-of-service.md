## Document

- **Purpose:** Patterns of the root Terms of Service that Turkish law makes invalid. This block adds no text to
  the document; its counts are the root's.

## Avoid on root sections

### privacy

- **Avoid:** treating use of the product as agreement to the privacy policy: about 12/23. KVKK
  Art. 3(1)(a) defines explicit consent as specific, informed and given with free will, and the
  Board's decision "Hizmetin Açık Rıza Şartına Bağlanması" holds that consent made a condition of
  a service is not freely given.

### communications

- **Avoid:** taking consent to marketing messages from the user giving a phone number: part of
  the 8/12 that cover text messages. Law 6563 Art. 6 and the Ticari İletişim ve Ticari Elektronik
  İletiler Hakkında Yönetmelik: commercial electronic messages need the recipient's prior consent
  (onay). Informational messages stay a pattern.
