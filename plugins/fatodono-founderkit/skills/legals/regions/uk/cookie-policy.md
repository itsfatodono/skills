## Document

- **Purpose:** Patterns of the root Cookie Policy that UK law makes invalid. This block adds no text to
  the document; its counts are the root's.

## Avoid on root sections

### intro

- **Avoid:** treating visiting or continuing to use the service as agreeing to cookies: 2/20.
  PECR reg. 6 with UK GDPR Art. 4(11), and the ICO's guidance on storage and access
  technologies: going on using a site is not consent.

### browser-controls

- **Avoid:** offering browser settings as the only control for cookies that need consent: 2/19.
  PECR reg. 6 and the ICO's guidance on storage and access technologies: browser settings do not
  give valid consent to non-essential cookies.

### changes

- **Avoid:** treating continued use after a change as acceptance: 2/11. A change that adds
  cookies needing consent needs a new choice in the consent tool (PECR reg. 6, ICO guidance on
  storage and access technologies).
