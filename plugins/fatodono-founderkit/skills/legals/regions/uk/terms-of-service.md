## Document

- **Purpose:** Patterns of the root Terms of Service that UK law makes invalid. This block adds no text to
  the document; its counts are the root's.

## Avoid on root sections

### privacy

- **Avoid:** treating use of the product as agreement to the privacy policy: about 12/23. UK
  GDPR Arts. 4(11) and 7(2) and the ICO's guidance on consent: consent is never inferred from use
  or bundled with acceptance of the terms.
