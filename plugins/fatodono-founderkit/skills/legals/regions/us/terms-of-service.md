## Document

- **Purpose:** Patterns of the root Terms of Service that US law makes invalid. This block adds no text to
  the document; its counts are the root's.

## Avoid on root sections

### communications

- **Avoid:** taking consent to marketing texts from the user giving a phone number: part of the
  8/12 that cover text messages. 47 CFR 64.1200(a)(2) (TCPA): marketing texts sent with an
  autodialer need the user's prior express written consent. A number given for the account still
  covers informational texts, which stays a pattern.
