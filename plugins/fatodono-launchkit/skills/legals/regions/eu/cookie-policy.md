## Document

- **Purpose:** Patterns of the root Cookie Policy that EU law makes invalid. This block adds no text to
  the document; its counts are the root's.

## Avoid on root sections

### intro

- **Avoid:** treating visiting or continuing to use the service as agreeing to cookies: 2/20.
  ePrivacy Directive Art. 5(3) with GDPR Art. 4(11), the CJEU in C-673/17 *Planet49*, and the
  EDPB Guidelines 05/2020 on consent: scrolling or going on browsing is not consent.

### browser-controls

- **Avoid:** offering browser settings as the only control for cookies that need consent: 2/19.
  ePrivacy Directive Art. 5(3) and the CNIL's cookie guidelines and recommendation (2020):
  browser settings do not give valid consent to non-essential cookies.

### changes

- **Avoid:** treating continued use after a change as acceptance: 2/11. A change that adds
  cookies needing consent needs a new choice in the consent tool (ePrivacy Directive Art. 5(3),
  EDPB Guidelines 05/2020).
