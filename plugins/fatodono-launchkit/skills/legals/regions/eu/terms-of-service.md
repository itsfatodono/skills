## Document

- **Purpose:** Patterns of the root Terms of Service that EU law makes invalid. This block adds no text to
  the document; its counts are the root's.

## Avoid on root sections

### privacy

- **Avoid:** treating use of the product as agreement to the privacy policy: about 12/23. GDPR
  Arts. 4(11) and 7(2) and the EDPB Guidelines 05/2020 on consent: consent is never inferred from
  use or bundled with acceptance of the terms.

### service-changes

- **Avoid:** changing a digital product for consumers without telling them: part of the about
  12/21 that change or stop it at any time without notice or liability. Directive (EU) 2019/770
  Art. 19: a change needs a reason the contract states, no cost to the consumer and clear
  information, and a change with a negative impact needs notice in advance on a durable medium
  and a right to end the contract; Art. 22 makes terms that depart from this non-binding on the
  consumer. For business users it stays a pattern.
