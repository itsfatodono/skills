## Document

- **Purpose:** Patterns of the root Cookie Policy that Turkish law makes invalid. This block adds no text to
  the document; its counts are the root's.

## Avoid on root sections

### intro

- **Avoid:** treating visiting or continuing to use the service as agreeing to cookies: 2/20.
  The KVKK Board's guide "Çerez Uygulamaları Hakkında Rehber" (2022): cookies that are not
  strictly necessary need explicit consent given by an active act, and going on browsing is not
  one.

### changes

- **Avoid:** treating continued use after a change as acceptance: 2/11. A change that adds
  cookies needing consent needs a new choice in the consent tool (KVKK Art. 3(1)(a); the Board's
  "Çerez Uygulamaları Hakkında Rehber").
