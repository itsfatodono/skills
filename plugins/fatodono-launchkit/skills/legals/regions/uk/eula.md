## Document

- **Purpose:** Patterns of the root End User License Agreement that UK law makes invalid. This block adds no text to
  the document; its counts are the root's.

## Avoid on root sections

### telemetry

- **Avoid:** framing telemetry as the user's consent: 4/12. UK GDPR Arts. 4(11) and 7(2) and the
  ICO's guidance on consent: consent inside a licence is neither separate nor freely given.
  Reading data from the device needs valid consent unless it is strictly necessary for the
  service (PECR reg. 6).
