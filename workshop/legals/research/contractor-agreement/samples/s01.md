## Document
- Shape: an old base agreement of 14 numbered paragraphs with no headings (only a *Recitals* heading), then fifteen amendments added over the years, each with its own preamble, recitals, headed numbered sections and signature block. Filed as one exhibit, the agreement as amended. The base text has no termination, independent-contractor status, tax, expense, invoicing, indemnity, liability, insurance or non-solicit clause.
- Register: formal legal drafting in the third person with defined roles ("Company", "Consultant"), "shall" throughout, long sentences, a male pronoun for the consultant. The amendments use stock WHEREAS / NOW, THEREFORE wording.
- Recurring elements: the consultant's address redacted as `[**]` in every preamble; conformed `/s/` signatures; defined terms in quotes inside parentheses; each amendment's recitals restate the full chain of earlier amendments by date; each amendment from No. 2 on repeats the same no-conflict, full-force and counterparts clauses word for word.
- Parties: a company engages an individual acting in his own name, a university professor expert in the company's software field, to help improve and develop its software products. The text states a prior consultant agreement between the same parties (this is a "continued engagement"), the consultant's university affiliations, which take precedence, and pay partly in company stock and later options. No officer or board title appears. From general knowledge, not the text: he is a co-founder. He is engaged for defined advisory services, so the sample passes the fit rule.
- Attachments: no exhibits or schedules. Referenced but not included: the superseded earlier consultant agreement, the company's contracts with universities, the stock incentive plan and the standard non-qualified stock option agreement. Amendments No. 1 to No. 15 are included in full.
