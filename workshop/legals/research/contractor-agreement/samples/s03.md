## Document
- Shape: titled agreement, one-paragraph preamble, two "Whereas" recitals, 28 numbered sections (two of them "Intentionally Deleted"), two unnumbered all-caps paragraphs (a warranty disclaimer after section 1, a liability cap after section 18), a blank signature block, then an exhibit that is a title page only. Running footer with the agreement title and page number.
- Register: formal US contract drafting, defined terms in quotes and capitals, long sentences, third person ("Contractor", "Company"). Mostly the contractor's paper: one-way company indemnity, no contractor indemnity, the company may not terminate for convenience, the company carries insurance naming the contractor. Reused-template traces: the governing-law clause calls it a "Settlement Agreement", "Products" is used but never defined, the contractor is once called "Consultant".
- Recurring elements: section numbers with bold run-in headings; lettered sub-clauses; cross-references by section number; survival stated inside each section that survives (15, 18) rather than in one clause; a footnote defining the cost-of-living index.
- Parties: a company engages a firm (a limited liability company); no individual is named as the person who performs the services. Services: information technology support, plus whatever the company directs and the contractor accepts. Stated relationship: the contractor succeeds to a predecessor that served the company and is owed past-due invoices; part of the pay (shares, cash, a note, an NFT held as collateral) settles those invoices, and both sides release claims about the predecessor's work. No officer, board or employment role, and nothing ties the engagement to a departure. Passes the fit rule as an engagement for defined services, though it doubles as a debt settlement.
- Attachments: Exhibit A, form of promissory note: referenced in section 5, only the title page is filed (text not included). Milestones for the additional shares: a separate writing to be agreed later and incorporated (section 2(b)); not attached.
