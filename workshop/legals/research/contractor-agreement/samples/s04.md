## Document
- Shape: short agreement: a logo image on top, title, one-paragraph preamble (no recitals), 13 numbered sections with run-in headings, the last one ("General") split into lettered clauses (a)–(f), a two-column signature block, then three one-page schedules (services, compensation and expenses, contact information). Each page carries a footer with the word-processor file name and a "Confidential - Not for Public Consumption or Distribution" legend.
- Register: plain but formal US contract drafting; defined terms in quotes and capitals ("Client", "Consultant", "Services", "Term"); third person; most sections one to three sentences. Reads as the consultant's paper: the consultant may suspend for non-payment, payments are without offset, the consultant is not liable for good-faith advice, its total liability is capped at fees paid, and it may demand expenses in advance. Silent on termination, ownership of work product, non-solicitation, insurance, taxes and survival.
- Recurring elements: numbered sections with bold run-in headings; defined terms set in the clause that first uses them; operative detail pushed to schedules by cross-reference ("as specified on Schedule 1"); file-name and confidentiality footer on every page.
- Parties: a company (a subsidiary of the filer, an LLC) engages a firm (a public affairs LLC), which signs through a partner; no individual performer is named. Services: government and regulatory affairs for a project: permitting with county officials, work with a federal transport regulator on its rules, and federal and state lobbying on request. No stated relationship with the company beyond this engagement; no officer, board or employment role and nothing ties it to a departure. Passes the fit rule.
- Attachments: Schedule 1, services (text included); Schedule 2, compensation and expenses (text included); Schedule 3, contact information for notices and invoices (text included). No separate confidentiality or invention-assignment agreement is referenced.
