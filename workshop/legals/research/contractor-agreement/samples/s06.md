## Document
- Shape: short agreement: title, one-sentence preamble, three WHEREAS recitals and a NOW, THEREFORE consideration line, then about twenty unnumbered clauses with underlined run-in headings (one recital cross-refers to "Section 2", so numbering was lost or never applied), a signature block (signatures as images), and a one-page Exhibit A listing the consultant's objectives and the members of the client's advisory board. Page numbers only; no footer legend. Yellow highlighting is left on the title, the parties and the compensation figures, a drafting artefact in the filed copy.
- Register: informal, loosely drafted US contract language; defined terms in quotes and capitals, used inconsistently ("Company" is the consultant's firm but is also used for the client; "Objectives" is defined as "Party Objectives"; "Board of Advisors", "Advisory Board" and "Board of Consultants" for one body); third person; several sentences are garbled ("to the extent, and only to the no extent, required"). Reads as the consultant's paper: liability only for misconduct, a one-way indemnity from the client, a no-guarantee-of-results clause and a broad survival clause. Silent on ownership of work product or IP, termination for cause, non-solicitation, insurance, taxes, assignment and dispute resolution.
- Recurring elements: underlined run-in headings; client and consultant referred to by short defined names; fees and share counts written in figures with some amounts repeated in words; S-1 effectiveness and post-offering funding used as triggers in the compensation terms.
- Parties: a company engages a consulting firm and its principal, an individual, as three parties; the firm receives the cash fees and expense reimbursements, the individual receives the shares and signs both as "Consultant" and as president of the firm. Services: management consulting acting as the company's head of sales and marketing (press releases, marketing plans, web and e-commerce content, ad funnels, social media, a product launch plan), plus help with bridge financing and with finding an investment bank for the offering. The recitals describe the role as fulfilling the "expected role of acting Global Head of Sales and Marketing"; this is a fractional functional role, not an officer or board title, and nothing ties it to a departure. The individual is paid partly in the company's restricted stock, so becomes a shareholder. Passes the fit rule.
- Attachments: Exhibit A, duties of consultant (objectives list and advisory board members; text included). No separate confidentiality or invention-assignment agreement, statement of work or compensation schedule.
