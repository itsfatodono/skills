## Document
- Shape: the company's standard consulting agreement form (footer names the form and its version), filed as an 8-K exhibit: a logo, title, one-paragraph preamble (no recitals), 12 numbered sections in capitals, several split into numbered sub-clauses with run-in headings (4.1–4.5, 5.1–5.4, 10.1–10.4, 12.1–12.7), a testimonium and a two-column signature block; then Exhibit A, "Form of Statement of Work", filled in for this deal: its own preamble, 7 numbered fields (project name, project managers, description, scope and deliverables, duration, fees, change requests), two deliverables tables for year 1 and year 2, and its own signature block. Every page carries a "Confidential" legend, the form name and "Page x of 6".
- Register: formal US contract drafting, company-side paper; defined terms in quotes and capitals ("Agreement", "SOW", "Innovations", "Confidential Information", "Forescout Property"); third person, the company by its short name and the other side as "Consultant". Long multi-clause sentences in the master terms; the SOW is plainer and deal-specific. Obligations run almost entirely one way, on the consultant: one-way indemnity, one-way confidentiality, one-way assignment ban, injunctive relief for the company only. No limitation of liability, insurance, warranty disclaimer, publicity, data protection or dispute-escalation clause.
- Recurring elements: numbered sections with run-in headings; cross-references by number and heading in parentheses ("Section 5 (Confidentiality)"), one of which quotes a heading that differs from the one in the text (Section 4); a master agreement that governs SOWs, with fees, scope and duration left to the SOW; confidentiality and form-version footer on every page.
- Parties: a company engages a firm (an LLC) that acts through two named principals, each of whom "may be providing services" and each defined as a "Consultant"; the firm signs through its CEO, the company through the chair of its board. Services: consulting and business development: customer meetings and speaking at company events, sales and marketing support, and a yearly written market and partnering/acquisition analysis by the second principal or another associate of the firm. Relationship stated outside the text: per the filing 8-K, the principal who personally performs the services is a sitting member of the company's board and CEO of the firm, and the agreement was approved by the audit committee and the independent directors as an Item 404 related-party transaction. The agreement text itself names no board role, carries no board duties, D&O cover or separation terms; the only traces are the board-chair signature and board approval of the equity grant. Passes the fit rule (clean services engagement, judged on the text).
- Attachments: Exhibit A, Form of Statement of Work (text included, filled in with the actual deal, including two deliverables tables); the company's 2017 equity incentive plan and its Acceptable Use Policy are referenced, not attached. No separate confidentiality or invention-assignment agreement: both sit in the master terms.
