## Document
- Shape: short agreement, four pages: company name and title on top, a one-paragraph preamble, a WHEREAS recital and a NOW, THEREFORE consideration line, then 10 numbered sections with run-in headings (section 1 split into (a)–(c)). Operative detail sits in Schedule A (services, hours, rate, invoicing, expenses). The filing prints Schedule A inside section 9: the severability sentence breaks off mid-clause, the schedule follows, and the sentence resumes after it, followed by section 10. The testimonium is text; the signature block is a scanned image. The filing labels it EX-10.41 while the page heading reads Exhibit 10.40.
- Register: formal US contract drafting in the company's favour; defined terms in quotes and capitals ("Company", "Consultant", "Consulting Services", "Confidential Information", "Company Intellectual Property"); third person; long sentences, sections of one to six sentences. Obligations fall almost all on the consultant. Only the company gets a confidentiality and IP benefit. Silent on liability caps, indemnity, insurance, warranties, non-solicitation, non-competition, notices, entire agreement, amendment and dispute resolution. Has typos ("truces" for taxes; "effective as" missing "of").
- Recurring elements: numbered sections with run-in headings; scope, time and fees pushed to Schedule A by cross-reference ("as set forth in Schedule A"); page numbers in the footer; executed "under seal".
- Parties: a company (a Nevada corporation, with its subsidiaries) engages an individual who is named together with his LLC ("<person> / <LLC>"), so it is unclear whether the contractor is the person or the firm; the text uses "he or she" and "himself". Services: a compliance framework overview plus general consulting and advisory services on request. Relationship stated: §3 says the consultant "is an independent director of the Company". The text does not appoint him, set board duties, give D&O cover or carry separation terms; it is a clean engagement for defined services by a sitting director, so it passes the fit rule (as s07).
- Attachments: Schedule A, services, time commitment and compensation (text included). No separate confidentiality or invention-assignment agreement is referenced.
