## Document
- Shape: very short agreement on four pages: title, a two-line preamble naming the parties, a one-line WHEREAS giving the effective date, "THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS", then 18 numbered sections with short headings (one number lacks its period), the last being the signature area as a fill-in field block. Page numbers only; no recitals of background, no definitions section, no exhibits.
- Register: plain, everyday contract English, third person, short sentences; defined terms in quotes and capitals but few (Services, Work Product, Parties). Most sections read as a lightly edited free do-it-yourself independent contractor template (headings such as "Business Licenses, Permits, and Certificates", "State and Federal Taxes", "Exclusive Agreement" for the entire-agreement clause, a "Reasonable cause includes" list), with a services description and a broad IP assignment added. Drafting slips left in: an undefined "Engagement", a garbled travel sentence ("All travel between shall be agreed"), convenience termination listed as one of the "reasonable causes", and a confidentiality-sounding heading ("Proprietary Information") over the work-product assignment. Leans toward the client: one-way indemnity from the consultant, full IP and moral-rights assignment with no retained portfolio right, publicity right for the client. Silent on warranties, limitation of liability, insurance, notices, survival, severability, non-solicitation, acceptance criteria beyond client approval, and any statutory notices.
- Recurring elements: parties referred to only as "Client" and "Consultant"; fee written in figures; cross-references to section numbers (Section 9, Section 13); Nevada named for both courts and governing law.
- Parties: a company engages a firm (a corporation, acting through a director who signs) for design services: concept and design consultation for a clothing collection of a stated number of SKUs, delivered as design documents. The filer here is the consultant, not the client; the text states no other relationship between the two companies. A clean services engagement; passes the fit rule.
- Attachments: none; the services, fee and payment trigger are written into sections 1 and 2. No statement of work, compensation schedule, or separate confidentiality or invention-assignment agreement is referenced.
