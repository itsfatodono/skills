## Document
- Shape: letter agreement: a dated letter addressed to the consultant's manager with a salutation, then a preamble that names the text a "Master Consulting Services Agreement", 16 numbered sections with run-in headings (section 15 split into lettered sub-clauses a and b), a sign-and-return request, the company's "Sincerely" signature and the consultant's "Acknowledged, agreed to and accepted" countersignature, then Exhibit A, a statement of work with 3 numbered sections and its own consultant signature line. Running header "Consulting Agreement" and a footer with the company's address, website and page number on every page.
- Register: formal US contract drafting in the third person ("Consultant", "CareCloud"), defined terms in quotes and capitals; long single-paragraph sections. Company paper and one-sided: one-way release and indemnity from the consultant, post-term non-solicitation of employees and of clients, one-year confidentiality tail, no-assignment and no-subcontracting, termination without cause on bare written notice, warranties only from the consultant. Loosely drafted: it uses "Commencement Date" without defining it beside "Agreement Date" and "Effective Date"; the letter date, effective date and signature date all differ; work-for-hire and IP sections overlap. Silent on notices, limitation of liability, insurance, dispute resolution or forum, severability, counterparts and electronic signature, and return of materials.
- Recurring elements: numbered sections with run-in headings; operative commercial detail pushed to Exhibit A by cross-reference ("in accordance with Exhibit A"); post-term periods stated in years with a "shall survive" sentence inside the clause; healthcare-regulatory clauses (patient data, HIPAA, anti-kickback) running through a general services agreement; running header and address footer on every page.
- Parties: a company (a healthcare IT and medical billing provider, "and its affiliates") engages a firm (an LLC) that signs through its manager; the text treats it as a one-person firm (termination on the death or disability of "Consultant's owner") while warranting about "Consultant's employee(s)". Services: technical consulting on developing AI capabilities, on site at the company's office on weekdays with travel to its offshore technology centre. The text states no other relationship with the company: no officer, board or employment role, nothing tied to a departure. The company signs through its HR vice president. Passes the fit rule.
- Attachments: Exhibit A, statement of work (scope, project term, fee schedule; text included). A subcontractor business associate agreement is referenced, to be signed "if necessary" (text not included). No separate invention-assignment agreement.
