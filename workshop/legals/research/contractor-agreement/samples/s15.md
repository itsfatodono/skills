## Document
- Shape: very short agreement on four pages: title, a one-sentence preamble naming both parties with entity form and state, four WHEREAS recitals, a consideration lead-in, then 15 numbered sections with short headings and the signature blocks. Section 1 ("Consulting Terms") carries the whole commercial deal in four unheaded paragraphs (services, working contact, outside activities, contractor status and term; fee; expenses; termination); sections 2 and 3 are one sentence each; sections 4 to 15 are closing boilerplate. Page numbers only; no definitions section, no exhibits.
- Register: formal, old-fashioned contract English, third person; defined terms in quotes and capitals inline ("Services", "Term", "Compensation", "Cause", "Disability"); numbers in words with figures in brackets. Drafting slips left in: a recital refers to the LLC consultant as "he", "act a consultant", "nolo contender", "reasonably action", and the entire-agreement clause names "the Exhibits hereto" though none are attached. Leans toward the consultant: the only indemnity runs from the company to the consultant, and the consultant may work for others. Silent on IP or work-product ownership, confidentiality obligations (only an acknowledgement), warranties, limitation of liability, insurance, taxes, notices, survival, termination by the consultant or for convenience, non-solicitation and any statutory notices.
- Recurring elements: parties referred to as "the Company" and "Consultant" (often without the article); fee and expense thresholds in figures; one US state named for governing law and another state's county for courts.
- Parties: a company engages a firm (a limited liability company, acting through its managing director, who signs) for business, consulting and advisory services and market research on the company's financial and operational matters. The text states no other relationship. The filer's S-1/A states the firm is owned by a person whom the board appointed director and Chief Financial Officer about six weeks after signing, with a separate stock award; this agreement's text carries no officer or board title, D&O cover or board duties, so it is a clean, short services engagement and passes the fit rule.
- Attachments: none. The entire-agreement clause refers to "the Exhibits hereto", but no exhibit, statement of work, compensation schedule or separate confidentiality or invention-assignment agreement is attached or named.
