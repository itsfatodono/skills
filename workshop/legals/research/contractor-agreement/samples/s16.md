## Document
- Shape: one docx in three parts. Part 1 is a removable help page: a key to the form's notation (yellow-bracketed fill-ins, grey italic drafting notes, parentheses for pick-one choices, square brackets for optional multi-choices, "x" for defaults) and a six-step setup checklist. Part 2 is a one-page cover page titled "Independent Contractor Agreement / Statement of Work": a two-column table of eight labelled fields, a one-sentence incorporation line, and a two-column signature block. Part 3 is the "Terms and Conditions": a one-sentence preamble, then ten numbered sections (Services & Restrictions; Payment & Taxes; Intellectual Property; Term & Termination; Confidentiality; Representations & Warranties; Indemnification; Limitation of Liability; General Terms; Definitions) with short bold-headed sub-clauses (1.1, 4.2(a) and so on). Definitions come last, not first. The footer carries the form's name, version 1.0 and the CC BY 4.0 licence; the issuer's live standards page links this file (served from its own uploads folder, dated 2025-05).
- Register: plain modern contract English in short paragraphs, third person; capitalised defined terms (Services, SOW, Work Product, Confidential Information, Applicable Laws, Governing Law, Chosen Courts) defined in the closing section and on the cover page; numbers in words with figures in brackets. Deal-specific values live only on the cover page; the terms hold fixed defaults (a one-year non-solicit, a 15-day cure period, a 60-day insolvency window, 30 days' notice for company convenience termination, a 12-month fee cap). Leans toward the company: confidentiality, warranties and indemnity run from the contractor only, and only the company's liability is waived and capped. Silent on non-compete, exclusivity, conflict-of-interest disclosure beyond a warranty, background IP or licence-back, acceptance testing, late-payment interest, invoice cadence (left to the cover page), data protection, publicity, dispute resolution beyond courts, and any statutory notice (no California Labor Code § 2870 invention notice, no DTSA immunity notice). Drafting slips carried over from Common Paper's other forms: the required-disclosure clause uses "Recipient" and "Discloser", which are not defined here; the Confidential Information definition refers to "each Cover Page" although the form calls it the SOW; the Moral Rights definition uses an undefined "Innovation"; the company's convenience termination is "immediately … by giving thirty (30) days' prior written notice"; the setup checklist speaks of "linked Standard Terms" though the terms are in the same file.
- Recurring elements: parties referred to as "Company" and "Contractor" throughout; cross-references by section number and title in brackets ("Section 5 (Confidentiality)"); version number and licence in the footer; running headers "SOW" and "TERMS AND CONDITIONS".
- Parties: written for a company that engages an outside contractor for services of any kind; the Definitions call the contractor "the individual providing Services", while the cover page's contractor field also asks for a legal business name "if any", so it reads as written for an individual, optionally trading under a business name. No other relationship with the company is contemplated; a clean services engagement, so it passes the fit rule.
- Attachments: the cover page / statement of work is included in full (one SOW per agreement; the terms let the parties amend it in writing, and termination can end "an SOW"). No other exhibit, schedule, compensation table or separate confidentiality or invention-assignment agreement is attached or referenced; confidentiality and IP assignment sit inside the terms. Common Paper's separate Professional Services Agreement is a different form and is not part of this sample.
