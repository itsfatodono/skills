## Document
- Shape: titled agreement, one-paragraph preamble with a one-line recital, 12 numbered sections with run-in headings, most split into lettered sub-clauses that carry their own headings; "(signature page follows)", a signed two-column signature block, Exhibit A (services and compensation), then Amendment No. 1 (later, filed in the same exhibit) with background recitals, seven numbered amendments and its own signature block. Page numbers as "-N-"; the amendment repeats its title as a running footer.
- Register: formal US company-paper drafting (a law-firm consulting template), third person ("Consultant", "the Company"), defined terms in quotes and capitals, long sentences; one-way throughout (the consultant indemnifies, the company's liability is capped, only the company may terminate for convenience until the amendment makes it mutual). The liability limit and the whole arbitration section are in all caps and read like an employment arbitration clause.
- Recurring elements: cross-references by section number (6.B, 12.G, 3.D); survival stated in one list (6.C) plus inside sections 2.B and 3.E; California law and venue throughout the dispute clauses; whistleblower and Defend Trade Secrets Act carve-out at the end of the boilerplate.
- Parties: a company engages an individual (not a firm) to give medical and scientific advisory services; he signs under an advisory title ("Chief Medical Advisor"). Stated relationship: a past-activities clause says he may have worked for the company before the agreement and has been paid for it; the amendment says any service he gives as a board director "if any" is not billed as Services, and keeps the company free to hire an employee Chief Medical Officer. No officer appointment, no prior employment, and nothing ties the engagement to a departure. Passes the fit rule as an adviser engaged for defined services; the board mention is a billing carve-out, not an appointment.
- Attachments: Exhibit A, Services and Compensation: included (contact, services, fee, expenses, invoicing, Section 409A). Exhibit B, form of Confidential Information and Assignment Agreement for subcontractors: referenced in 4.B, not included. Amendment No. 1: included (term, termination, notices, services and fee restated).
