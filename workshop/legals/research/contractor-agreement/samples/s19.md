## Document
- Shape: ten-page agreement: title, a one-sentence preamble naming both parties, one WHEREAS recital ("WITNESSETH"), a consideration lead-in, then seven numbered articles with capitalised headings (Engagement, Term, Services; Consideration, Expenses, Independent Contractor, Taxes; Termination; Confidential/Trade Secret Information, Company Property, Non-Solicitation; Mutual Representations and Limitation of Liability; Representations of the Consultant; Miscellaneous), sections numbered 1.1 to 7.14 with short run-in headings, then the signature blocks. Running footer "Consulting Agreement / Page N of 10". No definitions section and no exhibits; defined terms are set inline.
- Register: formal US contract English, third person ("the Company", "Consultant"), long sentences, numbers in words with figures in brackets, defined terms in quotes and capitals. Pay is equity only (restricted stock vesting on signing and on two product-launch milestones), so a large share of the text is securities paperwork: Rule 144 legend removal, investment and accredited-investor representations, two certificate legends quoted in full capitals. Leans toward the company on covenants (injunctive relief without bond, stated twice) but gives the consultant a company indemnity and a liability cap. Drafting slips left in: two sections numbered 2.1.1; "the Shares" used before any definition; section 3.3 pays "Consulting Fees" though the agreement sets no cash fee; section 4.3(c) repeats the employee non-solicit of 4.2; the confidentiality carve-out (a) says prior-possessed information "shall be included"; 5.4 and 5.5 carry no headings; "his affiliates" for the company in 2.4. Silent on ownership or assignment of work product and inventions, a DTSA immunity notice, insurance, dispute forum, jury waiver and arbitration, and termination by the company for convenience.
- Recurring elements: parties called "the Company" and "Consultant" (often without the article), each also a "Party"; cross-references by article ("ARTICLE IV"); a dollar threshold for expense approval and a dollar figure in the liability cap; one US state named for governing law; notice addresses written into section 7.1, the consultant's redacted with X's.
- Parties: a company engages an individual (who signs for himself) to provide services in researching, developing, packaging and marketing additional pharmaceutical and over-the-counter products, about ten hours a week, paid in restricted company stock. The text states no other relationship with the company (no officer or board title, no employment history, no separation terms), so it is a clean services engagement and passes the fit rule.
- Attachments: none. No statement of work, compensation schedule or separate confidentiality or invention-assignment agreement is attached or referenced; the services, pay and covenants all sit in the body.
