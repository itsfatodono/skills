## Document
- Shape: a standard form in three files, all linked from `https://bonterms.com/download-center/professional-services-agreement` (Version 1.2, CC BY 4.0). Part 1, the Cover Page example (docx, `https://framerusercontent.com/assets/4tiElDaspLDCpJzpK6gLwHuq4.docx`): one page that incorporates the standard terms by reference and holds the Key Terms (Effective Date, Rights in Deliverables checkbox, Governing Law, Courts), Attachments, Additional Terms and the signature blocks with notice addresses. Part 2, the standard terms (pdf, the row's URL): a heading, 17 numbered sections with run-in headings, subsections numbered 3.1 to 17.11 and lettered sub-clauses with their own headings; section 2 is a list of 41 alphabetical definitions, and sections 12 and 13 end with indented definition blocks that "apply unless modified on the Cover Page". Running footer with page number, copyright, licence and a no-legal-advice disclaimer. No signature block in the terms. Part 3, the SOW example (docx, `https://framerusercontent.com/assets/Q6ZSmhawLG442m9avBDO64ba70.docx`): a field form with capitalised section headings, each pointing to the standard-terms section it feeds ("See Section 3.4 of Professional Services Agreement"), and its own signature blocks. The cover page and SOW both open with the drafting note "[Example: Create your own version and remove this note and the footer before using]". The landing page's Explainer and Playbook are guidance for users, not part of the agreement, and were not extracted.
- Register: formal but compact US contract English, third person, capitalised defined roles "Customer" and "Provider"; short numbered clauses, numbers as figures only (15-day test period, 30-day payment, 1.5% late charge). Balanced by design: mutual warranties, mutual indemnities, mutual confidentiality, a mutual cap, but Provider-side duties dominate (acceptance testing, warranties of non-infringement and no harmful code, Customer-system rules, insurance). Written for a firm as much as an individual: Project Leads and Escalation Leads, approved subcontractors, Customer Affiliates signing their own SOWs; only the DTSA notice is switched on "only if Provider is an individual". Silent on non-compete and non-solicitation, background checks, a 1099 or W-9 mention, arbitration and jury waiver; data protection is only a hook to terms Customer names. Drafting slip: the SOW points the Copyleft field to Section 5.3(c), but the no-copyleft rule is 5.3(b).
- Recurring elements: Key Terms set on the Cover Page and used by capitalised name in the terms (Governing Law, Courts, General Cap, Uncapped Claims, Insurance Requirements); defaults stated in the terms and overridable by the Cover Page or a SOW ("Unless the SOW states otherwise", "unless modified on the Cover Page"); cross-references with section number and heading in brackets ("Section 3.5 (Subcontractors)"); the Escalation Process reused as the dispute step for change requests, acceptance and payment disputes; refunds of "pre-paid, unused fees" as the standard exit remedy; notice addresses taken from the Cover Page.
- Parties: written for a company ("Customer") that buys professional services from an outside provider ("Provider"), an individual or a firm acting through its staff and approved subcontractors; the services are any professional services described per SOW, with Deliverables that may include code, content or reports. States no other relationship between the parties; a clean services engagement, so it passes the fit rule.
- Attachments: the Cover Page and the SOW form are included (examples with blanks); the standard terms incorporate both by definition. Cover-page Attachments (policies, a DPA or other documents) and Additional Terms are blanks the parties fill; Change Orders are referenced with no form; Customer Policies and travel policies are referenced, not attached.
