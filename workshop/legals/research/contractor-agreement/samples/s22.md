## Document
- Shape: one exhibit holding two instruments: the original agreement (title, preamble, three recitals, a consideration line, then thirteen lettered sections A–M with bold headings, some split into numbered clauses, a "[Signature Page Follows]" marker and a separate signature page), followed by the Fourth Amendment (title, preamble, two recitals, two numbered clauses, its own signature page). Every page carries an exhibit label, a page number and a document-management number. The First to Third Amendments and the form of warrant are not in the exhibit.
- Register: formal US contract drafting; defined terms in quotes and capitals ("Engagement", "Services", "Consulting Fee", "Warrant", "Loss", "Claims"); third person; most sections a short paragraph, but the fee clause and the indemnity clause each run as one long paragraph. Reads as the consultant's paper: the fee is irrevocably earned on signing, the company may not cancel the warrant and must help lift resale restrictions, the consultant is liable only for gross negligence, wilful misconduct or bad faith, gives no warranty, and gets a one-way indemnity. Silent on confidentiality, ownership of work product, non-solicitation, non-competition, insurance and conflicts of interest. Drafting slips: the indemnity cross-refers to a "Section 7" that does not exist, the amendment's counterparts sentence says "Third Amendment", and the amendment adds to a "Cash Fee" the original never defines (it came in an earlier, unfiled amendment).
- Recurring elements: lettered top-level sections with numbered sub-clauses; defined terms set in the clause that first uses them; cross-references by letter and number ("Section B(2)"); exhibit label and document-management footer on every page; signature pages marked as such.
- Parties: a listed company engages a firm (an advisory LLC that describes itself as advising companies on business development, corporate strategy and management), which acts through one named principal, the only person who may perform the services; firm and principal together are "Consultant". Services: general corporate advice (strategic planning, deal-flow analysis, introductions, sector growth advice, other advice on request), reported to the CEO or the board; the amendment adds work on a new business strategy in another sector. Paid in warrants, not cash, in the original. No officer, board or employment role and nothing ties the engagement to a departure; neither the agreement nor the filing's 8-K states any other relationship (the 8-K lists the firm among several consultants engaged on similar services). Passes the fit rule.
- Attachments: Exhibit A, form of pre-funded warrant (referenced only, not included); Fourth Amendment (text included); First, Second and Third Amendments (referenced only by name and date in the Fourth Amendment's recitals).
