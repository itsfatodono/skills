## Document
- Shape: one exhibit: a "Confidential" header line, the title, a preamble, one recital and a consideration line, then thirteen numbered sections with run-in headings, several split into lettered sub-clauses (sections 12 and 13 give each sub-clause its own run-in title). A "[Remainder of page intentionally left blank. Signature page follows.]" marker comes before a two-column signature page, followed by Exhibit A (services and compensation). Every page has a "Page n of 12" footer. The filing index calls it a "Service Agreement"; the document calls itself a "Consulting Agreement". The effective date is the date given on the signature page.
- Register: formal US contract drafting; defined terms in quotes and capitals ("Agreement", "Effective Date", "Consulting Services", "Term", "Proprietary Information", "Shares"); third person throughout; the damages exclusion and the governing-law clause are in capitals. Reads as the company's paper: the company may terminate at once and the consultant only on notice, the consultant gives a tax indemnity and an uncapped securities-breach indemnity, equitable relief runs only to the company, the company may assign freely, and the restrictive covenants are long. A listed company paying in stock adds a long securities-law section. Silent on ownership of work product or inventions (only Proprietary Information and furnished materials are the company's), on insurance, on a payment schedule (the fee is shares earned on signing), on deliverables and acceptance. Drafting slips: §2's survival line names "Consultant's obligations set forth in Sections 5 through 13", which takes in sections that are not consultant duties; §9 uses "Losses" and "Party" without defining them; §12(g) calls §9 a "limitation of liability", though §9 only excludes kinds of damages and sets no cap.
- Recurring elements: numbered sections with lettered sub-clauses; cross-references by number and letter ("Section 8(b)", "Section 13(c)"); page footer "Page n of 12"; confidential header with the company name and document title; defined terms set where first used.
- Parties: a Nasdaq-listed company engages a firm (an LLC) that signs through a named authorised representative. Exhibit A gives the firm a "Position" label as a payments consultant ("Independent Contractor"), not an officer title. Services: strategic advice, business development and solutions architecture for payments (card processing, merchant accounts, EBT acceptance, multi-currency acceptance, payment vendor management). The fee is paid only in company shares, earned and vested on signing. The agreement states no other relationship. The S-1/A that files it registers those shares for resale; its selling-stockholder note says the firm's representative controls the shares, and that apart from this service relationship and the shares the firm has had no material relationship with the company in the past three years. Passes the fit rule.
- Attachments: Exhibit A, Consulting Services and Compensation (text included). Referenced only: the company's Insider Trading Policy (the consultant acknowledges receiving it), the company's expense policies and safety rules.
