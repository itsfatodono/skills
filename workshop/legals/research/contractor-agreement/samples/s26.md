## Document
- Shape: medium-length agreement filed as an S-1/A exhibit: title ("Sponsor Consulting Agreement") that differs from the name the preamble gives the agreement ("Program Launch Advisory Agreement"), a one-paragraph preamble, an unnumbered appointment paragraph, a long unnumbered services list (11 numbered service areas with lettered and roman sub-items), then 20 numbered sections, the earlier ones with headings and lettered or roman sub-clauses, the later ones (7-13, 15-17) bare numbered paragraphs with no heading; a notice-address block inside section 17; "[Remainder of page intentionally left blank]"; a two-column signature block. Page numbers only, no footer legend.
- Register: dense US securities-industry contract drafting; defined terms in quotes and capitals ("Issuer", "Company", "Service Provider", "Confidential Information", "Damages", "Claims", "Additional Services"); third person; long sentences, two clauses in capitals (consequential-damages exclusion, jury waiver). Reads as the service provider's paper adapted from its managing-dealer form: it uses terms never defined here ("Selling Group Member", "PPM", "Units", "Offering", "Managing Dealer" named as a party), its cross-references point to sections that do not exist or do not match (Section 2.01, 6(b), 8, 9, 13, 21), and both the issuer and the company are named though the company is the only client party. Favours the provider: short payment window, deemed acceptance of invoices, late interest, broad issuer indemnity for offering documents. Silent on ownership of work product, non-solicitation, insurance, taxes on fees, and data protection.
- Recurring elements: defined terms set in the clause that first uses them; cross-references to a separate concurrent agreement (the Managing Dealer Agreement) for expenses, the managing-broker role and the entire-agreement clause; carve-outs for "undisputed" amounts; page numbers at the foot.
- Parties: a company (the filer, a real-estate investment manager acting as sponsor of investment offerings) engages a firm (a broker-dealer LLC registered with the SEC and a FINRA member), which signs through its CEO; no individual performer is named and the work is done by the firm's employees. Services: advisory to prepare and launch a new investment offering (orientation, market-readiness review, product structuring, choice of counsel, document review, due-diligence coordination, custodian, transfer-agent and escrow set-up, investor forms, reporting, marketing support, introductions to broker-dealer and adviser firms). Stated relationship: under a separate agreement signed at the same time the firm also acts as managing dealer for a private placement; section 3(d) says this agreement does not itself make it the managing broker. No officer, board or employment role and nothing ties it to a departure. Passes the fit rule.
- Attachments: none attached. Section 4(a) speaks of "all Exhibits thereto" but no exhibit is filed with it; the Managing Dealer Agreement is referenced throughout (expenses, the managing-dealer role, entire agreement) but its text is not included.
