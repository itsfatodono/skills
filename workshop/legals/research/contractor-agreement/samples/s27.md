## Document
- Shape: short agreement filed as an 8-K exhibit: a title ("Advisory / Consulting Services Agreement") that differs from the name the preamble uses ("Consulting Services Agreement"), a one-sentence preamble, a short "Recitals" paragraph and a consideration line, then 13 numbered sections with run-in headings, only section 5 split into lettered and roman sub-clauses; a two-column signature block; after it an untitled, unlabelled schedule headed "SERVICES" with one numbered item and nine lettered service items. A running footer "Consulting Services Agreement- Page N" on each page.
- Register: plain-to-standard US small-cap drafting; defined terms in quotes and capitals ("Client", "Consultant", "Term", "Equity Compensation", "Anniversary Date") but "Services" is used capitalised throughout and never defined, nor tied to the trailing schedule; third person; no clauses in capitals. Loose in places: typos ("or/or", "to be become", "exclusive,.", "one and the instrument"), a section number without its period, "cause" defined in the term section though termination there is "for any reason", and the cause test used only in the equity clause. Light and fairly balanced, leaning to the consultant: non-exclusive, time at the consultant's discretion, no guarantee of results, no expenses either way. Silent on ownership of work product, non-solicitation, indemnity, limitation of liability, severability, data protection, tax forms, and any disclosure of the consultant's paid status to investors.
- Recurring elements: defined terms set in the clause that first uses them; securities-law language in three places (appointment warranty, termination "cause", confidentiality's list of material non-public information); a page footer with the agreement's short name.
- Parties: a company (the filer, a small listed golf and sports-property holding company) engages a firm (an investor-relations corporation), which signs through its president; no individual performer is named and the text speaks of the consultant's "employees and/or agents". Services: investor relations and communications planning (disseminating public information to investment professionals, ongoing contact with the brokerage community, conference calls, investor meetings and conferences, introductions to research analysts and newsletter writers, news-release review and distribution, social-media and IR best practices, review of investor materials, media introductions), with any capital-raising or promotion work to be set out in an addendum. No officer, board or employment role and nothing ties it to a departure; the release required for the unvested shares on a termination without cause concerns this engagement only. Passes the fit rule.
- Attachments: one untitled "SERVICES" schedule after the signature block, text included; section 1 mentions a possible addendum for capital-raising services, none attached; section 13's "documents referred to herein" names none.
