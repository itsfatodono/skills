## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-10.13 to an S-1/A): title, one-paragraph preamble, three lettered recitals, a one-line words-of-agreement clause; 9 numbered articles in capitals, each split into numbered sub-clauses with run-in headings (1.1–1.5, 2.1–2.4, 3.1–3.5, 4.1–4.16 under a lead-in, 5.1–5.8 with 5.3 "Intentionally omitted", 6.1–6.2, 7.1–7.3, 8.1–8.2, 9.1–9.8); a one-line testimonium and two stacked signature blocks (By, Name, Title, Date), the consultant's carrying its notice address and email. Then Exhibit A, Statement of Work, filled in: preamble, numbered sections 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 (no section 3), two tables (deliverables with due date and objective completion criteria; share consideration allocated per deliverable with work done, accounting value and dates), an "Initialed by Consultant" line. Then Exhibit B, Certificate of Completion of Services, filled in and signed, with a company acceptance block. Page numbers only, no running header.
- Register: formal US securities-lawyer drafting, company-side paper; defined terms in quotes and capitals ("Services", "Performance Period", "Completion Date", "Shares", "Work Product", "Measurement Date"); third person, "the Company" and "the Consultant". The text is built around paying a fixed block of restricted stock for services and registering its resale: the services are defined negatively against capital-raising and market promotion in six places, the consultant gives 16 securities-style representations, and an article grants piggyback registration rights. The commercial terms are brief (confidentiality, IP, indemnity and each closing clause in one to three sentences). No limitation of liability, insurance, non-solicit, non-compete, data protection, DTSA notice, publicity or dispute-escalation clause.
- Recurring elements: cross-references by section or article number; the "bona fide services, not in connection with a capital-raising transaction, not promoting or maintaining a market" formula repeated (recital B, 1.3, 3.2, 4.3, Exhibit A §7, Exhibit B ¶4); reporting to the chief executive and chief legal officers stated in 1.5 and three times in Exhibit A; deliverable-led structure (each deliverable with a due date and an objective completion test); compensation earned only on full performance, a signed completion certificate, written acceptance and a board resolution.
- Parties: a company engages a foreign firm (a company organised in another country) that performs through one natural person, its director, named only in Exhibit B; the company signs through a director. Services: buy-side transaction advisory on acquisitions: due-diligence request list and report, redrafting a share purchase agreement, pre-completion balance-sheet structuring, and scoping a regulator's change-of-control application. Paid wholly in restricted common stock. Relationship stated: the firm, its affiliates and its owners are represented not to be affiliates, directors, officers, employees or 10% holders of the company, nor broker-dealers; the firm will be a selling stockholder in a resale registration. Passes the fit rule: a clean engagement for defined services with no departure, officer or board terms; the stock and registration rights are how the services are paid, not an investor's advisory fee (the firm holds no other company securities).
- Attachments: Exhibit A, Statement of Work (text included, filled in, two tables); Exhibit B, Certificate of Completion of Services (text included, filled in, signed, with the company's acceptance). Referenced, not attached: the company's insider trading policy, a selling stockholder questionnaire "in the form provided by the Company", a confidential schedule naming the targets and advisers, IRS Form W-8BEN-E. No separate confidentiality or invention-assignment agreement.
