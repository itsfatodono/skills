# cookie-policy: merged section list

First batch: s01–s15 (5 large, 5 mid, 5 small; c01–c15 in pool order), written before any
saturation batch (`build/build.md` step 3). All 15 are valid; none was replaced. Saturation batch 1
(s16–s20) is judged under *Saturation* and folded into the counts below; it added no id (s18's
binding-language clause was first taken as `language-version`, then dropped under decision 44;
see *Changes*). The build is saturated at s20 with 20 samples. The *First-batch samples* column
stays as written before saturation. Each meaning below
is fixed from here: change one only with a dated note under *Changes*. One sample row can map to
more than one id when it gives more than one kind of fact (s04 §3 gives `purposes`,
`data-collected`, `retention`, `third-parties` and `data-sharing`). The scope is the policy
only; the consent banner is site chrome (owner, 2026-10-27).

**A saturation sample is compared against the 26 ids in *Section ids*.** Ids marked
*(regional)* stay known ids for saturation even though step 4 leaves their content out of the
root.

## Patterns (s01–s20)

How samples split on the ids where they differ. Template wording counts once (decision 39): s06,
s08 and s09 add their template's wording once each, and the CMP category wording shared by s07 and
s10 counts once. Batch 1 brought no template text.

- **purposes:** 10/20 a list of categories (s02 s03 s04 s06 s10 s11 s14 s17 s19 s20), 4/20 a
  table (s01 s08 s13 s16), 6/20 prose (s05 s07 s09 s12 s15 s18). 5/20 cut by purpose of use or by
  tool rather than the standard four categories (s01, s14, s02's function headings, s10's extra
  "communications" and "registered users", s18 one clause per tool); s06 mixes party and purpose
  categories. 11/20 say per category whether it can be switched off (s02 s03 s04 s05 s06 s07 s10
  s15 s17 s19 s20; s12 says so under its consent tool).
- **cookie-list:** 7/20 on the page or a linked page (tables s01 s08 s12 s20; by provider s02 s14
  s18); 4/20 only a pointer to the consent tool (s05 s07 s10 s15); 2/20 announce a list the page
  lacks (s13 s17), as s02 does for its website cookies; 7/20 have no list and no pointer (s03 s04
  s06 s09 s11 s16 s19; s16 and s19 name providers per category or partner instead). 4/20 add a
  caveat that names and number change (s01 s05 s13 s14); s07 and s13 state how the list is kept
  current. s20's table mixes web cookies and app SDKs in one list.
- **browser-controls:** 11/20 generic advice only; 5/20 send the reader to an external help site
  (s04 s07 s12 s13 s19); 4/20 link each major browser's help page (s08 s13 s18 s19). 5/20 note
  that choices are per device or browser, or reset when cookies are cleared (s07 s09 s11 s15
  s19).
- **consent-tool:** 5/20 put a settings button on the policy page (s02 s04 s05 s08 s13); 4/20
  describe the first-visit banner's options (s02 s03 s05 s14); 7/20 route US users to a "Your
  Privacy Choices" / "Do Not Sell or Share" link (s05 s07 s11 s15 s16 s17 s19); 2/20 also name
  the app's own settings (s17 s20).
- **refusal-effects:** mostly one warning sentence inside a controls section; 5/20 state it per
  category (s04 s10 s11 s19 s20); 2/20 give it its own short section (s01 "Your Choices", s02 §3).
- **what-are-cookies:** 13/20 explain first- vs third-party, 9/20 session vs persistent; s11
  opens the whole policy with the generic explainer.
- **similar-technologies:** 6/20 fold every tracker into the defined term "cookies" (s01 s02 s16
  s17 s19 s20).
- **contact:** 10/20 an email only (s10 adds its Help Center; s16 the DPO), 3/20 email plus
  postal address (s06 s12 s15), s11 phone and post only; 6/20 give no contact at all (s01 s05 s08
  s09 s13 s20; s20 points to the privacy policy for it).
- **changes:** 4/20 only post the change and update the date (s03 s07 s12 s20), s02 "reasonable
  means", 5/20 notice for material or significant changes (s10 s11 s15 s17 s18), s06 email to
  customers.
