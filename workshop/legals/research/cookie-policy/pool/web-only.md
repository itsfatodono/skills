# cookie-policy pool: web-only

R4 company site, web-first companies with no app or where the website is the product. Candidate URLs came from footer and `/cookies`-style paths for SaaS, developer tools, e-commerce and media companies, and each was fetched. Redirects were followed, and the final URL is recorded. Fetches that returned 403/401/404/no response were dropped. Privacy pages with only a cookie section were dropped as unverified. Store id is `-` and downloads are not applicable.
