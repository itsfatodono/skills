## Document
- Shape: policy page with an effective date and an intro, then H2/H3 sections in question form;
  the two core sections are two-column tables (term | description). The cookie list itself lives
  on a linked table page, grouped by purpose, one table per purpose.
- Register: plain corporate, "we/you", medium-length sentences; many "For example" sentences
  inside table cells; frequent cross-references to numbered sections of the Privacy Policy.
- Recurring elements: effective date at the top (policy) and last-updated date (table page);
  defined term "cookies" covering all similar technologies; links to the Privacy Policy, Help
  Center articles and account settings pages; two leftover "ENTER A SUMMARY" CMS placeholders
  above the tables; no contact details anywhere in the policy.
