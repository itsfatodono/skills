## Document
- Shape: one standalone page with an "Updated" month-year line and a "Change Cookie Preferences" button above the text. An unnumbered opening (scope and publisher) comes first, then 8 numbered bold-paragraph sections. No summary box and no table of contents.
- Register: "we/you", fairly formal, with the stiff phrasing of a French-to-English translation ("deposit of cookies", "constitue the Plateform"). It mixes cookie-law wording (consent, deposit) with an app-SDK inventory. The inventory reads like an internal audit list with hedges left in ("potentially requiring disclosure", "could require disclosure").
- Recurring elements: last-updated date at the top; a preferences button at the top and links to the "Cookies manager" in the text; a pointer to the Privacy Policy; the privacy email is given twice (transfers, contact).
- Scope: one policy for the website, the web app and the mobile apps, defined together as "the Platform". Trackers are defined broadly so that SDKs, device identifiers and fingerprinting are included.
- Gaps: section 5 ends by announcing "a detailed overview of the cookies that may be placed during your website visit", but the served HTML has nothing after it. The per-cookie list is missing, or else injected client-side by the consent tool (Didomi). No cookie names, lifetimes or first/third-party split appear in the text. The only duration is how long the consent choice is kept.
