## Document
- Shape: one standalone web page; title and "Last Updated" date, an untitled intro, then question-style headings ("How long do cookies last?"); category subsections in bold run-in labels; the opt-out section is long and mixes many mechanisms. No cookie table on the page: the per-cookie list lives in the consent-tool preference center, reached from a footer link. Live US version (`en-us` default); no language switcher.
- Register: plain-to-formal, "we/you", medium-length sentences; questions as headings; several hedges ("may", "at times"). One paragraph on per-device repetition appears twice, once saying "Service" and once "Website" (an editing slip).
- Recurring elements: "Last Updated" date at top; repeated pointer to the "Your Privacy Choices" footer link (five times); links to the Privacy Policy (with California rights) in the intro and the closing section; contact email at the end.
