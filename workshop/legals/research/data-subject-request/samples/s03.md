## Document
- Shape: one-page web form on the company's own site (Framer). A short intro of five paragraphs above the fields, then contact fields, a requester-type choice, a single request-type dropdown, a sworn certification checkbox, a CAPTCHA and a submit button. No sections with headings beyond the page title; field labels act as headings.
- Register: plain but legalistic, "we/you"; long sentences in the intro and the certification; request types are numbered and written as statutory rights.
- Recurring elements: pointers to the Privacy Policy and California Privacy Notice (twice); repeated reminder that only data linked to the identifiers given is in scope; one request type per submission. The form is framed entirely around US state laws; there is no general (non-US) route on the page.
- Version taken: the default English page; no language switcher. Authorized-agent detail fields are referred to in the intro ("select Authorized Agent to provide information about yourself") but are not in the served HTML; they are presumably revealed by script after selecting that option.
