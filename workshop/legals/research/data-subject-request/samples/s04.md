## Document
- Shape: a hosted privacy-request portal (Transcend), not a page of prose. A short landing intro, then a step flow: pick requester type, verify identity, pick a request, read that request's explainer, fill its few fields, submit. Status messages follow the request after submission.
- Register: plain, "we/you", product-specific (ChatGPT, Sora, GPTs). Request names are user goals ("Download my data", "Delete my GPTs"), not legal rights. Only one request names a law.
- Recurring elements: each request has a one-line summary plus a longer explainer; links to the Help Center and the global privacy policy; the support email privacy@openai.com; required acknowledgement checkboxes. Region is asked on the form and gates which requests appear. The portal ships in 61 locales. English is the default.
- Requesters split two ways: account holders get five requests and no free-text fields (identity comes from the OpenAI account login), and people without an account get only the removal-from-responses request, verified by an email link.
