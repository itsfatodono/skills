## Document
- Shape: one-page web form on the company's own site (server-rendered, read from the page HTML). Title "Privacy Request Form", then three headed field groups, a Submit button, and two short notice paragraphs plus a privacy-policy link placed below the button.
- Register: plain, "we/you", neutral; the notices hedge with "based on governing law" instead of naming a law.
- Recurring elements: required fields marked with an asterisk; no intro text above the form; a hidden honeypot field ("Please leave this field blank") for spam; the site's shared strings include a generic "Thank you for your submission" confirmation.
- Absent: no identity-verification step or ID upload, no state/province field, no authorised-agent option, no attestation or signature checkbox, no account identifier, no response-channel choice.
- Region-neutral design: one form for all countries; the request-type list mixes GDPR and US state-law rights, and the residence country routes the request.
