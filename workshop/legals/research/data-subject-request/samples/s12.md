## Document
- Shape: OneTrust web form, one page. A welcome block on top, then country and state/province, then a request-type menu whose options change with the jurisdiction picked. Each request type can reveal its own notice or free-text box. Contact fields follow, then Submit, an email-verification step and a confirmation screen. Read from the form's `/publish` JSON. Only the active fields (status 10) are counted; about 50 inactive OneTrust template fields are left out. English (`en-us`) is the default of 8 form languages.
- Register: plain "we/you". The welcome is signed by "The NHL Privacy Officer". Notices are short, one to three sentences each, and say what will stop happening and what will not.
- Recurring elements: links to the privacy policy, cookie policy and cookie preference center (in the welcome and in the US opt-out notice); a "go-forward basis" phrase in every sharing opt-out; privacy@nhl.com in the welcome and the footer; a "Powered by OneTrust" badge.
- Jurisdiction gating: the portal is limited to the US, Canada, the EEA, Switzerland and the UK. Everyone else is sent to a general contact page. Request types come in 5 separate menus (California; the 4 older state laws; the 13 newer state laws; other US states; Canada; EEA/CH/UK), so most legal wording sits in the option labels.
- Verification: email link only. ID upload, phone OTP and sign-in are off; reCAPTCHA is on.
