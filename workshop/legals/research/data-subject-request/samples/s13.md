## Document
- Shape: one web page (Next.js) under a "← Back to Privacy Notice" link. It has a title with a one-line purpose, then a "Before you submit" box with three short blocks (self-service in the app, a privacy inbox, complaints and other inquiries) and a verification note. A single-column form follows (country, name, email, role, request type, data categories, login email, agent box, details) with a submit button, and a success panel replaces the form after submission. The server HTML holds the static form. The page script adds the role-dependent parts (request types filtered by role, data-category checkboxes) and the post-submit text. Submissions post to HubSpot's forms API, but the form itself is the company's own page.
- Register: plain and short. "We/you" in the notes; first-person option labels ("Download my data", "Who accessed my data", "My child's athlete data").
- Recurring elements: asterisks on required fields; the privacy email named twice (hidden by Cloudflare email obfuscation in the HTML); "See our Privacy Notice for how we handle requests and verification" printed twice; footer links to the Privacy Notice, this page, a separate "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" page, Terms, Cookie Policy and Consent Preferences. No date and no response deadline anywhere on the page.
- Scope: worldwide. The country select lists every country and "Other", and there is no state or region gating. Law names appear in only one option label (CCPA). The form offers the GDPR-style rights (restrict, object) to everyone.
- Product-specific shape: the role select (rower, coach, parent or guardian, agent, no account) drives which request types and data categories appear. A query string can prefill role and request type, so the app can deep-link to it.
- Absent: no attestation or consent checkbox, no proof-of-authority upload for agents, no identity-document request, and no statement of the response time.
