## Document
- Shape: one short web page (Next.js, server-rendered). A title and a one-sentence intro with an outbound "learn more" link sit above a form in two titled groups (About You, Your Request). An identity-verification notice, an attestation checkbox and a submit button follow. A "Luzid is now Glasslake" banner sits at the top of the page.
- Register: plain and neutral. The intro speaks to "you". Request types are first-person menu items ("Access my personal data"). The attestation is one first-person sentence.
- Recurring elements: required fields marked with an asterisk; inline validation messages per required field. There is no contact email, no postal address, no response time, no date and no link to the privacy policy in the page body (the policy is linked only from the footer).
- Scope: global. A region select lists EU/EEA, UK, Brazil, US and Other. No law is named on the page. Rights are given in generic GDPR-style terms, with no US-state wording.
- Behaviour (from the page script): submitting shows only a browser alert and clears the form. Nothing is sent and no next step is given. A Portuguese version of the same form ships in the same script; the English default was taken.
