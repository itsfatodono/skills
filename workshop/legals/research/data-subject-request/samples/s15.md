## Document
- Shape: one-page web form on the company's own site (Next.js; the page HTML carries the intro and fields, and the dropdown options and post-submit messages sit in the page's embedded form data). Title "Personal Data Request", a short intro with a numbered list of rights, then the fields and a Submit button.
- Register: plain, "you/Bolt", short sentences; each right gets a one-line plain-language gloss with an example.
- Recurring elements: every field has a helper line under it ("The email registered to your Bolt account."); dependent dropdowns (service, then account type for that service); inline error messages per field.
- Absent: no name field, no ID upload requirement (attachments optional), no authorised-agent option, no attestation checkbox, no stated response time or fee, no law named anywhere on the form.
- Region-neutral design: one form for all markets; the country dropdown says which country the request concerns, and the communication-language field covers multilingual markets.
- Verification is by account match: email and phone must match the Bolt account, a mismatch error tells non-users to pick "I am not a user of the platform", and processing starts only after the person clicks an email confirmation link.
