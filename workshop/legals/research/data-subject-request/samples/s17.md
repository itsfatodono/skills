## Document
- Shape: one server-rendered page. An intro block (what a SAR is, the response deadline, a
  "before you make a request" list), three short info blocks (when we'll respond, registration
  for Pay as you go, other ways to ask), then the online form in four parts (contact details,
  account details, information wanted, declaration and uploads), then a closing "other ways to
  get information" block. Read in full from the curl HTML; no script-loaded form.
- Scope: access requests only (subject access request); no request-type picker, no deletion,
  correction or opt-out route. The "request type" choice is replaced by a checklist of the
  telecom record categories the requester wants.
- Register: plain, "we/you", contractions, help-page tone; legal names (Data Protection Act,
  GDPR) appear in the intro and declaration only.
- Recurring elements: guidance-notes PDF linked three times (intro, incoming-calls item,
  declaration); identity verification and written authority for third parties stated three
  times; 12-month retention of call/text records repeated per item; required fields marked
  with an asterisk.
- Alternative channels: a downloadable postal SAR form (PDF) and a phone number; third parties
  and solicitors must use the postal form.
- Typo on the page: "we'll respond within calendar month" (number missing).
- Site chrome left out: header, mega-nav, footer.
