## Document
- Shape: a single-page OneTrust web form. The company page (`philips.co.uk/c-e/privacy-request.html`,
  which redirects to `philips.com/c-e/privacy-request`) holds nothing but an iframe of the form on
  Philips's own OneTrust tenant (`philips-privacy.my.onetrust.com`). The form has a welcome block,
  then country and relationship, then a branching tree: each answer shows the next question. There
  are three branches (consumer, employee or applicant, other relationship), and country adds US-only
  and China-only content. No rights explainer, no response deadline, no contact details, no
  attestation and no file upload. Read from the `/publish` JSON and a headless-Chrome render with
  each branch clicked; about 50 template fields (CCPA, PIPEDA, PIPL, agent, minor) are configured
  but never shown, and one shareholder follow-up field holds untranslated Dutch text but never
  shows either.
- Register: plain, "you/we", brand named in the third person ("processed by Philips"); option labels
  in the first person ("I am a consumer"); a few typos left in ("an an employee").
- Recurring elements: country prefilled from the visitor's location (geolocation on); language
  switcher (English default, about 40 languages); reCAPTCHA; email confirmation after submit; footer
  with a privacy-notice link and copyright line.
- Vendor template: OneTrust. The welcome text, the branch questions, the product lists and every
  notice block are the company's own words; field help lines and post-submit messages are OneTrust
  defaults.
