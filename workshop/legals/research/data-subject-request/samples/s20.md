## Document
- Shape: OneTrust web form, one page. A one-word welcome, then a long scope notice on what the form does not cover, then requester type, request types, a US-notice block, a Washington/Nevada question that opens a health-data menu, contact fields, and an "after you submit" block. Submit leads to an email-verification step and a confirmation screen. Read from the form's `/publish` JSON. Only the active fields (status 10) are counted; about 60 inactive OneTrust template fields are left out. English (`en-us`) is the only language.
- Register: plain "we/you", short notices. Request-type labels are first-person ("Request a Copy My Personal Information") apart from one "Ask Us to Delete Your…" label.
- Recurring elements: "does not apply to Protected Health Information (PHI)" repeated in the scope notice, in a tooltip on each core request type and in the health-data block; links to the HIPAA Notices of Privacy Practices of the company's clinics; a "Powered by OneTrust" badge. No footer, no privacy-policy link, no contact email or postal address anywhere on the form.
- Scope split: the form covers web, app and marketing data only. Members and clinic patients are sent to HIPAA notices, and plan members to the app login or their health plan. This exclusion is the form's largest block.
- Jurisdiction gating: none by country (full world list, no geolocation), but every right is offered to everyone except an Oregon/Minnesota-only option and a Washington/Nevada-only health-data menu.
- Verification: email link only. ID upload, phone OTP and sign-in are off; reCAPTCHA is on. Phone number and date of birth are both required.
