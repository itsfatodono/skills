## Document
- Shape: one page inside a legal hub (side nav lists ToS, DPA, privacy, etc.); short unnumbered preamble, then 11 headed sections of numbered clauses (cross-referenced as "clause 9.3", "8.1").
- Register: formal contract English, "Sketch"/"You" with capitalised defined terms ("You", "Use", "License Key"); medium-length sentences, "shall".
- Recurring elements: version date under the title ("Version 06 March 2025"); "Copy for LLMs" / "View as Markdown" buttons; contact email in the last section; the full company identity (address, chamber of commerce and VAT numbers) sits inside the definition of "Sketch", not in a footer.
- Scope: a paid desktop licence-key product (perpetual-style licence with 12 months of updates), also linked to the SaaS Terms of Service for Workspace editors.
