## Document
- Shape: one web page, a short italic-style caveat on top, an untitled preamble, then 15 numbered clauses; most clauses carry an inline run-in heading, clauses 10–15 have none. Body follows Apple's Standard Licensed Application EULA almost word for word, with Apple's developer-EULA "minimum terms" (maintenance, product claims, IP claims, Apple as beneficiary) added and an EU governing-law and CISG clause appended.
- Register: formal legal, defined terms in quotes ("Licensed Application", "Licensor", "External Services"), "you" vs "Licensor" in third person, with one slip into "we"; warranty clause and most of the liability clause in all caps.
- Recurring elements: version reference at the end ("Ref:EULA-v4"); repeated "Licensor, not Apple" allocation; no last-updated date; no contact block inside the agreement (the site footer gives the support email).
- Gap: clause 12 ends with "the countries identified below" but no list follows (left over from the Apple template).
