## Document
- Shape: one group-wide EULA page for all Wondershare products: base EULA (20 numbered articles plus a closing acknowledgment), then product addenda. Extracted per rule 6: the base EULA, the generative-AI addendum (applies to all Wondershare software) and the Filmora addendum; the addenda for Dr.Fone/MobileTrans/Recoverit, Virbo, Edraw, Wondershare Drive and PDFelement cover other products and are left out.
- Register: formal licensor/licensee, with third-person "Licensee" and "you" mixed in; long sentences; warranty, liability and indemnity in all caps; the AI addendum switches to plain "we/you".
- Recurring elements: an all-caps "license, not a sale" banner; a preamble naming the clauses to read closely (Art. 3, 5, 14, 15, 16, 19); privacy policy brought in by reference; the addenda say they prevail in a conflict; no last-updated date; support email given only in the publicity clause; the Filmora addendum repeats base clauses (restrictions, IP, term, liability) nearly word for word.
