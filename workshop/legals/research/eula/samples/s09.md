## Document
- Shape: single page titled "Terms and Conditions" that works as a combined terms of use and app license agreement; an untitled intro with capitalised call-outs (auto-renewal, not a healthcare provider, arbitration), a linked table of contents, then 21 numbered sections, most split into numbered subsections with their own headings.
- Register: mixed; the intro and data/billing parts use "we/you", the license, liability and warranty parts switch to "Licensor" and "Moodworks Inc." in the third person. Moderate legalese, mid-length sentences, key warnings in ALL CAPS in the intro only.
- Recurring elements: "Last updated" date under the title; the medical/mental-health disclaimer repeats in the intro, Eligibility, Purpose, No Warranty for Outcomes, Liability and the SMS AI part; the support email and the Austin mailing address recur across sections; Apple and Google are named together throughout. Limitation of liability, warranty disclaimer and severability each appear twice in different sections. Apple's minimum EULA terms (maintenance, product claims, IP claims, third-party beneficiary) are spread across sections rather than grouped.
