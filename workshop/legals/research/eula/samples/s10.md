## Document
- Shape: one long page titled "Terms of Use" (version label v1.5, posted date) that acts as the app's EULA ("licensed to you, not sold"). Three blocks in tabs/anchors: main terms (preamble + 23 numbered clauses, clause 19 missing because governing law moved out), *Product Specific Terms* (one third-party software list per app version and platform, iOS and Android, current and past builds), and *Location Specific Terms* (per-residence add-ons). Ends with a link list of previous terms versions. Word count covers main terms and location terms; the per-version third-party lists add roughly 350 words each.
- Register: formal US contract drafting, "you/we/Dolby", defined terms in quotes and parentheses, long sentences; warranty, liability, export and arbitration waiver text in all caps.
- Recurring elements: version number and posted/effective date at the top with a note that residence-based terms apply; arbitration notice up front; cross-references by section number (several point to the wrong number after renumbering); GDPR carve-outs repeated in arbitration and venue clauses; tables used only for age minimums and law/venue by country; contact email at the end; archive of previous versions at the bottom.
