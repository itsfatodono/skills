## Document
- Shape: 28-page PDF (Word export, "Version No.05112026" in every footer, page X of 28), titled "End User Agreement" by URL only; inside, four stacked agreements each with its own right-aligned title and restarted numbering: Foundation Agreement, Software Module, SaaS Module, Services and Education Module. Extracted here: the Foundation Agreement (base terms every module incorporates) and the Software Module §§1–7 (the software licence). Left out as not EULA content: Software Module §§8–10 (hardware use, hardware warranty and remedy), SaaS Module, Services and Education Module.
- Numbering: decimal clauses (1.1, 3.1.4(a)), ALL-CAPS section headings; General Terms clauses have bold run-in titles ("Amendments.", "Force Majeure."). No table of contents, no summary.
- Register: enterprise B2B contract, not a consumer click-through. Parties are "Broadcom" and "Customer" (an organization) in the third person; "you"/"your organization" appear only once or twice. Heavy defined terms with capitals, long sentences, "shall". Warranty disclaimer, liability limits and evaluation disclaimer in ALL CAPS.
- Architecture: layered contract — the agreement is formed by a Transaction Document (quote/order/PO), which names the Broadcom entity, start date and modules; product-specific use rights live in external Specific Program Documentation (SPD) and other "URL Terms" incorporated by link "(or successor URL)". Order of precedence clause ranks the layers.
- Recurring elements: copyright line at the top; version number in footer; no "last updated" date in the text; no acceptance-by-installing clause (acceptance is by order/PO); contact only as a legal-notice email and a notice-address URL. Same text covers all CA, Symantec and VMware products; the product is never named.
