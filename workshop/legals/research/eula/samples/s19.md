## Document
- Shape: one EULA in two tiers. The hub page holds an all-caps acceptance banner, five numbered intro clauses (parties, who "You" is, acceptance, arbitration warning, structure) with a product-to-link table, then "General Terms" as about 50 unnumbered all-caps H3 sections and a closing "Update" line. Product licence grants and limits sit on linked "Specific Terms" pages, one per product family (graphics/CorelDRAW, MindManager, WinZip), and the Specific Terms win in a conflict. This extraction covers the hub plus the CorelDRAW page (13 numbered H2 sections). The MindManager and WinZip pages cover other products and were not taken. Default English (`/en/`) version.
- Register: formal licensor contract with capitalised "We/Us/Our" and "You/Your", many defined terms in brackets ("Subscription License", "Assets", "Sunsetted Software", "Mandatory Updates"), long sentences with (a)/(b) and (i)/(ii) lists. Disclaimers, the liability cap, the jury/class waiver and the notice method are in all caps. The intro clauses are plainer, with question-style headings ("Who 'You' Are").
- Recurring elements: points to the License Certificate or license key to show which terms apply; BULA override for business editions; links to the privacy statement, cookie notice, acceptable use policy and prohibited-countries list; carve-outs for EEA users and non-waivable consumer rights; written arbitration opt-out with two postal addresses; "updated on" date at the end.
- Scope: one shared EULA for the group's consumer desktop and SaaS products (Corel, WinZip, MindManager brands), with perpetual, subscription, OEM, trial and academic licences; entity users are covered through the "Assets" concept and audit rights.
