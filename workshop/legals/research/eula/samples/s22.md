## Document
- Shape: one modular contract in five lettered parts (A Master Terms, B Software, C Hardware, D Professional Services, E Jurisdiction Specific), each with a one-paragraph scope note; deeply numbered clauses (A.1, B.1.1, B.1.1.1); a preamble lists the parts and says which apply to whom. Same page also carries a separate DPA on a tab (not extracted).
- Register: formal German-drafted B2B contract; third person "TeamViewer" / "Customer" with capitalised defined terms; long sentences with (i)/(ii) enumerations; the Americas liability and disclaimer clauses in all caps; the Australian consumer clause switches to "We/You".
- Recurring elements: cross-references by section number; links out to contracting-entities, product-descriptions, privacy notice, AI terms, EU Data Act addendum pages; "Version as of <date>" and a "Download EULA" link at the end; free licence vs. subscription distinguished throughout.
