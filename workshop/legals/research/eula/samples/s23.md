## Document
- Shape: one long page; short scope sentence and "Updated" date above an untitled preamble, then 19 numbered "§" sections with decimal sub-clauses (some to three levels). No table of contents, no summary box.
- Register: formal German-style contract drafting in the third person ("the Licensor", "the User"), long sentences, defined terms in quotes and parentheses; US-only clauses in all caps.
- Recurring elements: "Updated <month, year>" at the top; one EULA covering every product line, with the licensing entity chosen by brand; a series of license-model sections (perpetual, subscription, teams, render, education, trial, free) each built from the same grant formula; split regimes for users inside vs outside the US (warranty, liability, governing law); free-licence models swap the warranty and liability sections for an "as is" substitute; link to the privacy policy.
