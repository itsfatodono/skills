## Document
- Shape: standalone agreement, 25 numbered clauses each opening with a run-in heading ("1. Purpose of Agreement —"), company and employee signature blocks, then two exhibits (A: excluded prior inventions list, B: Defend Trade Secrets Act notice). Company letterhead logo on top and an address footer on each page.
- Register: first person from the employee ("I agree", "I will"), the company in the third person; formal legal prose, long sentences, defined terms in quotes; the advice-of-counsel clause in capitals.
- Recurring elements: defined terms built up across clauses (Inventions, Assigned Inventions, Excluded Inventions, Proprietary Information, Third Party Materials, Moral Rights); cross-references by section number; most obligations stated as running "during my employment and after its termination".
- Signer and context: an employee signs; the stated consideration is employment ("in consideration of, and as a condition of my employment"), made retroactive to the first day of any service. No host document: filed as its own exhibit to the S-1.
- Form origin: titled with the company's name ("Squarespace, Inc. Employee Invention Assignment and Confidentiality Agreement") and on company letterhead; signature blocks left blank; no "Form of" label, no law-firm footer or document number. The title is shared with other filers' forms (see the rule 4 watch in `candidates.md`). One typo in a heading ("COVENENT NOT TO COMPETE"). No US state invention-assignment limit notice (California Labor Code § 2870 or similar) appears anywhere, though the assignment reaches inventions made outside work.
