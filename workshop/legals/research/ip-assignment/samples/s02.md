## Document
- Shape: one standalone agreement (EX-10.11 to a 10-K/A) titled as a combined confidentiality, non-competition, non-solicitation and inventions assignment agreement; preamble and recitals, eight numbered sections with lettered sub-clauses, signature page. The IP assignment is section 4 of 8; restrictive covenants take as much room as the assignment. The whole exhibit is the sample.
- Register: formal US contract drafting, third person ("Executive", "Company"), long single-sentence clauses with "including, but not limited to" lists; capitalised defined terms ("Confidential Information", "Inventions", "Competing", "Restricted Period", "Restricted Territory", "Prior Inventions").
- Recurring elements: "for any reason or for no reason" for every termination trigger; obligations run both during and after employment; repeated cross-references to Sections 2, 3 and 4 as the protected core; a footnote on how "trade secrets" is read; New York law named in the footnote and the governing-law clause.
- Signer and context: one named executive signs; the company countersigns by an officer. Given "as a condition of" employment and effective with a separate Executive Employment Agreement, which it points to for notice addresses and for the executive's permitted "Outside Activities". Section 1 states that the consideration is separate from the offer of employment itself. No host agreement in the file.
- Form origin: an executed copy, not a "Form of": the executive's and company's names, signatures and dates are filled in. The company appears under its former name (AeroClean Technologies), and the competing-business definition names its product line. No law-firm footer or document number. Drafting seams suggest a text assembled from more than one template: section 4 restarts its lettering ((a)-(c) twice), and 7(a) refers to "Section 2" for the prohibited employment and its temporal and geographic scope, which section 3 holds. Exhibit A (prior inventions) is referenced but not included in the filing.
