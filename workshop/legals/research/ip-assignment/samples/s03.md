## Document
- Shape: short standalone deed-style contract: title, party line, "Recitals" block of WHEREAS clauses, "Agreement" with nine numbered sections, signature page set off by bracketed start and end markers, then two schedules listing the registered IP assigned. No table of contents, no confidentiality or future-inventions terms: it transfers existing, identified IP once.
- Register: formal US contract drafting, third person with capitalised defined terms ("Assignor", "Assignee", "Modular"); long run-on sentences; some defined terms used without definition (Capital Raise, Affiliates, Parties).
- Recurring elements: effective date in the party line; defined terms in quotes and brackets; page numbers; "[Remainder of page intentionally left blank]"; schedules referenced from the definitions.
- Signer and context: an individual inventor (the founder, who also signs for the assignee as its CEO) assigns his patents and related IP to a small company that is being acquired by the filer in a share exchange; the filer is a third party to the deed and a named beneficiary. Consideration as written is the acquisition (shares of the parent issued to the assignor) and a capital raise whose proceeds fund the assignee. No host document: standalone exhibit.
- Form origin: an executed text, not a "Form of": real names, addresses and dates filled in, no law-firm footer or document number. The governing-law clause speaks of "releases among New York residents", wording carried over from a different agreement, and a recital says "therein" for "herein", so the text looks adapted from another template rather than drafted fresh.
