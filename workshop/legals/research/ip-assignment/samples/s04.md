## Document
- Shape: standalone agreement (EX-10.1 to a 10-Q) whose title names a state version, "(ARIZONA)", and four subjects: non-competition, non-solicitation of employees, confidential information and inventions assignment. One-paragraph preamble, then 11 numbered sections; sections 1, 2 and 11 are split into decimal subsections (1.1-1.5, 2.1-2.9, 11.1-11.11) with bold run-in headings, and 2.3 has lettered sub-clauses (a) and (b). Then a signature page, with a "Prior Inventions" schedule below the signatures on the same page. The IP assignment is section 2 of 11. Confidentiality (section 1) and the restrictive covenants (sections 4-6) together run longer than it. Filed as scanned page images with a hidden text layer; every page carries a footer with a short title and page number.
- Register: first person from the employee ("I agree", "I will"), the company in the third person. Formal US contract prose, long sentences, "by way of illustration but not limitation" lists. Defined terms in bold italics; the advice-of-counsel clause in bold capitals.
- Recurring elements: two defined parties used on purpose: "Employer" (the contracting entity) takes the assignment, the power of attorney and the signature, while "Company" (Employer with its parents, subsidiaries, affiliates, successors and assigns) holds the confidentiality and covenant protections. Cross-references by section number. Obligations framed as "during my employment" or "during my employment and thereafter". A two-year "Look Back Period" is defined in section 5 and reused by the non-compete.
- Signer and context: one employee signs, a senior officer of the filer. An officer of the employer countersigns (signature, printed name, title). Given "in consideration of my employment or continued employment", pay, and access to confidential information. The entire-agreement clause states it replaces an earlier, dated "Employee Confidential Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement" the same employee signed, so this is a re-papering that adds state-specific restrictive covenants. Effective on the employee's signature date. No host document: filed as its own exhibit.
- Form origin: an executed copy, not a "Form of": both signatures ("/s/"), printed names, the employer signer's title and the date are filled in, and the "No Prior Inventions" box is ticked. The page footer gives a shorter title ("Employee Confidential Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement") than the heading, which suggests the company's standard invention-assignment form with non-compete and non-solicit sections added for one state. Drafting seams: a typo in the title ("NON-SOLICTATION") and one in section 6 ("to meat any time"). Section 9 splits an arbitral body's fees (naming JAMS as an example) although the agreement has no arbitration clause. The non-assignable-inventions carve-out follows the California Labor Code § 2870 test but names no statute. No law-firm footer or document number.
