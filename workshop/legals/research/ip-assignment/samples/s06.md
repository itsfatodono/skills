## Document
- Shape: the exhibit is an Executive Employment Agreement (COO) with three exhibits; Exhibit B is the "Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement" (CIIAA), extracted here with host clause 5.2 that requires it. The CIIAA has 12 numbered sections, most split into numbered sub-clauses with run-in headings, an effective-date line, employee and company signature blocks, then its own Exhibit A (California Labor Code § 2872 notice with an acknowledgement signature) and Exhibit B (Previous Inventions memo form). Page numbers restart at 1 inside the CIIAA.
- Register: first person from the employee ("I agree", "I hereby assign"), the company in the third person and often without "the" ("Company has a protectable interest"); formal legal prose with long run-on lists; defined terms in quotes, some defined twice; the advice-of-counsel clause in capitals.
- Recurring elements: defined terms (Confidential Information, Inventions, Intellectual Property Rights, Moral Rights, Trade Secrets, Third Party Information, Company Inventions, Prior Inventions, Work Product); obligations stated as running "during and after my employment"; carve-outs added "to the extent permitted by law". Drafting slips throughout: person shifts in the prior-consulting paragraph ("I have has performed", "I agrees"), "the my breach", "I will all do lawful acts", "shall not be effected", "County of California", a defined term that changes name mid-sentence ("Facilities Information Technology and Access Resources"). "Inventions" is defined in 1.2 (technology and IP) and again in 2.1 (a list that also takes in contracts, customer lists and sales information); 2.2 says it limits 2.1 although 2.1 holds only the disclosure duty.
- Signer and context: an executive employee (COO) signs; the stated consideration is employment and pay ("my employment by AppTech Corp. ... and the compensation now and hereafter paid to me"), and host clause 5.2 makes signing a condition of employment. The signer had worked for the company as an independent contractor before, and 1.1 pulls that period in. Host is the employment agreement; besides 5.2, host clauses lean on the CIIAA without assigning anything: 1.3 refers to "Exhibit B to the Confidentiality Agreement" for permitted interests (that exhibit is the prior-inventions form), 5.5 injunctive relief, 6.3 cause and 6.5 severance forfeiture on CIIAA breach, 6.11 return of property, 16 a separate DTSA notice, and the form of release (host Exhibit C, clause 6) reaffirms the CIIAA.
- Form origin: titled with the company's name ("AppTech Corp. Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement"); no "Form of" label, law-firm footer or document number; signature and date lines left blank though the host names the executive. The title is shared with other filers' forms (rule 4 watch in `candidates.md`). Several clauses share wording with common Silicon Valley CIIAA forms (2.2 three-part test with a § 2870 proviso, 2.7 moral rights, 12.9 advice of counsel) mixed with own text (security and IT access, export, non-disparagement).
