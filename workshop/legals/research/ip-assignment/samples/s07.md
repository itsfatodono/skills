## Document
- Shape: an executive offer letter (compensation, bonus, sign-on bonus, equity, severance, other details, signatures, Appendix A on vesting dates) with the assignment attached as Appendix B. The assignment has 9 numbered sections in capitals, most split into numbered sub-clauses with run-in headings (1.1, 1.2 ...); a "[Signature Page Follows]" marker, an effective-date line, dual signature blocks, then Exhibit B-1 holding the prior-inventions list and the state-specific notices. Only the host clauses that point to Appendix B and the whole of Appendix B are extracted.
- Register: first person from the employee ("I will", "I hereby assign"), the company in the third person and without an article ("Company"); formal legal prose with long enumerated sentences ((a), (b), (c)); the acceptance line above the signatures in capitals.
- Recurring elements: two defined parties ("Employer" is the contracting entity, "Company" adds subsidiaries, parents, affiliates, successors and assigns); defined terms built up across sections (Confidential Information, Third Party Information, Invention, Intellectual Property Rights, Moral Rights, Prior Inventions, Company Inventions, Governmental Entity); cross-references by subsection title ("the subsection titled Government or Third Party") and by number; obligations stated as running "during and after my employment"; Exhibit B-1 named as the place where state law modifies the text.
- Signer and context: an employee (here a newly hired senior officer) signs; consideration stated as employment or continued employment, pay, and access to confidential information. Host document: the offer letter, which makes signing the assignment a condition of employment and names it as part of the complete employment agreement. The filed copy is executed: signatures, the employee's initials on the arbitration acknowledgement, and the "None" box ticked on the prior-inventions list.
- Form origin: the offer letter calls it "Snowflake's standard form of Employee Proprietary Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement"; the filer's name appears in the preamble and signature block; no law-firm footer or document number. The title is shared with other filers' forms (see the rule 4 watch in `candidates.md`). Exhibit B-1 is headed "(as applicable)" and holds only the California notice, though sections 2.3 and 5 say it modifies terms for "certain states" and by jurisdiction; the employee in this copy works in Georgia.
