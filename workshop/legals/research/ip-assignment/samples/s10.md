## Document
- Shape: standalone form, no cover or table of contents. Title, one unnumbered preamble paragraph (parties, consideration, effective date), then 18 numbered sections with decimal sub-sections; the invention-assignment part is one section (9) of eight sub-sections placed after the restrictive covenants. Definitions sit in the middle (12), not first. Signature page, then three appendices: A, a prior/excluded-inventions disclosure form; B, the state invention-assignment notices quoted from eleven statutes; C, sixteen state covenant addenda (California to Wisconsin), each amending named sections of the main text and each with its own acknowledgment and signature lines. The appendices make up more than half the text.
- Register: formal US employment drafting in the third person; "Employee" and "the Company" throughout, no "I/you" except in the signed acknowledgment lines ("I represent", "I acknowledge"). Long sentences with lettered lists in brackets; "promises and agrees", "directly or indirectly", "alone or with any other(s)". Mostly careful, with visible drafting slips: an unfinished sentence in 9.4, "subjection" for "subsection" in 9.8, a breach clause that calls its fixed sums a "penalty", and addenda that point to sections the main text does not have (Wisconsin amends "Section 2(b)", Minnesota cites a "Minnesota Addendum set forth in Appendix B").
- Recurring elements: running footer on every page with the title, "(USA Version)", page number and the month and year of the form; capitalised defined terms collected in section 12; cross-references by section number; each appendix page footed "Appendix A/B/C"; each state addendum opens with the same "Covered [State] Employee" definition (residence or primary work location) and closes with the same acknowledgment paragraph and print-name, sign and date lines.
- Signer and context: an employee (new or current). The stated consideration is eligibility for the company's bonus, equity, commission and other incentive programmes in the year of signing and later years, plus employment or continued employment and the mutual promises. Effective on the later signature date, or later where a state addendum requires a notice period. No host document: the restrictive covenants (non-compete, customer and employee non-solicit, confidentiality, liquidated sums for breach) and the invention assignment are one agreement.
- Form origin: an unsigned "USA Version" form dated December 2024 in the footer. The employing entity is a bracketed choice between two group companies in the preamble and signature block; the addenda name only one of them. No law-firm footer or document number. The cross-references in the Wisconsin and Minnesota addenda to sections and appendices this text lacks suggest the addenda were carried over from an earlier or sibling form.
