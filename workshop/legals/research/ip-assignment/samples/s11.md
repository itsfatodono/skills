## Document
- Shape: standalone agreement: title, a one-sentence preamble, 8 numbered sections (2, 3, 7 and 8 split into lettered sub-clauses with run-in headings), a signature page, then Exhibit A (prior inventions list). The text points to an Exhibit B (California Labor Code § 2870) and an Exhibit C (Termination Certification), and 2(c) to an unlettered "Exhibit" listing earlier restrictive agreements; none of these is in the filing, which ends after Exhibit A.
- Register: first person from the employee ("I agree", "I hereby assign"), the company in the third person; formal US legal prose, long sentences, defined terms in quotes and capitals (Relationship, Confidential Information, Invention, Intellectual Property Rights, Moral Rights, Prior Inventions, Company Inventions). The advice-of-counsel clause is in capitals. Careless in places: a missing noun ("all Confidential shall be"), "perpetual" twice in one licence, 3(d) refers to itself as "Section 5", the signature page calls the signer "Service Provider" while the body says employee, and the Termination Certification is also called "Termination Certificate".
- Recurring elements: the defined "Relationship" as the period obligations run in ("during the term of my Relationship ... and thereafter"); cross-references by section and exhibit; the company defined to include subsidiaries, affiliates, successors and assigns; page numbers.
- Signer and context: an employee signs, given as a condition of being hired or of continued employment, in return for the employment and the pay. In this copy the signer is the CEO, who signs both as the individual and for the company, dated 2015; the effective date is the start of his service. No host document: filed as its own exhibit to the S-1.
- Form origin: no "Form of" label, no law-firm footer or document number; the company's name is in the title and the text, not only in blanks. Title shared with other filers' forms (see the rule 4 watch in `candidates.md`). The "Service Provider" label on the signature page and the consulting-relationship wording in Section 5 suggest a form adapted from one that also served contractors. No Defend Trade Secrets Act notice (the signed date is before that Act).
