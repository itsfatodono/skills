## Document
- Shape: standalone employee PIIA filed as an 8-K exhibit (EX-10.37). Company name and title on top, one preamble paragraph (condition of employment, consideration, effective date), thirteen numbered sections with headed lettered sub-clauses, a signature page, then Exhibit A (list of other inventions) and Exhibit B (termination certification). No definitions section: terms are defined inline where first used. Besides the IP assignment it carries a full restrictive-covenant set: employee non-solicit, customer non-interference and a one-year non-compete with a worldwide territory.
- Register: first person for the employee ("I agree", "I understand"), the company in the third person; capitalised defined terms. Section 1 and governing law in full capitals. Careless in places: "I and hereby irrevocably assign", a doubled full stop, "deemed to by the language", "its employment obligations" for the employee, Section 8 pointing to records "maintained pursuant to Section 3(d)" when they are kept under 3(c), Exhibit B naming "Foxx Development Inc." against "Holdings Inc." above, and stray square brackets left from a template (a closing "]" after the Territory definition with no opening one; an opening "[" before the arbitration-fee sentence with no closing one).
- Recurring elements: headed numbered sections with lettered sub-clauses; inline defined terms in quotation marks; cross-references to Sections 3(a) and 3(d); page numbers; "(Remainder of this page intentionally left blank)"; signature blocks for both parties with "By:" lines; blank-line exhibits for the employee to fill and sign.
- Signer and context: the employee. Given as a condition of employment and in consideration of receiving confidential information and the compensation paid now and later; the company also promises to provide confidential information (2(f)). Effective on the earlier of signing or of an employment agreement and NDA being entered into. No host document: standalone, although it names the employment agreement and NDA it runs alongside. At-will employment restated.
- Form origin: the exhibit description and filing title say "Form of"; the employee name is a blank "[INSERT EMPLOYEE NAME]", the filer's name is typed in throughout. No law-firm footer or document number. The template's own brackets survive in two places, and the structure (no-employment-contract clause first, "Other Inventions" exception with Exhibit A, Termination Certification as Exhibit B, notification to new employer) follows the common US startup PIIA form; the Texas non-compete, Texas law and Austin arbitration are local additions.
