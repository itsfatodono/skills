## Document
- Shape: executed offer letter to a new chief financial officer, filed as a 10-K exhibit (EX-10.23A), with the company's employee confidential information and inventions assignment agreement attached as Attachment A. Only the attachment and the three host paragraphs that point to it are extracted; the letter's pay, equity, benefits and at-will sections are not. The letter also names a change-of-control severance agreement as Attachment B, which is not in the file. Attachment A: a cover sheet, title, one preamble paragraph, thirteen numbered sections (2, 3 and 13 with headed lettered sub-clauses), an acknowledgment in capitals with signature blocks, then five exhibits: A list of prior inventions, B text of California Labor Code § 2870, C termination certification, D photography consent and release, E conflict of interest guidelines. No definitions section: terms are defined inline. Besides the IP assignment it carries a ban on competing work during employment, a 24-month employee and customer non-solicit, a non-disparagement clause, a photography release and a conduct policy.
- Register: the agreement is in the first person for the employee ("I agree"), the company in the third person; the host letter speaks to "you" from the company. Capitalised defined terms. The acknowledgment and Exhibit D's closing line are in full capitals. Careless in places: the disclosure sentence in 3(b) has lost its object ("will promptly make full written disclosure to the Company, will hold in trust"), the DTSA section inside the agreement says "Nothing in this Certification", copied from Exhibit C, and the signer's name is written in two forms. The photography release carries a date before the offer letter's.
- Recurring elements: numbered sections with headed lettered sub-clauses; inline defined terms in quotation marks; references to the exhibits by letter; page numbers; "[SIGNATURE PAGE FOLLOWS]"; the DTSA text quoted in full twice (Section 2(d) and Exhibit C); signature lines for the employee on the agreement, Exhibit C and Exhibit D.
- Signer and context: the employee, here an officer (CFO). Signed as a condition of employment and in consideration of employment and present and future pay; effective from the first day of employment. The host is the offer letter, which makes signing Attachment A a condition of the offer and lets its own outside-activity paragraph override the agreement in a conflict. At-will employment is stated in both documents.
- Form origin: the cover sheet titles it the company's "2010" agreement, so a company form dated by year and reused; the employee's name is typed into the preamble, the company's name runs throughout, including Exhibits C to E. No "Form of", no law-firm footer or document number. The structure (at-will first, prior-inventions list as Exhibit A, § 2870 text as Exhibit B, termination certification as Exhibit C, notification to new employer) follows the common California employee CIIAA form; the DTSA section, the photography release, the conflict-of-interest exhibit, the non-disparagement clause and the biotech items in the confidential-information list (cell lines, plasmids) are additions.
