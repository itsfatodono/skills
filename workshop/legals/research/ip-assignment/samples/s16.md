## Document
- Shape: an offer letter (12 numbered clauses, signatures) whose clause 7 points to the attached agreement; then, under "FOR REFERENCE ONLY / Exhibit 1", the assignment agreement: title, a one-sentence preamble, sections numbered 1, 2, 4 to 12 (there is no Section 3), most split into lettered sub-clauses with run-in headings, a signature block, then Exhibit A (prior inventions list), Exhibit B (California Labor Code § 2870 text) and Exhibit C (conflict of interest guidelines, 13 numbered items). After it the host's Exhibit 2, a Mutual Agreement to Arbitrate, which belongs to the offer letter and is not extracted (Section 12.A of the assignment points to it).
- Register: first person from the employee ("I agree", "I understand", "I hereby irrevocably assign"), the company in the third person as "Figure" or "the Company"; formal US legal prose with long sentences, defined terms in quotes and capitals (Company Confidential Information, Associated Third Parties, Inventions, Prior Inventions, Moral Rights, Electronic Media Equipment and Systems). The assignment exception in 4.G is in capitals. Careless in places: cross-references run one number off (2.B calls itself "Section 3.B", Section 8 calls itself "Section 9" and points to "Section 3" for confidentiality), missing full stops ("sole property of Figure I also agree"), a stray semicolon in 2.B, "Parties" used in 12.D without definition, and the defined name "Confidential Information Agreement" set up in the preamble but the text says "this Agreement".
- Recurring elements: "during my employment ... and thereafter" as the period of duties; cross-references by section and exhibit; the company defined to include subsidiaries, affiliates, successors and assigns; officers named by title (President, CEO, General Counsel, Director of People) as those who authorise or receive notices; "to the extent permitted by law" hedges.
- Signer and context: an employee signs, given as a condition of employment and in return for the employment and the pay. In this copy a newly hired executive signs, and the Chief People Officer signs for the company, same day as the offer letter. Host document: a letter agreement offering employment (EDGAR calls it a compensation agreement) that sets title, pay, bonus, options and at-will status; its clause 7 makes the attached agreement a condition of employment. Section 1 of the assignment also reaches back over a period of earlier engagement before the employment.
- Form origin: the host calls it the company's "standard" agreement and the attachment is marked "FOR REFERENCE ONLY". The host names it "At-Will Employment, Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement", the attachment's own title drops "At-Will Employment", and its numbering skips Section 3 with cross-references off by one: a section (likely the at-will clause) was cut from a longer form without renumbering. The company's name is written into the body, not only in blanks. No law-firm footer or document number; Workiva source file. Title shared with other filers' forms (see the rule 4 watch in `candidates.md`). No Defend Trade Secrets Act notice although signed after that Act.
