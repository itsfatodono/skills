## Document
- Shape: a consulting agreement (recitals, six numbered sections, signature page) with Exhibit A (commercial terms: services, fee, options, bonuses, expenses, insurance, days off; not extracted) and Exhibit B, the Non-Competition, Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement (the sample). Exhibit B has a preamble, a one-line consideration paragraph, and numbered sections 5 to 10 with decimal sub-clauses (5.1 to 5.6, 6.1 to 6.9, 7, 8, 9, 10.1 to 10.5) under bold run-in headings, then a two-column signature block. The numbering starts at 5 and two cross-references point to sections that do not exist in it ("Section 2.2 below", "Section 1.5"), so the text was moved from a form numbered from 1. No prior-inventions schedule, although "Prior Inventions" is defined; no state-law notice, no exit certificate.
- Register: third person throughout, "Consultant" without article and "Company"; "he/she" and "its/his/her" for the consultant; formal, long sentences with open "including without limitation" lists; capitalised defined terms in quotes, some borrowed from the host ("all capitalized terms ... in the Consulting Agreement"). Several typos and slips ("that that", "use of disclosure", "offer to sell use", "a any claims").
- Recurring elements: footer "Proprietary and Confidential Page N of 15" on every page; cross-references from the host to "Section 5" and "Exhibit B"; Israeli statutes named inside an agreement governed by New York law.
- Signer and context: an individual consultant who, per Exhibit A, provides "Professional CEO services" full time and exclusively, paid a monthly fee plus recommended option grants and bonuses; the PIIA is given "as a condition and part of" the consulting engagement "for no additional consideration", and the host agreement's fee is stated to be the sole consideration for everything, IP included. Host document: a consulting agreement governed by New Jersey law that incorporates the PIIA by reference (Section 5), makes its breach a ground for immediate termination (3.2(c)), lists Section 5 among surviving terms (3.3) and has the consultant warrant that assigned IP is original and unencumbered (4.2.1(v)-(vi)); the host also carries independent-contractor and employer-employee indemnity clauses typical of Israeli practice.
- Form origin: an executed text, not a "Form of": the consultant's name and an identity-document number are filled in and the consultant's signature shown as /s/; the company's signatory name and title are left as blank lines. No law-firm footer or document number. The text points to Israeli drafting (Israeli Patents Law § 134 and Copyright Law § 45 named, reasonableness and "special trust" acknowledgements backing the non-compete, "Ordinary Shares" in Exhibit A), and its title matches another Israeli form in the pool (c23); the renumbering from 5 and stale cross-references show a standalone house form pasted in as an exhibit.
