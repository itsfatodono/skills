## Document
- Shape: an Israeli employment agreement (10 numbered sections with decimal sub-clauses, signature block) followed by five exhibits: A employment terms table, B the Non-Competition, Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement (the sample), C a Hebrew general approval under Section 14 of the Severance Pay Law, D its English summary, E a company computer policy consent (C, D, E not extracted). Exhibit B has 13 numbered sections, the first two split into lettered sub-sections with run-in headings, an untitled preamble, a short recital headed "AGREEMENT", and a two-column signature block. No prior-inventions schedule: the employee instead represents in 2(a) that there are none. No table of contents, no definitions section: terms are defined where first used, some after first use.
- Register: third person throughout ("Employee", "Company", "he or she"); formal US-style drafting with very long sentences and long open lists ("including but not limited to"); capitalised defined terms in quotes. Loose drafting in places: "Assignable Inventions" is used in 2(b) before 2(c) defines it, "Company Inventions" is never defined, the 2(e) exception is garbled ("which useful with and does not relate"), host 8.3 slips into the first person ("my rights"), host 9.2 points to "this Section 8" from inside Section 9, and Exhibit B's signature line repeats Exhibit A's "Employment Terms Agreement".
- Recurring elements: a "Kaltura Confidential Page N of 16" footer on every page; DocuSign envelope id on every page; cross-references by section and letter (Section 2(b), 2(e), Paragraph 5(a)); Israeli statutes cited by section number and year.
- Signer and context: an employee (a finance vice-president) signs; the host agreement's clause 8.1 makes signing Exhibit B a condition of employment, and Exhibit B itself names employment, the compensation and the benefits as consideration. Effective from the first day of employment. The host also carries IP-related clauses of its own: injunctive relief for breach of Exhibit B (8.2), salary as full consideration with a waiver of statutory royalty claims (8.3), and an open-source and malicious-code ban on work product (8.4). Host document: the employment agreement, governed by Israeli law, which also serves as the statutory notice of employment terms.
- Form origin: an executed text, not a "Form of": the employee's name, the HR officer's name and the DocuSign signature date are filled in, while some blanks (effective date, the employee's name line) stay empty in the filing. No law-firm footer or document number. The text reads as a US invention-assignment form adapted to Israel: it keeps US "works made for hire" language, a no-employment-contract clause, "the laws of the state" in the non-compete reformation clause and a US-statute-shaped exception to assignment, and adds Israeli Patent Law and Copyright Law provisions and a fixed "Special Compensation". Its title is shared with another Israeli filer's form in this pool.
