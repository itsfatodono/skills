## Document
- Shape: short standalone agreement of about one and a half pages: title, dated opening line, two party lines (assignor, assignee), two "WHEREAS" recitals, a "NOW, THEREFORE" paragraph that already assigns, then nine numbered headed sections (only section 4 has numbered sub-clauses, two levels deep), then a witness line and two signature blocks. No definitions section (the one defined term, "Intellectual Property", is set in the recital), no schedules or exhibits, no confidentiality, non-compete, moral-rights, power-of-attorney or employment terms: a one-time transfer of one described platform for a nominal sum.
- Register: plain US commercial drafting, third person with capitalised roles ("Assignor", "Assignee"); short operative clauses, long boilerplate warranty and venue clauses. Careless in places: "2014between", "IN WITNESS WHHEREOF", "no rights of any third party is prejudiced", an undefined "Company" in the assignee's warranties, and a non-conflict warranty (4.2.4) that has lost its "will not", so it reads as warranting a breach.
- Recurring elements: capitalised party roles; numbered headed sections; mutual warranties in parallel lists (assignor then assignee); dated opening line; "/s/" signature blocks.
- Signer and context: the founder, as an individual assignor, assigns the hotel-booking platform he designed, which the recital calls the basis of the company's business, to the company. Given for a nominal sum of ten dollars. He also signs for the company as its chief executive officer, so one person signs both blocks. No host document: standalone exhibit, dated two days before the S-1/A that files it.
- Form origin: an executed text, not a "Form of": real names, the company's address and the date filled in, signatures as "/s/". No law-firm footer or document number. Reads as a short generic asset-assignment template adapted for IP, with corporate-transaction boilerplate (stockholder and creditor consents, articles and bylaws) carried into the assignee's warranties.
