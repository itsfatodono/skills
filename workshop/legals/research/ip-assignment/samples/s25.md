## Document
- Shape: a standalone employee agreement: title, one-paragraph preamble, 9 numbered sections (1 Nondisclosure, 2 Work Product, 3 Security, 4 Records, 5 Restrictive Covenants, 6 No Conflicting Obligation, 7 Exit Obligations, 8 Notification of New Employer, 9 General Provisions) with run-in headed sub-sections and lettered and roman sub-clauses, an effective-date line, a two-party signature block, then Exhibit A (an empty schedule of existing personal investments) and Exhibit B (a one-line Colorado attestation addendum with its own signature block). One footnote ("Note to Form") marks a bracketed optional clause. No table of contents, no definitions section: terms are defined in quotes where first used. No prior-inventions schedule: the list is delivered separately after signing.
- Register: first person for the employee ("I agree and covenant", "my employment"), the Company in the third person; formal US drafting with very long sentences and long open lists ("including without limitation"); one clause (9.10) in capitals. Loose numbering: the sub-sections of sections 2, 5 and 9 are numbered 1.1, 1.2 and so on, and section 5 restarts at 1.1 after 1.3, while cross-references use the intended numbers (5.2(a), 5.3, 5.6); this table uses the intended numbers. The title ("Employee Restrictive Covenants, Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement") and the preamble's name ("Employee Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement") differ. Some typos ("fault or mine", "the limited allowed").
- Recurring elements: page numbers; cross-references by section and letter; statutes cited by section number (17 U.S.C. § 101, 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), C.R.S. § 24-34-407); defined terms reused across sections (Proprietary Information, Inventions, Work Product, Business, Geographic Scope, Post-Termination Non-Compete Term).
- Signer and context: an employee signs, with the Company countersigning; given in consideration of employment or continued employment and the compensation paid; the post-employment non-compete is aimed at executives and management staff and its length is tied to the severance period of a separate executive change-in-control and severance plan. Standalone, no host document. Governed by Colorado law.
- Form origin: a form, not an executed text: the filename says "form of", a footnote reads "Note to Form", the practice-of-law clause is bracketed as optional for attorneys, and the effective date, names and dates are blanks. No law-firm footer or document number. The Company's name and its named subsidiaries are written in, not left blank. The title mismatch with the preamble suggests a PIIA extended with a restrictive-covenants section.
