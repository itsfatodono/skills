## Document
- Shape: title, "Effective as of" date, numbered table of contents (13 sections), then 13 numbered sections. The long middle sections (2-5) are built from multi-column tables; the rest is short prose and bullet lists. Section 11 is a statistics table of rights requests.
- Register: plain, "we/you", short sentences, contractions ("we'll", "It's your right to..."); defined terms introduced with "From now on, we'll call it...". No GDPR-style legal-basis vocabulary: the US version names purposes and data categories only, and refers to "certain U.S. states" and the CCPA rather than citing articles.
- Recurring elements: effective date at top; links out to companion resources (Safety & Privacy Center, Account Privacy, Cookies Policy, explainer videos, California Notice at Collection) in sections 1-4; "contact us" as a fallback in nearly every rights row; capitalised data-category names (User Data, Usage Data, ...) defined in section 3 and reused as the join key in the section 4 and 5 tables; "For example, ..." sentence after almost every rule; separate under-18 carve-outs inside the advertising and marketing rows; DPO email, postal address and controller entity at the end.
