## Document
- Shape: one long page titled "Telegram Privacy Policy"; 12 numbered sections with decimal subsections (up to four levels, e.g. 6.5.1); no summary box and no separate table of contents beyond a prose list in 1.3.
- Register: plain and conversational, "we/you", with jokes and blunt asides; explains product mechanics (encryption, chat types) in depth; frequent cross-references by section number.
- Recurring elements: in-app settings paths ("Settings > Privacy & Security > Data Settings"); "As of <date/version>" feature notes; product-feature subsections rather than generic data categories; dated changelog inside section 11 instead of a single "last updated" line; contact routes are in-app support and the EEA representative, no email address.
- Controller named in the text is the group's US entity, while the App Store seller is a sister group company named in 8.2.
