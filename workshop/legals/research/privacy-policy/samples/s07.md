## Document
- Shape: web page with a title, one-line purpose statement, effective date and a PDF printer-friendly link on top, then a jump-link list of seven parts; three short framing blocks (commitment, scope, exclusions, related notices) precede the body; the main parts are accordions ("Expand All") whose panels are question-style headings ("How does Capital One ...?"); later parts (retention and security, children, social media, updates) are plain all-caps headings; ends with a contact card.
- Register: formal but readable, "we/you", defined capitalised terms ("Online Services", "Device Data", "Online/Mobile Activity Data"); heavy use of "may" and "such as" lists; every category is a bolded lead-in plus an example list.
- Recurring elements: effective date in the header (the Updates section points back to it); repeated inline links to the online tracking section ("Learn more about how we use online tracking technology"); repeated cross-references to the separate U.S. Consumer Privacy Notice (GLBA), CCPA Disclosure and Workforce CCPA Disclosure; live GPC detection widget embedded in the opt-out panel; footer link "Your California Privacy Choices".
- Scope: online (web and app) practices only; financial-product data is deferred to the GLBA notice, California rights to the CCPA disclosure, children to a separate children's policy.
