## Document
- Shape: umbrella policy for an account that spans several products; short preamble that links
  out to nine product-specific privacy policies, then seven numbered sections. Section 2 is a
  run of numbered paragraphs (2.1–2.14), each opening with a bold-style label ("2.5 IP
  logging:"). Section 4 lists processors as name + bulleted Purpose / Location / Transfer
  guarantee entries.
- Register: plain but formal, "we/you", with a privacy-advocacy voice ("collect as little ... as
  possible"); states the legal basis inline inside almost every processing paragraph rather than
  in a separate legal-basis section.
- Recurring elements: "Last modified" date above the title; "Learn more" links to help articles
  after technical paragraphs; cross-references to section numbers (one is stale: processors cite
  "section 2.8" for payments, which sit in 2.9); legal basis sentence ending each processing
  paragraph; English-prevails clause at the end. No separate cookies, retention, children,
  security or contact-details section; retention is described per item inside section 2.
