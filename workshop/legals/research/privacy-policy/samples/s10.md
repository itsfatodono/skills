## Document
- Shape: two-column layout; every numbered subsection opens with a one-sentence plain summary (shown beside or above the full text), followed by the full paragraphs. Five numbered parts (Data We Collect, How We Use, How We Share, Your Choices & Obligations, Other Important Information), preceded by an unnumbered introduction. Short table of contents at the top. "Key Terms" sidebar boxes close several parts with glossary definitions (Affiliates, Social Action, Partners, Consent).
- Register: plain, "we/you", product-specific examples throughout ("e.g." clauses in nearly every paragraph); defined capitalized terms (Members, Visitors, Services, Designated Countries, Affiliates) set in quotes on first use.
- Recurring elements: effective date under the title; frequent "Learn more" links out to Help Center, Cookie Policy, European Regional Privacy Notice and California disclosure; "subject to your settings" qualifier repeated across use and sharing sections; regional carve-outs (Designated Countries / UK) pointed to a separate notice rather than inlined; contact handled by links (online form, DPO form), no email address in text.
