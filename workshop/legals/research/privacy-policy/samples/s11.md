## Document
- Shape: friendly intro plus a check-mark list of key points on top, then an overview, a linked table of contents, and nine numbered sections; most numbered sections open with a one-line "Summary:"; long sections split into unnumbered subheadings.
- Register: "we/you"; the intro is warm and joking, the body openly switches to formal legal drafting ("it reads like a legal document"); defined terms in quotes ("Services", "Financial Data", "Plan").
- Recurring elements: "Last Modified" and separate "Effective" date at the top; privacy@ynab.com repeated in nearly every section, with in-app chat as an alternative; cross-links to a separate Cookie Policy, U.S. State Privacy Notice, Applicant Privacy Policy, Security Policy, Terms and a Data Subject Rights Request form; footer carries a regulatory agency disclosure.
