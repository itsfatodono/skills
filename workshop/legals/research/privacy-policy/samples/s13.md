## Document
- Shape: one long page on the help center; linked table of contents at the top; short intro and contact block, then lettered parts (Section A to F) each holding headed subsections; a product-specific supplement (games) appended as the last part; "Last Updated" date at the very end; language switcher below.
- Register: formal but readable, "we/you"; long bulleted lists with a bold lead-in label per item; defined terms capitalised and reused across sections ("Partners", "Service Providers", "Advertising Companies", "Netflix Marketing Providers").
- Recurring elements: every purpose and every recipient bullet ends with a fixed list of the data categories or purposes it uses, cross-referencing the category and purpose names defined earlier; many outbound links to help-center articles for detail (data download, deletion, retention, transfers, cookies, parental controls); privacy@netflix.com repeated in three places; controller identity delegated to a separate corporate-info page.
