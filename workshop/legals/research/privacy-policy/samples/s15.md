## Document
- Shape: iubenda-generated policy, embedded on bear.app/privacy in an iframe (with a "no-iframe version here" link to the iubenda URL). The default iubenda view stacks a short "Policy summary" layer (purposes, then per-service blocks with data types, then controller contact) above a "Full policy"; the `/full-legal` view, extracted here, is the full policy alone. Full policy: H1 title, one-line intro, then 9 top-level headings, several with H3 sub-blocks; definitions glossary at the end; no table of contents, no tables.
- Register: generator boilerplate, third person throughout ("the Owner", "the User", "this Application") with capitalised defined terms; neutral, mid-length sentences; generic GDPR wording not tailored to the product.
- Recurring elements: per-service blocks under a purpose heading (service name, provider in brackets, one-line description, "Personal Data processed", "Place of processing" with a link to the provider's policy); cross-references to other sections by quoted title; "Latest update" date at the bottom, not the top; generator credit footer ("created with the iubenda Privacy and Cookie Policy Generator") plus an iubenda hosting note.
- Coverage notes: covers only two processing purposes (website contact form, Mailchimp newsletter); says nothing about the note-taking app itself, sync, or iCloud. GDPR rights only; no US state, CCPA or children's section; no DPO or EU representative; retention by criteria only; recipient list "may be requested from the Owner". Company-written text: none on bear.app/privacy outside the iframe beyond the page title and the no-iframe link; inside the policy only the controller name, address, email, and the selected service blocks are company input.
