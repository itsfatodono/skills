## Document
- Shape: one long page; "Updated" date and a Spanish-version link on top, then a short changelog box, then question-style sections, a region-specific block, and an appendix table; a sticky in-page navigation lists every section.
- Register: plain-to-formal corporate, "we/you", every main heading phrased as a reader's question; heavy use of "may" hedging; definitions in bold-lead bullets ("Category, such as ...").
- Recurring elements: cross-references to other sections by quoted heading name; links out to companion notices (specialized operations, ALPR, VIZIO, California notice, FAQs); "Your Privacy Choices" link named as the rights channel in several places; sub-subheadings as plain bold paragraph labels rather than real headings.
