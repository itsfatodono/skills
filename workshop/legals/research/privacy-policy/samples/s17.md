## Document
- Shape: single long page; "Last Modified" date and a one-line commitment under the title, then a linked table of contents, 14 numbered sections, and four annexes (personal-data table, cookie policy, US residents notice, California/state rights). Annex 2 (about 1,400 words) is a full cookie policy: per rule 6 only its existence is recorded below.
- Register: formal, UK-legal, "we/you"; long sentences and lists. The annexes switch to capitalised "Member" and "FEELD" and US statutory terms ("Personal Data", "Valid Request"). Some typos and loose drafting; table-of-contents labels differ slightly from the headings.
- Recurring elements: cross-references to the annexes ("If you are a US resident, please see Annex 3") at the end of several main sections; privacy contact emails repeated throughout; section 12 says the date is "at the end" while it sits at the top.
- Sensitive-data product: dating app, so the special-category rules get their own topic notices inside section 3 (sexual data, SMS verification, age verification, session replay), each naming the vendor.
