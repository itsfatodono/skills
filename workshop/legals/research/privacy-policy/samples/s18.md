## Document
- Shape: "Version" date and "Last updated" date, list of the apps the policy covers, joint-controller block (two entities with role labels and addresses), then 12 numbered ALL-CAPS H2 sections forming the "Main Policy", then an "ANNEXES" part with one app-specific annex (Annex A, the flagship app) of 7 numbered H3 sub-sections. Layered by design: the introduction explains that the main policy covers cross-app data and annexes cover per-app (sometimes sensitive) data. Only one annex is published although five apps are listed.
- Register: plain, "we/you", medium-length sentences; GDPR-oriented throughout (principles listed, legal bases named, Article 6(1)(a) cited once in the annex); defined terms borrowed from the Terms of Service ("User Inputs", "User Content", "ToS") with "as this term is defined in the ToS". Bullets open with a bold-style label followed by a full sentence ("Right of Access. You can ...").
- Recurring elements: one privacy contact email repeated in most sections; cross-references to section numbers ("as described in Sections 5 and 7"); explicit "we do not ..." reassurances (do not sell, do not store card details, not used for marketing); app names used as examples of per-app processing; parent-subsidiary structure (EU operator, US parent) returned to in sharing, transfer and billing text.
