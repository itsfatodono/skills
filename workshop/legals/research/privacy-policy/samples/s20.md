## Document
- Shape: one long page titled "Privacy Policy"; a dated version note, a bulleted "key points" overview, a consent/acceptance paragraph and a linked table of contents come before 11 numbered sections, several split into unnumbered sub-headings; the core data inventory is a four-column table.
- Register: formal US-template legal style mixed with reassuring plain-language promises up front; "we/you"; heavy use of capitalized defined terms (Personal Data, Private Sensitive Personal Data, Child under the Protected Age, Minor, Valid Request, Service Providers, Advertising/Analytics/Fundraising Partners); later sections often cross-refer back to earlier ones instead of repeating them.
- Recurring elements: version note giving both a publication date and an effective date, with a link to prior versions; a single support email repeated across sections (with set subject lines for request types); asterisk footnotes on the table marking state-law "sensitive" data and minors' limits; product-specific features worked into the generic structure (community/groups, giving campaigns to churches, kids profiles, school accounts, AI features, Apple Health); named EU and UK representatives in a two-column contact table; English-version-controls line at the end. The site-wide cookie banner and preference center are separate from the policy text.
