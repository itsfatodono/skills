## Document
- Shape: title "Privacy Notice" + last-updated date, then 7 numbered H1 sections and an unnumbered contact section. Sections 2-4 are each built around one multi-column table followed by a "Please (also) note the following..." block of bolded-label paragraphs; section 6 is split into one sub-heading per right. No table of contents, no summary box.
- Register: plain, "we/you", short sentences, warm opener ("We respect the privacy of your data..."); legal terms kept to the minimum (legal bases named, then defined in one-line plain words). No statute articles cited.
- Recurring elements: last-updated date at top; support email repeated in intro, rights and contact; the data-type names from the section 2 table (Email address, UUID, Demographic Data, Health/Sleep Data, ...) reused as row labels in the section 3 table and as the "Data we disclose" column in section 4; "(where you have opted in)" qualifier on every consent use; in-app settings named as the primary control everywhere, email as fallback. Product-specific risks (audio picking up other people in the room, legacy users) get their own bolded notes. Not present: separate security, international-transfer, children or cookies sections; transfer location appears only as a table column ("EU").
