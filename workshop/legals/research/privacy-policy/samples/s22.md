## Document
- Shape: H1 title, two supplement pointers (California statement; Washington/Nevada consumer health
  data policy), a chatty welcome paragraph, effective-date line with a link to the previous version,
  then a linked table of contents and 12 numbered sections. Sections 3, 4, 5 and 8 are each built
  around tables (3: three tables split by source; 4: purpose / legal ground / data-category matrix;
  5: recipient / reason / data-category matrix; 8: right / how to exercise it). Contact section is
  split into one H4 per jurisdiction. A sign-off paragraph closes the document. No summary box.
- Register: deliberately playful brand voice ("Hey there!", "digital wingmate", "make your heart skip a
  beat") wrapped around precise legal content; "we/you"; legal bases and transfer mechanisms named
  by their GDPR terms but no articles cited. Jokes sit in section openers; table cells are dry.
- Recurring elements: a fixed set of named data categories (Account Data, Profile Data, Content,
  ... Face Data, ID Data) defined once in section 3 and reused as the data column in sections 4 and
  5; sections cross-reference each other by number ("as laid out in Section 4"); each section opens
  with a plain-words summary paragraph before the table ("Read on for more details"); jurisdiction
  scoping throughout ("If you live in...", "where required under applicable law"); many "here"
  links out to help-center articles, FAQs and feature-specific notices (face data, non-members,
  profiling); group-company (Match Group) sharing treated as its own topic. Not present: security
  section, cookies section (pointer to separate Cookie Policy only), last-updated date separate from
  the effective date.
