## Document
- Shape: one combined legal page. A two-sentence intro and anchor links come first, then the Terms of Service, then the Privacy Policy. Only the Privacy Policy part is extracted. It has a short intro paragraph and five short headed sections, with no table of contents of its own.
- Register: plain and brief, "we/you" mixed with the third-person "Signal". It leads with the product's encryption design and says what the company cannot access instead of listing what it collects. There is no legal-basis, rights or retention language.
- Recurring elements: bold run-in labels open each paragraph ("Account Information.", "Messages.", "Third Parties."). End-to-end encryption claims are repeated in the intro and in several sections. Both dates ("Effective as of" and "Updated") come at the very end and are the same 2018 date. One contact email, privacy@signal.org, is shared with the Terms.
- Defects: no GDPR/CCPA-specific content, no data-subject rights, no retention periods, no children's section and no international-transfer section in the policy itself. Transfer consent sits in the Terms instead. The text has not been updated since 2018.
