## Document
- Shape: two banner notices above the title (upcoming revision; enterprise agreements may override this policy), title with "Posted" and "Effective" dates plus a link to the previous version, a two-sentence intro, then seven short top-level parts named by question words (What & Why, With Whom, How, Where, Your Control and Access of Your Data, Changes, Contact). No table of contents, no numbering, no tables. Each part is a run of paragraphs that open with a bolded run-in label ("Account information.", "Retention."). Detail (legal bases, processor categories, AI/ML use, CCPA-style categories) is pushed out to a separate FAQ page linked from many paragraphs.
- Register: plain and friendly, "we/you", contractions ("we'll", "won't", "don't"), short paragraphs, opens with "Thanks for using Dropbox!". One brand defined term, "Your Stuff". Legal vocabulary appears only where required: the Data Privacy Framework passage, the controller/processor paragraph, and the lawful-disclosure list.
- Recurring elements: "provide, improve, protect, and promote our Services" repeated as the purpose formula across collection and sharing paragraphs; "For example, ..." sentences after rules; "please see our FAQ" / "Learn more here" / "click here" links in most paragraphs; privacy@dropbox.com as the single contact address (complaints, objections, DPO); Dropbox Team / admin carve-outs inside collection, sharing and controller paragraphs.
