## Document
- Shape: site banner announcing the new effective date with a link to the prior version; title + "Effective" date; untitled intro; then 9 unnumbered H3 sections. No table of contents. Every section opens with a one- or two-sentence italic summary of what the section tells the reader, then the full text. Long sections are split by plain unnumbered sub-headings (Information You Provide, Keep Things Up & Running, Recipients & Reasons for Sharing...). The region-specific part is only a link list to separate country notices.
- Register: very plain and conversational, "we/you", contractions, light humour and product examples ("like those latest sneakers", dog photos, coffee, basketball), "Pro tip:", "Let's get started". Defined terms are introduced inline in the intro ("Services", "Terms", "Snapchatter"). Data categories are named in plain words with the definition in parentheses right after. No legal-basis vocabulary in the main text; legal bases are pushed to a linked page.
- Recurring elements: italic summary lead per section; heavy "learn more here / go here" links out to companion pages (Privacy by Product, Privacy Center summary and video, data-to-purpose mapping table, service-provider category list, partner list, Cookie Policy, Support Site, Transparency Report, country notices); "For example, ..." after almost every claim; repeated promise that "private content and communications" are excluded from personalization, ads and sharing; product names as examples throughout (Memories, My AI, Spotlight, Snap Map, Family Center, SPECS, Bitmoji).
