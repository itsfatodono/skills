## Document
- Shape: "LAST UPDATED" date, title, a three-sentence summary paragraph, links to the company's other privacy notices, then one H2 per product area (security updates, browser features, Rewards, Ads, Wallet, premium services, how we improve Brave) with an H3 per feature. After the prose comes a block of collapsible per-feature tables (purpose / data / legal basis / storage duration). It closes with a rights list, EU representative, a one-paragraph change clause and a footnote that defines "personal data".
- Register: plain, "we/you", product-marketing tone mixed with privacy statements ("It's your choice."); the sections are written feature by feature, not by data category. Each feature says what leaves the device, what the company never sees, which third-party provider is involved (named: Google, AWS, Lingvanex, Stripe, 8x8, Guardian, Backtrace.io, Inca.Digital, 0x, Jupiter) and how long data is kept. Legal bases appear only in the tables, not in the prose.
- Recurring elements: negative assurances ("does not collect", "never leaves your device", "is not logged"); IP-stripping proxy described for several features; "See data processing details" in-page links to the matching table; "Learn more here" links to wiki/help pages; brave:// settings paths given as controls; a short "privacy protections" bullet list under the bigger features; a numeric retention given in prose for most features (24 hours, 14 days, 30 days, 12 months, 1 year); bolded "Note:" / "Please note:" / "Tip:" asides.
