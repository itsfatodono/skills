## Document
- Shape: single long page titled "Privacy Notice"; a "Last Modified" date and a scope paragraph, an Overview with a bulleted topic list, then about 12 H2 sections, and a closing H1 "Other Important Privacy Information" grouping six more H2s. Most sections are numbered lists whose items open with a bold run-in label. No summary box, no tables, no in-page links.
- Register: formal US corporate-legal "we/you", long multi-clause sentences, hedged with "may" and "such as"; lawful bases are written inline into each purpose ("as a matter of our legitimate interests") rather than in a separate table.
- Recurring elements: four capitalised defined data categories (Contact Information, User Account Information, User Activity Information, Content, plus Interactive Chat Information) set up in the collection section and reused by name in use, cookie and rights sections; cross-references to other sections by quoted heading, some of which do not match the actual headings; a single privacy email address repeated as the channel for every request; terms like Submissions and Output borrowed from the Terms of Service. Drafting flaws are visible: one cookie paragraph is printed twice, a sharing item carries stray bold markers, the Overview list says "California residents" while the heading says "U.S. Residents", and a biometric sentence is cut off mid-word.
