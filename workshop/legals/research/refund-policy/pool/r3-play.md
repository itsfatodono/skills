# refund-policy pool: r3-play

Route R3 (Google Play charts), storefront `hl=en&gl=US`, categories PRODUCTIVITY, EDUCATION, HEALTH_AND_FITNESS, DATING, COMMUNICATION, TOOLS, MUSIC_AND_AUDIO, PERSONALIZATION, BUSINESS. The category pages (`/store/apps/category/<CATEGORY>`) did not expose a separate top-grossing list to a plain fetch; I used the apps each page returns (its top-ranked mix), so rank here is not grossing rank. DATING returned no apps at all, and PERSONALIZATION and TOOLS had no candidate with a refund document that I could confirm, so those three categories contribute no rows. Downloads come from each `details?id=<pkg>` page; the Website comes from its App support section. The refund document was found by following R4 (web search `<company> refund policy`, then the developer's help center or site). Companies already in `r1-grossing-prod.md` and `r4-software.md` were skipped. Zendesk and Cloudflare-protected help centers returned 403 to curl, so those URLs are marked `(help) (blocked)` and were confirmed by the search result title and snippet, not by fetch. Most rows are help-center articles, and several mostly route app-store buyers to Apple or Google (`(store-pointer)`).
