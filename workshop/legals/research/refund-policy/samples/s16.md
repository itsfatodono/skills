## Document
- Framing: buyer-facing refund policy of a merchant of record. Paddle is the authorised reseller of other companies' software and digital products ("Suppliers"), so the policy governs refunds for products Paddle sells, not Paddle's own product; the buyer deals with Paddle, the supplier is a first stop only for defects. Expect it to differ from a seller's own policy (no product-specific terms, supplier-escalation step, reseller identity in the intro).
- Shape: single page in a legal tab set (Buyer Terms, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy, Summary of changes); H1 title, untitled intro of six short paragraphs, then 9 numbered H2 sections with decimal-numbered clauses (1.1, 2.2.1); section 2 has H5 country subheadings; date ("31 March 2026") at the very end, unlabelled; no table of contents, no tables.
- Register: formal but readable, "we/you" mixed with "Paddle" in the third person; capitalised defined terms taken from the Buyer Terms (Product, Transaction, Subscription, Supplier, Consumers, Businesses); short sentences, frequent "unless required by law" carve-outs.
- Recurring elements: cross-references between sections by number ("see section 3 below"); the buyer support site paddle.net named repeatedly as the request channel; "highest level of rights applies" / statutory-rights-not-affected savings lines repeated in intro, sections 1, 2, 3 and 6; policy stated to form part of the Buyer Terms, which prevail on conflict and supply governing law.
- Coverage notes: default is non-refundable with a discretionary 14-day window; country-by-country statutory withdrawal periods (EU/EEA/CH/UK 14 days, Turkey/Israel 14, South Korea/Brazil/China 7, Canada 7, Singapore 5); EU online withdrawal button described; no app-store redirection; no refund amounts or pro-rating rules beyond "full or partial"; Singapore clauses are misnumbered 2.5.x (copy error on the page).
