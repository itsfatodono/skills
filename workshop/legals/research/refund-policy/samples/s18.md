## Document
- Shape: standalone "Refund and Cancellation Policy" for all of the company's apps, served as a static HTML export (page title still carries the file name "03042025 - Final"); "Last update" date and trademark name on top, intro paragraph, then 11 numbered sections (1–11) with numbered sub-clauses (e.g. 7.4), no summary box, no table of contents, no tables.
- Register: formal contract style, "we/you", defined terms in quotes and capitalised ("App", "Subscription", "Stores", "Web", "Refund Policy", "In-App Content"); long sentences, "to the extent permitted by applicable law" hedging, internal cross-references by section number.
- Recurring elements: split by purchase channel (Web vs. Stores) throughout; arrow-chain click paths ("Settings → Tap your name → ...") for store steps; support email repeated in several sections, a second general contact email in the transfer section; references to the Terms of Use (statutory withdrawal waiver, governing law).
- Coverage notes: covers far more than refunds (billing cycles, trials, payment processors, price/tax changes, invoices); a voluntary 14-day first-purchase cooling-off refund for Web purchases only, framed against an EEA statutory-withdrawal waiver; store refunds handed to Apple/Google with step lists; in-app purchases are final with an express-consent withdrawal waiver; no refund-request form, no chargeback clause, no regional sections beyond the EEA references.
