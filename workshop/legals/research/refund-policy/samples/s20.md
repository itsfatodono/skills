## Document
- Version taken: the candidate URL `https://comfax.com/refund/` ("Refund policy", effective July 11, 2025) is still linked from the Terms of Use, but the site footer, homepage, app page and the `/refund/` page's own footer all link to `https://comfax.com/billing-refund-policy/` ("Billing & Refund Policy", effective July 17, 2026), which carries the same refund text with additions (directive citations, KYC, business-user carve-out, payment-dispute clause, chat contact). Rule 2: the newer page is the current version, so it is the one extracted.
- Combined page: H1 "Billing & Refund Policy"; section 1 "Billing Policy" (billing cycle, packages, free trials, special offers, payment methods and processors, fee changes, auto-renewal, FTC-style cancellation by channel, long-term plans) is the billing part and is not extracted; its cancellation bullets do not mention refunds. The refund part is the shared intro plus sections 2-7; `words` counts those only.
- Shape: single page; H1, "Effective Date" line, untitled intro paragraphs, then 7 numbered H2 sections; refund section split by unheaded sub-labels ("Subscriptions Purchased via our Website" / "via another platforms"); no table, no summary, no table of contents.
- Register: formal, "we/you", defined terms in quotes and parentheses ("Municorn", "Fax App", "Service"); bolded lead-in labels ("Label. text") on almost every bullet; mixes hedged discretion ("may", "we reserve the right") with plain conditions.
- Recurring elements: support email repeated in nearly every contact point; full mailing address block (email + postal address) repeated four times (EU withdrawal, US cooling-off, US death/disability, Contact Us); fraud/misuse refund-denial reservation stated twice (intro and Important Notes); platform split (own website vs Apple/Google) as the organising axis.
- Coverage notes: refunds tied to usage (no faxes sent) rather than a fixed day window; first purchase only; no partial-period proration except the US death/disability clause; no chargeback clause; regional parts for EU/UK/CH and named US states; no currency or refund-amount rules beyond fee deduction.
