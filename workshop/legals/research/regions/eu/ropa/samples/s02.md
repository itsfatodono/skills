## Document
- Shape: spreadsheet, five tabs: tutorial, processing list, one form per operation, worked
  payroll example, drop-down lists.
- Status: blank regulator model with a worked example, for small businesses.
- EU content: in the form's own fields; drop-down lists carry the GDPR vocabulary (transfer
  guarantees, countries flagged adequate or not).
- Law named: GDPR Art. 30 in the tutorial; Arts. 9 and 10 categories as fixed rows; Chapter V
  guarantees in the lists.
- Absent: legal basis (the model keeps to Art. 30's list), processor record (controller only),
  DPIA flag.
