## Document
- Shape: a Word document, not a grid: revision history and approval table, then numbered
  parts that follow the Art. 30(1) points in order for the whole university (controller and
  DPO, data subjects, personal data by function, purposes, recipients, transfers outside the
  EEA, retention, security), an appendix with the organisation chart. The landing page says
  each school and office keeps its own per-activity record; this is the summary above them.
- Status: published high-level record ("high-level ROPA").
- Activities: none as rows; data categories are grouped by function (HR, academic
  administration, research, IT services, finance, compliance, student welfare, other, AI).
- Register: formal, first person plural in places ("Maynooth University collects ...").
- Law named: "GDPR Article 30" in the title; the DPC's 2024 guidelines in the revision history.
- EU content: DPO block with name, mailbox, address and phone; "joint and separate controller"
  relationships named; special categories listed per function; a transfers part bounded by
  the EEA with the mechanisms named.
- Absent: legal bases; Art. 9 conditions; Art. 27 representative (EU controller); processor
  record; per-transfer safeguards.
