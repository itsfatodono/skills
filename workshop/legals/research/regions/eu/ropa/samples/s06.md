## Document
- Shape: 17-page guidance note from the DPC's 2022 sweep of 30 organisations' records, ending
  in four model pages (images, read by rendering): Approach A and B (well-completed grids for
  a fictional "A Company Ltd"), Approach C (insufficient detail), and Table A (each
  requirement, its type, two filled examples, an insufficient example, a recommendation).
  Extracted: the four model pages, with the guidance text as instructions.
- Status: regulator model (worked examples), aimed at companies.
- Shape of the models: contact block on top (controller, address, email, DPO), then one grid,
  one row per sub-activity (A) or per data category within a purpose (B); rows numbered 1.1,
  1.2. Approach A splits columns into green "mandatory" and blue "as applicable".
- EU content: Table A types each field Required / If relevant / If possible / Recommended,
  following Art. 30(1); legal basis written as "Article 6(1)(b) Contract"; Art. 9(2) condition
  beside it; a "Legislation" column for the statute; third country, international
  organisation and safeguards as three columns. The text asks the record to mark which fields
  Art. 30 prescribes and which are extras, and explains Art. 30(2) and the Art. 30(5)
  exemption (HR and payroll are "not occasional", so the exemption rarely holds).
- Absent: Art. 27 representative filled (field "If relevant", NA in the examples); DPIA column.
