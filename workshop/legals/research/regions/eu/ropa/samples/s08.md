## EU content

Counts toward the `eu` ids by meaning; no wording is taken from it into the block (decision 36).

- eu-gdpr-source: the register page states the Art. 31 duty of EU institutions.
- eu-required-marks: joint controller and processor fields marked "(where applicable)",
  security "where possible".
- eu-controller: one shared DPO mailbox in every record; no representative field.
- eu-legal-basis: no field; the staff record cites its regulations inside the purpose.
- eu-special-categories: special-category data flagged inside the data field of the
  recruitment record.
- eu-transfers: one field asking whether, to which third countries or international
  organisations, and with which safeguards; names adequacy decisions and SCCs used by
  processors.
- eu-processor-record: none (controller records only).
