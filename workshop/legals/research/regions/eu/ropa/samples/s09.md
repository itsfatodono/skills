## EU content

Counts toward the `eu` ids by meaning; no wording is taken from it into the block (decision 36).

- eu-gdpr-source: register page and form banner cite Art. 31.
- eu-field-citations: the article after every field heading.
- eu-controller: DPO mailbox in the controller field; no representative.
- eu-legal-basis: no field of its own; the purpose instructions ask for it and the answer
  cites the Staff Regulations.
- eu-special-categories: tick boxes for general, sensitive and criminal-conviction data.
- eu-transfers: yes/no boxes, then the transfer grounds as a tick-box checklist (adequacy,
  safeguards, derogations); international organisations named.
- eu-processor-agreement: processors named with the agreement that covers them (an SLA).
- eu-processor-record: none (controller records only).
