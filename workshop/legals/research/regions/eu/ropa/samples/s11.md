## EU content

Counts toward the `eu` ids by meaning; no wording is taken from it into the block (decision 36).

- eu-gdpr-source: the disclaimer names the regulation and Art. 31(5).
- eu-field-citations: the data field heading cites the special-categories article.
- eu-required-marks: asterisks mark mandatory fields, explained in an endnote on every record.
- eu-controller: DPO named with mailbox, the same in every record; no representative.
- eu-legal-basis: a "purpose and legal basis" field: legal instruments listed, then the basis
  point quoted in full.
- eu-special-categories: named in the data field heading; no condition column.
- eu-transfers: one field; usually a one-sentence no-transfer statement.
- eu-processor-record: none (controller records only).
