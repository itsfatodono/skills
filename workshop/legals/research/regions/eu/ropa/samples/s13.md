## EU content

Counts toward the `eu` ids by meaning; no wording is taken from it into the block (decision 36).

- eu-gdpr-source: register section and record part heading cite Art. 31.
- eu-field-citations: article numbers printed in the field labels.
- eu-controller: DPO named with address and shared mailbox; no representative.
- eu-legal-basis: its own field in the consolidated register: the basis points, then the
  Union law behind them.
- eu-special-categories: checklist of the special categories with the allowing grounds as a
  second checklist; criminal-offence data in a field of its own.
- eu-transfers: N/A/Yes for third countries and international organisations, then a
  checklist of legal grounds; the adequacy decision named in a footnote.
- eu-processor-agreement: internal processors with the processing agreement noted; outside
  processors bound by contracts.
- eu-processor-record: none (controller records only).
