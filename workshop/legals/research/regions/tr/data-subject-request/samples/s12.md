## Document
- Shape: one HTML page with two tabs (user, business). Each tab holds a printable petition text, written as a first-person letter to the controller, followed by an online web form with its own fields. A shared "Önemli Bilgiler" box closes the page. No PDF.
- Register: the petition is first-person from the applicant ("istiyorum", "talep ederim") and addressed to the controller by name. The web form uses short field labels, and the closing box uses bullets.
- Recurring elements: "Son Güncelleme: 07 Temmuz 2025" under each petition text. Each right is tagged with its article reference (Mad 11/1 (a)…(g)). The business petition copies the user petition word for word, including "LOOKBUBBLE Uygulaması kullanıcısı olarak", and only the identity block differs. The rights list leaves out Art. 11/1 (d) transfers abroad, (e) correction, (f) erasure/destruction, the notice to third parties, and (ğ) damages. The page gives no submission channels and does not cite the Tebliğ.
