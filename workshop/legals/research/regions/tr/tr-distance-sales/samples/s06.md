- Striking:

  - Madde 2 defines the seller as providing a "hizmet" and the buyer as buying a "hizmet",
    while the rest of the text speaks of "Ürün" (template wording for services left in).
  - Madde 3 states the product attributes are "as stated above", but they come after it, in
    Madde 4.
  - 5.4 bars buyers under 18 and puts the risk of a false age on the buyer.
  - 5.11 says the buyer consents to the GSM number being stored "for automatic renewal", a
    subscription clause with no subscription elsewhere in the text.
  - 5.13 gives the buyer 7 days to report defects or missing items, after which delivery counts
    as complete and defect-free, and a 30-business-day cure period before rescission.
  - 6.1 is worded so that the SATICI acknowledges the buyer's withdrawal right; 6.2 requires the
    invoice, box and packaging and return within 10 days; 6.6 puts withdrawal costs on the seller.
  - 6.7 copies the full statutory exception list (a–ğ, h) and adds a catch-all "j)" for goods
    outside distance-sale scope; for a coffee shop, the perishables item is the relevant one.
  - Madde 7 force majeure: buyer may cancel or wait, refund within 10 days.
  - Madde 8 is a short confidentiality clause (no sharing with third parties, card data not
    stored, marketing only with consent) instead of a KVKK reference.
  - Madde 9 names the "Gümrük ve Ticaret Bakanlığı" (the ministry's pre-2018 name), consumer
    arbitration committees at the place of purchase, but consumer courts and civil courts at the
    SELLER's domicile.
  - Madde 10: for bank-transfer payments the contract takes effect only when the money arrives,
    and never takes effect if it does not.
