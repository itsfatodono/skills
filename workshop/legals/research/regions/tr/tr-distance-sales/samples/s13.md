## Document
- Shape: article-numbered "MADDE 1 …" to "MADDE 12" (MADDE 11 is missing; numbering jumps from 10 to 12). Mid length (~2,070 words). MADDE 6 and 7 use decimal sub-clauses (6.1–6.20, 7.1–7.7); MADDE 8 uses a lettered list. Page title is "Satış Sözleşmesi" (not "Mesafeli Satış Sözleşmesi"). Served as a static page on the shop's footer "Sözleşmeler" menu, next to the usage and membership terms.
- Register: formal contract Turkish; parties as "SATICI"/"ALICI" in capitals in MADDE 6, but "Satıcı"/"Alıcı" in title case in MADDE 4, 5, 7, 9, which suggests clauses joined from different sources. Long multi-clause sentences; third person throughout.
- Recurring elements: cross-references to "1. Madde" for the seller's address and channels; named carrier (UPS Kargo) repeated in delivery and return clauses; "İnternet Sitesi ve/veya Mobil Uygulama" as the sales channel.
- Combined with: Ön Bilgilendirme Formu, by reference and partly in wording. MADDE 3 (Konu) calls the document "İşbu Ön Bilgilendirme Formu" and states the information purpose of a pre-contract form, and MADDE 12 has the buyer accept "Ön Bilgilendirme Formu ve Mesafeli Satış Sözleşmesi" together; MADDE 4.1 points to a separate Ön Bilgilendirme Formu for item, price and payment details. There is no separate Ön Bilgilendirme Formu part on the page, so the whole text was extracted as the contract. No KVKK notice is merged; MADDE 6.6 has only one sentence on disclosure of personal data to authorities.
- Placeholders: MADDE 2 Alıcı Bilgileri has blank fields (Adı Soyadı, Adresi, Telefon, Email); MADDE 4 has no order table and defers item and price to the Ön Bilgilendirme Formu.
- Template blocks: none found. Searches (quoted, 2026-10-26): 6.19 obvious-pricing-error sentence, 0 hits; MADDE 5 "İlan edilen fiyatlar ve vaatler güncelleme yapılana…", 0; 6.17 bulk-purchase cap sentence, 0; 6.16 "2 ile 3 haftayı bulabileceğini", 0; 7.2 "Satıcı Alıcı'ya bu yönde bir teklifte bulunmadığı takdirde…", 0; MADDE 9 complaint-handling sentence, 0; 6.2 liability-disclaimer list, 0; 6.13 card-misuse clause, 0. 6.9 (recipient refusing delivery) appears in near-identical form on 2 other shops (direnc.net, one shop's terms on lawinsider), so it is common boilerplate but below the three-shop threshold, and it was kept. 7.1 restates the regulation and its wording also appears in IdeaSoft and ikas sample contracts; as a statutory restatement it was not used for the template check.
- Striking: MADDE 10 (Yetkili Mahkeme) names the consumer arbitration committee (tüketici sorunları hakem heyeti) or consumer court of the place of purchase or residence within yearly monetary limits, but it is worded as "Satıcı şikâyet ve itirazları konusunda başvurularını … yapabilir" (seller as subject, apparently a slip for the buyer), and it cites the old "Gümrük ve Sanayi Bakanlığı". MADDE 12 is an electronic-acceptance clause. Several seller-protective clauses go beyond the law: bulk orders capped at 3 items, cancellation for obvious pricing errors, ID requests after 3 wrong card PINs, buyer must return goods within 3 days if the bank does not pay because of card misuse. A sector-specific note says there are no instalments on cosmetics (6.20). Clauses better suited to website terms (site changes, third-party links, IP ownership: 6.3–6.5) are mixed into the sales contract.
