- Placeholders: unfilled field labels with no values: buyer "AD- SOYAD:"/"ADRES:" in Madde 1,
  the Madde 5, 6 and 8 field blocks, the whole Madde 7.3 order table, and "ALICI:" / "TARİH:"
  in the signature block. Seller fields are filled.
- Striking:
  - Outdated authority and figures: defines BAKAN/BAKANLIK as the "Gümrük ve Ticaret"
    minister/ministry, and Madde 13 gives the 2014 arbitration-committee thresholds (2,000 /
    3,000 / 4,000–6,000 TL "28/05/2014 tarihinden itibaren").
  - Madde 13 ends with "İşbu Sözleşme ticari amaçlarla yapılmaktadır", which contradicts the
    consumer definition of ALICI.
  - Internal contradictions on cost and timing: 7.4 says the buyer pays shipping, 10.1 says
    withdrawal costs fall on the seller; 9.5 and 9.8 use 14-day refunds, 10.2(d) gives a
    10-day refund and a 20-day window for the seller to collect the goods (the pre-2014 rule).
  - Defines SATICI as a "şirket" though the seller is an individual; says the contract was
    "imzalanmıştır" while Madde 14 makes payment the act of acceptance.
  - Madde 3 second paragraph and 7.2 repeat the same price-validity sentences word for word.
  - 9.9 grants the seller a right to contact the buyer for marketing by mail, email, SMS and
    phone, accepted by accepting the contract (no separate commercial-message consent).
  - 9.13–9.16 are site-use and membership terms (lawful use, spam/viruses, third-party links,
    indemnity for "üyelik sözleşmesi" breaches) carried inside a sales contract.
  - Withdrawal exceptions list underwear, swimwear, make-up and data-storage devices, none of
    them sold by a bag shop; a second paragraph adds unopened-packaging conditions for cosmetics,
    books, software, discs and toner.
  - 10.2(f) cancels a campaign discount if a withdrawal takes the order below the campaign limit.
