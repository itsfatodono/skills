## Document
- Shape: article-numbered in capitals, "MADDE 1 - TARAFLAR" to "MADDE 7 - YETKİLİ MAHKEME", seven articles, about 1,980 words. Only Madde 1 has sub-numbers (1.1 SATICI, 1.2 ALICI). Madde 4 "GENEL HÜKÜMLER" is about 60% of the text: 19 unnumbered paragraphs on unrelated topics (liability, IP, delivery, payment fraud, refunds, order cancellation, campaigns, resale ban). Madde 5 has a lettered a)–c) list of what a return needs. Madde 6 is a lettered a)–h) list. The seller block is a label : value table. There is no date, version or "last updated" line. The page is served server-side on the company's own domain. The same HTML also carries the membership agreement, the KVKK notices and the cookie texts in hidden modals and the footer. Those are not part of this contract and were not extracted.
- Register: formal, dense legal Turkish of the long-sentence "işbu … kabul ve taahhüt eder" kind. It uses third person throughout, with the capitalised party labels SATICI and ALICI. It drifts into "Şirket", "Saat ve Saat", "üye", "Müşteri" and "Tüketici" in places. There are small typos and doubled spaces ("belirtiği", "Internet Sitesinde"). Several clauses are written for a website-terms context (the legal-notice page, linked sites, materials/IP) rather than a sale.
- Recurring elements: "İnternet Sitesi ve/veya Mobil Uygulama" as the sales channel; references to the 6502 Law and the Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği of 27.11.2014 (Madde 2 and 6); counted periods written as digits plus words ("14 (ondört)", "3 (üç)"); "hakkını saklı tutar" for seller-side cancellation rights; cross-references to other articles ("Madde 3'te", "6. madde", "birinci maddede").
- Combined with: none on the contract itself. No Ön Bilgilendirme Formu on the page; Madde 4 only has the buyer declare that they read the pre-contract information. The KVKK, membership and cookie texts sit elsewhere in the page markup, not inside the contract.
- Placeholders: "1.2 ALICI" is a heading with no fields under it. Madde 3 says the product type, quantity, brand/model, colour, price and delivery details are "aşağıda belirtildiği gibidir", but nothing follows. Both are filled per order.
- Template blocks: none confirmed. Grep of the full text of the Forbes TR template, the IdeaSoft default and the Law Insider TR copy (6RY9Tlbkh6r), plus two more Law Insider TR copies (9HSOCBT1vEp, cZE92if0HTA) that searches surfaced, for 28 phrases covering every article: 26 had 0 hits. Two had partial overlaps, both on law-derived wording. "ön bilgileri okuyup bilgi" appears once in IdeaSoft (it restates the pre-information duty). "eşit kalite ve fiyatta farklı bir ürün" appears once each in Forbes and Law Insider, but there it carries "bilgilendirmek ve açıkça onayını almak suretiyle", which this text drops. Four quoted web searches returned no page with the phrase: Madde 4 "ALICI'nın süper taksit ile yapmış olduğu alışverişlerde", Madde 4 "…her girişte yasal uyarı sayfasını ziyaret etmelerini tavsiye etmektedir", Madde 4 "toptan nitelikteki alımlarda satın alınanların 3 (üç) adet ürünü aşması halinde", and Madde 5 "iade faturasıdır" + "5 (beş) gün içerisinde". Caveat: several Madde 4 paragraphs (force majeure "hava muhalefeti", the bank-refund 2–3 weeks, "3 hatalı şifre", obvious price error) read like common Turkish e-commerce boilerplate. Search tools handle Turkish exact phrases poorly, so "no hits" is weak evidence that they are original.
- Striking:
  - Dispute clause (Madde 7): it names the "Tüketici Sorunları İl ve İlçe Hakem Heyeti" or the "Tüketici Mahkemesi" at the place of purchase or residence, within limits set each December by the "Gümrük ve Ticaret Bakanlığı". Both the ministry and the committee names are pre-2018 wording. The heading says "Mahkeme", but the text is addressed to "Tüketici".
  - No formation, acceptance, e-signature or effective-date clause. The only acceptance is the buyer's declaration of having read the pre-information and given "elektronik ortamda gerekli teyidi" (Madde 4).
  - Seller-friendly unilateral rights in Madde 4: a broad exclusion of all direct and indirect liability; cancellation of orders above 3 units as "toptan" or beyond need; cancellation at an obvious price error; cancellation of stacked campaigns; cancellation after 3 wrong card PIN entries unless ID is provided; substitution of an equal product without any consent step; termination plus blocking of the membership, IP and TC/VKN for resale.
  - It names the carrier (DHL eCommerce) "as of the date this Contract is approved", and puts all shipping fees on the seller.
  - The withdrawal procedure adds conditions: notice by fax, phone or e-mail; the product must be fit for resale; return within 10 days with box and accessories; the original invoice must be signed "iade faturasıdır" and sent within 5 days, otherwise the item is sent back cash-on-delivery. Return shipping is free only through the contracted carrier.
  - Madde 6 copies all of the 2014 Regulation's Art. 15 exceptions (a)–(h) in its own wording, "…ilişkin ürünler", though most cannot apply to watches.
