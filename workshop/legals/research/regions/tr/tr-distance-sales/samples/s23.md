## Document
- Version taken: live page, Turkish (no language switcher), served as an ikas storefront page (`/pages/…`). The text names the site as `kahvediyari.com.tr`, while it is served on `kahvediyari.com`.

- Register: plain-ish legal Turkish, third person, party labels in title case with apostrophes ("Alıcı'nın", "Satıcı'nın"), not capitals. Short declarative sentences ("… taahhüt eder", "… kabul eder"). Lighter than the usual "kabul, beyan ve taahhüt eder" style.

- Template blocks: none verbatim. Kept sections are close paraphrases, though. Sections 4–8 and 11 follow the widely copied Forbes Türkiye template (forbes.com.tr/mesafeli-satis-sozlesmesi; the same text is on Law Insider 6RY9Tlbkh6r) clause by clause in condensed wording, with the same points in the same order:
  - campaign features viewable during the campaign; price validity
  - 3-day notice when delivery is impossible, then refund
  - force majeure options
  - inspect on delivery, refuse a torn package

  - third-party links
  - corporate return invoice
  - 10-day refund and 20-day pick-up
  - campaign limit cancels the discount
  - credit-card default with interest, bank costs and attorney fees
  - acceptance on payment, plus the seller's "yazılımsal düzenlemeleri" duty

  Read section 5 onward as a condensed rewrite of that template, not as original drafting. Sections 1–3 (opening paragraph, Tanımlar list incl. "Bakanlık: Ticaret Bakanlığı"), 5.5 (card security disclaimer, marketing contact) and 10 (İzmir courts) do not come from it.

- Striking:
  - The contract accepts marketing contact by email, SMS and phone in a general clause (5.5), with no separate consent.
  - Card-security liability is disclaimed onto the banks.
  - Withdrawal notice may go by phone, yet it must be "yazılı olarak".
  - The service-contract withdrawal start is given as the signing date, though the shop sells goods only.
  - Payment and delivery terms appear twice (5.3/5.4 and 9).
  - The jurisdiction clause names only "İzmir Mahkemeleri ve İcra Daireleri", with no Tüketici Hakem Heyeti or Tüketici Mahkemesi.
  - The contract counts as accepted once the order is placed and paid.
  - There is no coffee- or food-specific clause beyond the generic perishables exception.
