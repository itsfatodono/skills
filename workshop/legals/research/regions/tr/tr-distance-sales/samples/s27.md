## Document
- Version taken: live page, Turkish (no language switcher), server-rendered; fetched directly
  with curl, no Wayback needed. Word count is the contract body only, from "1.TARAFLAR" to the
  signature lines (page incl. menus and footer: about 2,800).
- Platform: Qukasoft ("Qukasoft E-Ticaret Altyapısı" footer link, assets on `cdn.qukasoft.com`).
  Not ikas, as an earlier run recorded: the "ikas" string hits in the page are inside Turkish
  words ("politikası"). Footer copyright "© Toysishop 2021".
- Template text: the widely copied forbes.com.tr / Law Insider distance sales contract, with
  the shop's details filled in at the top and small local additions (toy examples in Madde 11,
  a shipping-cost line 7.4). Extracted in full like any other text.
- Shape: page title, then "1.TARAFLAR" to "14. YÜRÜRLÜK" (number + dot, capitalised heading, no
  "MADDE"); dotted sub-clauses in Madde 7 (first one numbered "1." instead of "7.1"), 9 (9.1–9.16)
  and 10 (10.1–10.2, with lettered a)–f) under 10.2); lettered a)–c) list in Madde 13; label
  field blocks in Madde 4, 5, 6 and 8 and a field-label product table in 7.3; closing
  SATICI / ALICI / TARİH lines. About 2,220 words. Madde 9 GENEL HÜKÜMLER is roughly 40% of the
  text. No table of contents, no withdrawal form annex.
- Register: formal legal Turkish, third person, SATICI / ALICI / SİTE / SÖZLEŞME in capitals,
  long sentences closing on "kabul, beyan ve taahhüt eder"; many buyer-duty and
  seller-protective clauses; also mixes "üye" and "tüketici" for the buyer.
- Recurring elements: "kabul, beyan ve taahhüt eder" formula; day counts sometimes written as
  digit + word ("14 (on dört) gün"); repeated "SATICI'ya ait internet sitesi"; statute citations
  (6502, Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği with its Official Gazette date, Art. 68).
- Combined with: none. No Ön Bilgilendirme Formu on the page (9.1 refers to the pre-contract
  information shown on the site); KVKK, terms, delivery/returns and cookies are separate footer
  pages.
- Placeholders: the generator/template fields are left empty: seller phone and fax blank,
  buyer, orderer and invoice blocks are bare labels, the 7.3 price table lists only column
  labels, and the signature lines (SATICI / ALICI / TARİH) are empty. Typos carried in the filled
  parts ("Mo:9/1" in Madde 1, "toysishop .com", "Dogal").
- Striking:
  - Outdated references: "Gümrük ve Ticaret Bakanlığı" and the 2014 arbitration-committee limits
    (2,000 / 3,000 TL "valid from 28/05/2014") written out as current.
  - Internal contradictions: Madde 3 and 7.2 repeat the same price-validity paragraph; 7.4 puts
    shipping on the buyer while the site banner promises free shipping over a threshold; 10.1
    says withdrawal costs fall on the seller; 10.2(d) gives a 10-day refund and 20-day pickup
    window (the pre-2014 timing) while 9.5 and 9.8 use 14 days.
  - Madde 13 ends with "İşbu Sözleşme ticari amaçlarla yapılmaktadır.", at odds with a consumer
    contract.
  - 9.9 folds marketing consent (SMS, email, calls) into contract acceptance.
  - 9.12–9.16 are site-use and membership clauses (no unlawful use, spam/virus, third-party
    links, indemnity, "üyelik sözleşmesi"), carried over from a user agreement.
  - Madde 11 lists exceptions beyond the regulation (underwear, swimwear, make-up, single-use
    items) and adds a toy-specific one: teethers and plush toys that touch a baby's skin.
  - Madde 14 deems acceptance on payment and puts a duty on the seller to build the click-accept
    step into the site software.
