## Document
- Version taken: live page, Turkish (no language switcher); fetched directly with curl (HTTP 200,
  UTF-8), no Wayback needed. Word count is the contract body only, from the "MESAFELİ SATIŞ
  SÖZLEŞMESİ" title to the closing "TARİH:" line (page incl. menu/footer: about 2,220).
- Platform: WordPress 7.0.4 + WooCommerce 7.1.2 (generator meta tags), theme "theretailer";
  footer "Created with Cetework Corp." The three "ikas" string hits are inside Turkish words
  (e.g. "Politikası"), not the ikas platform.
- Template text: the widely copied forbes.com.tr / Law Insider TR distance sales contract, pasted
  essentially unchanged (same 14 articles, headings, Genel Hükümler 9.1–9.16, cayma list a)–f),
  Temerrüt clause and 2014 monetary limits). Extracted in full like any other text.
- Shape: title, then "1.TARAFLAR" to "14. YÜRÜRLÜK" (number + dot, uppercase headings, some
  without a space after the dot); dotted sub-clauses in Madde 7 (first one numbered "1." instead
  of "7.1."), 9 (9.1–9.16) and 10 (10.1–10.2, with lettered a)–f)); lettered a)–c) list in
  Madde 13; empty label lists for seller, buyer, orderer and invoice details; an empty order
  table (product, quantity, unit price, subtotal incl. VAT, shipping, total, payment plan,
  addresses, order and delivery dates); closing SATICI / ALICI / TARİH signature lines. About
  2,140 words. No table of contents, no withdrawal form annex.
- Register: formal legal Turkish, third person, parties in capitals (ALICI, SATICI, SİPARİŞ
  VEREN, also "üye" in places); long sentences closing with "kabul, beyan ve taahhüt eder";
  many seller-protective clauses on card misuse, site use and member liability.
- Recurring elements: "kabul, beyan ve taahhüt eder" formula; "SATICI'ya ait internet sitesi"
  instead of a site name; capitalised defined terms from Madde 2; day counts in digits, some
  with words ("14 (on dört) gün").
- Combined with: none. No Ön Bilgilendirme Formu section and no KVKK text on the page (9.1
  refers to pre-contract information confirmed separately; KVKK, refund and privacy policies are
  separate footer pages).
- Placeholders: all left unfilled. Seller "AD- SOYAD: / ADRES:" in Madde 1 and the Madde 4
  seller fields (Ünvanı, Adres, Telefon, Faks, Eposta) are blank, so the shop's own identity
  appears nowhere in the contract; buyer, orderer and invoice fields and the order table are
  empty labels; the signature lines are blank.
- Striking:
  - Outdated content kept from the template: "Gümrük ve Ticaret Bakanlığı" in the definitions,
    and 2014 arbitration-committee limits (2,000 / 3,000 TL) "valid from 28/05/2014".
  - Madde 13 ends with "İşbu Sözleşme ticari amaçlarla yapılmaktadır." in a consumer contract.
  - 10.1 says withdrawal costs are the seller's; 10.2(d) gives a 10-day refund and 20-day
    take-back period (wording of the pre-2014 regulation, not the current 14 days).
  - Madde 11 lists underwear, swimwear, make-up, books and software alongside the statutory
    exceptions, nothing specific to coffee; perishables are covered only by the generic
    statutory item.
  - 7.4 makes the buyer pay shipping, while the site banner offers free shipping over 700 TL.
  - Madde 3's second paragraph repeats 7.2 word for word; 9.14–9.16 address a "member" and an
    "üyelik sözleşmesi", carried over from membership terms.
  - Madde 14: acceptance occurs when the buyer pays; the seller undertakes to build the
    software step for reading and accepting the contract.
