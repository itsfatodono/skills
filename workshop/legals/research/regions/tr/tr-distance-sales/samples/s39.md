## Document
- Version taken: live page, Turkish (no language switcher), server-rendered; fetched directly
  with curl (HTTP 200), no Wayback needed. Word count is the contract body only, from "MADDE 1-
  TARAFLAR" to the closing "Tarih" line (page with menus and footer: about 910).
- Platform: custom PHP site (own `/js/fonksiyonlar.js`, `/resimler/` uploads); no IdeaSoft,
  Ticimax, T-Soft, ikas, Shopify or WooCommerce markers. The seller is a second-hand toy
  marketplace-style shop, but the text treats it as the only seller.
- Template text: an older shop template that predates Law 6502's regulation (it cites the
  repealed "Mesafeli Sözleşmeleri Uygulama Esas ve Usulleri Hakkında Yönetmelik" and the
  "Sanayi ve Ticaret Bakanlığı"); the earlier run found it on 2–10 unrelated shops. Not the
  s15 text. Extracted in full.
- Shape: title, then "MADDE 1- TARAFLAR" to "MADDE 8- YETKİLİ MAHKEME" (MADDE n- HEADING in
  capitals); dotted sub-clauses in Madde 1 (1.1–1.2), 4 (4.1–4.9) and 7 (7.1–7.2); two "GENEL
  HÜKÜMLER" articles (4 and 7); label/value blocks with `{ALICIADSOYAD}`-style tokens for the
  buyer and delivery data; closing acceptance line and SATICI / ALICI / Tarih block. About 650
  words. No table of contents, no withdrawal-form annex, no date.
- Register: formal legal Turkish, third person, SATICI / ALICI in capitals with the apostrophe
  dropped before suffixes ("ALICInın", "SATICIya"); medium sentences, "beyan eder" and
  "yükümlüdür" rather than the "kabul, beyan ve taahhüt eder" formula.
- Recurring elements: platform tokens in curly braces filled per order; day counts as digits
  (3, 10, 14, 30); "Law 6502" named with the old regulation title.
- Combined with: none. Separate footer pages for cancellation and returns, privacy, membership
  and KVKK.
- Placeholders: buyer name, address, phone, e-mail, delivery and invoice address and date are
  per-order tokens (`{ALICIADSOYAD}`, `{ALICIADRES}`, `{ALICITELEFON}`, `{ALICIMAIL}`,
  `{TARIH}`), shown literally on the public page. Seller block gives only the brand and an
  e-mail: no legal entity, address, MERSİS, tax number or KEP.
- Striking:
  - 4.1 has the buyer accept that delivery "may take up to 3 months depending on workload",
    while 4.2 restates the 30-day statutory cap.
  - 4.5 and 4.9 make delivery and validity depend on the buyer sending a signed copy by fax or
    post, which contradicts the closing line that ordering is acceptance.
  - Refund timing mixes the old 10-day rule (force-majeure cancellation, 4.7) with 14 days after
    the return documents arrive (Madde 5).
  - Madde 5 takes withdrawal notice by fax, e-mail or phone and needs the carrier's delivery
    receipt and the original invoice; without the invoice, VAT is not refunded. Return
    shipping is on the seller.
  - Madde 6 gives a short own-words exception list (non-returnable by nature, single-use,
    copyable software, consumables, perishables, services), not the statutory list.
  - 4.6 puts return shipping on the buyer after card misuse, where the common template puts it
    on the seller.
  - 7.1 bars under-18s from buying; 7.2 disclaims liability for typesetting and system price
    errors.
