## Document
- Shape: one page in the site's policy hub (beside the customer notice and a separate foreign-transfer consent text); a title block, one long run-on consent request built of clauses and bullet lists, the controller's contact block, then a first-person consent statement. No numbered headings. The page's footer modals (a membership agreement, a campaign notice, newsletter checkboxes) are other documents and are not extracted.
- Register: formal legal Turkish; "we" request your consent ("açık rızanızı talep ediyoruz"), the closing switches to the data subject's first person ("açık rıza veriyorum"). Long sentences, "Müşteri" capitalised as a defined term.
- Consent items: one bundled consent, not split by purpose. It covers (a) processing of a listed set of customer data for a long purpose list, and (b) transfer to public authorities and to shareholders, business partners, suppliers and third parties "yurtiçinde veya yurt dışında", i.e. transfer abroad is inside the same single consent. No separate item for marketing messages, profiling or transfer.
- Acceptance: the text itself ends with a written first-person declaration that the person has read the customer notice and consents freely ("hiçbir etki altında kalmadan"); the page shows no checkbox, signature or button for this text.
- Recurring elements: cites Law 6698 with its Official Gazette date and number; links consent to the separate Müşteri Aydınlatma Metni; no date or version.
