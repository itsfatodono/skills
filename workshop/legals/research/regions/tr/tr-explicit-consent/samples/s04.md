## Document
- Shape: one-page signable PDF form. Title, one acknowledgment paragraph, three lettered consent
  items (A–C) each followed by an "ONAY VERİYORUM / ONAY VERMİYORUM" choice, then a signature
  block. Taken from a hub page that links six PDF consent forms (one general, five per policy line:
  DASK, health, traffic, motor own-damage, personal accident); the general form is the sample.
- Register: formal legal Turkish, first person singular from the data subject ("tarafıma",
  "verilerimin"), company addressed as "Şirketiniz"; long single sentences, each item a
  dative-infinitive clause ending in ";".
- Recurring elements: every item repeats the same limiting phrase (within the notice and limited to
  its purposes) and the same data scope (personal and special-category data); no purposes, data
  categories, recipients or countries are listed in the text itself, all are delegated to the
  separate Aydınlatma Metni; no withdrawal wording, no controller contact, no date or version.
- Acceptance: per-item binary choice printed as two labels side by side (approve / do not approve),
  meant to be marked on paper, then name, national ID number, date and wet signature for the whole
  form. A footer marks a printed unsigned copy as an uncontrolled copy.
