## Document
- Shape: one short page, title then unheaded paragraphs, one bulleted data list, closing acceptance and refusal lines. A single consent item: transfer abroad of live-support chat data to the chat vendor (KVKK Art. 9). Linked as "Açık Rıza Metni" in the site footer; the page title names it a live-support transfer-abroad consent.
- Register: formal Turkish, company as "Biz"/"bizler", user addressed as "siz"; long sentences; reassuring tone on freedom of choice.
- Recurring elements: points back to the separate Aydınlatma Metni (twice); privacy email for complaints; call-center number as the alternative channel; no date, no version, no signature block.
- Consent items:
  - Item 1. Purpose: answering questions sent over the website's live-support channel in real time. Data: identity (name), customer transaction (purchase history, appointments), contact (phone, email), other (subject, message content). Recipient/transfer: a US-based chat software vendor, named, as a transfer abroad because no adequate countries have been declared. Acceptance: a checkbox placed before the live-support form's "Gönder" button; ticking it is defined as consent; refusing means using the call center instead.
