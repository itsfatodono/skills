## Document
- Shape: a paper patient form published as an HTML page. Title, two acknowledgment and blanket-consent
  paragraphs, an untitled list of data categories (the category labels of a source table are lost,
  only their definitions remain), a "known to me" statement on mandatory transfers, seven numbered
  consent items each followed by "Onay veriyorum. / Onay vermiyorum.", a separate titled consent for
  disclosure to family members and named third parties with blank fields, a warranty about third
  parties' data, and a signature block. The site's KVKK Aydınlatma Metni and legal warning appear
  only as site-wide pop-ups on the same page and were not extracted.
- Register: formal legal Turkish, first person singular of the patient ("verilerimin", "şahsıma");
  the clinic is addressed as "muayenehaneniz" / "tedarikçileriniz" in some items and named in the
  third person in others; very long sentences, items written as dative-infinitive clauses ending
  in "aktarılmasına" or "işlenmesine"; typos and unfinished clauses (the purposes sentence before
  item 1 has no verb).
- Recurring elements: names the controller (a limited company) and "data processors" defined as the
  clinic's own SGK-registered staff; cites KVKK 6698 and the health-data regulation; mixes consent
  with "I know / I accept, declare and undertake" wording; a blanket advance consent to all
  processing comes before the itemised choices; no withdrawal wording, no contact, no date or
  version; patient and relatives (and the patient's dependants) covered by one form.
- Acceptance: per-item binary choice printed as two labels side by side, to be marked on paper;
  the family-disclosure consent has its own choice plus blank rows for the authorised persons
  (name, relationship, national ID, contact), filled in by hand; then one name, relationship
  (for a representative), date and signature for the whole form, and an optional interpreter or
  witness name line.
