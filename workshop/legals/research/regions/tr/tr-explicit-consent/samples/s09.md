## Document
- Shape: a standalone page. A boxed notice at the top says which consents are optional and which are required. Then a one-line declaration, four numbered consent items (each one checkbox clause, with an "optional" or "required" label in its heading), a withdrawal section, and a closing note on how consent is recorded.
- Register: the consent clauses are first person ("açık rıza veriyorum"). The notice box and the explanatory notes are second person ("vermemeniz", "geri almanız"). Sentences are short and plain, and legal references are kept few (KVKK, m.9, İYS).
- Recurring elements: version number and effective date under the title; repeated links to the Aydınlatma Metni (by article number) and to the Çerez Politikası; a ☐ before each consent clause; an "isteğe bağlı" or "gereklidir" label in each item heading; a contact email in the withdrawal section and in the footer.
- Acceptance: each item is ticked separately, at signup in the mobile app and on the website forms. Items 2 and 3 are required in the app and are taken together at signup. On the website, analytics runs only after the cookie banner is accepted. Every consent given or refused is logged with date, text version and source.
