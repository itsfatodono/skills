## Document
- Shape: a one-page PDF on the bank's letterhead (the "hadi" app brand in the header band; footer with trade name, website, KEP address, trade registry and MERSIS numbers, phone, address and a version code); a centred title, one lead-in sentence, two bullets, and a closing verb. No checkbox, signature, name or date fields. Turkish only.
- Register: first-person declaration by the data subject ("kişisel verilerimin ... onay veriyorum"); the whole text is one sentence split by bullets, formal legal Turkish.
- Recurring elements: version code in the footer instead of a date; controller identification block in the footer; the lead-in names a specific information notice by title (the payment-services aydınlatma metni) and defines the data scope by reference to it. No rights, withdrawal, contact for consent or retention.
- Consent structure: two consent items as bullets (processing for marketing purposes; sharing for the same purposes), joined under a single closing "onay veriyorum", so the text gives no separate choice per bullet.
- Consent item 1: purpose: identifying suitable campaigns, discounts, promotions and offers; invitations and information about the bank's events; prize draws and competitions; sending content matched to preferences; customer modelling, reporting, product work and prospect identification; promotion and marketing of products the bank offers with business partners and group companies. Data: the personal data within the scope of the referenced information notice; no categories named. Recipient: the bank itself. Acceptance: the declaration wording "onay veriyorum"; the PDF shows no mechanism, so it is presumably taken in the app or channel that links it.
- Consent item 2: purpose: the purposes of item 1. Data: the same. Recipients/transfer: the bank's group companies and the business partners and suppliers it works with directly or indirectly; domestic or foreign is not stated, and transfer abroad is not mentioned. Acceptance: the same single "onay veriyorum" as item 1.
