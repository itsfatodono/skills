## Document
- Shape: one long page inside a "Kişisel Verilerin Korunması" hub that lists a dozen product-specific notices (account opening, credit, card, guarantor, insurance, investment, cookies) plus the application form; this is the general customer notice. Numbered headings 1–8, with three unnumbered blocks slotted in (special categories and children under 2; retention and security between 6 and 7). The purposes/legal-bases table (3) and the recipients table (4) are published as JPG images, not text (files dated 30.08.2024).
- Register: formal bank Turkish with "biz/siz", plus one plain-language mission line in the intro; long lists of statutory bodies and laws by name (5411 Bankacılık Kanunu, 5941 Çek Kanunu, MASAK, FATCA/CRS).
- Recurring elements: version date in the changes section ("03.09.2024 tarihi itibariyle güncellenmiştir"); cross-references by section number ("3 numaralı başlıkta"); link to the cookie notice and to the İlgili Kişi Başvuru Formu; the 30-day reply rule and the Board's fee tariff.
- Transfer abroad is not a separate section: foreign recipients (foreign banks, SWIFT/Mastercard/VISA, foreign group shareholders, foreign tax authorities via FATCA/CRS) appear as rows of the recipients table, each with a domestic Art. 5/6-style basis or Bankacılık Kanunu Art. 73; none of the Art. 9 tools (adequacy, standard contract, BCR, incidental transfer) is named.
- Legal bases are written as the Art. 5(2) wording paraphrased per row; explicit consent appears only for marketing/profiling, product-design surveys and pre-approved credit limits.
