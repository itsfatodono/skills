## Document
- Shape: one long web page; title, controller name, "Son güncelleme" date and a print link on top, then a linked table of contents; 16 numbered sections followed by six annexes (Ek 1–6) holding short-form notices, separate consent texts and request guidance. The main sections are prose with four tables (data categories, purposes/legal bases, domestic recipients, retention).
- Register: formal but direct legal Turkish, "biz/siz" voice, with the controller also named in caps (SOLVİERA); dense, hedged sentences that keep stating limits ("does not mean", "is not a substitute for"). Cites KVKK articles by number (5/2(c), 6, 8, 9, 11) and the Communiqué on application procedure, plus VUK, TTK and Law 6563.
- Recurring elements: last-updated date at top; defined terms (Kanun, Kurul, Kurum) in section 1; repeated controller vs processor (veri sorumlusu / veri işleyen) split for customer-business content; repeated statements that accepting the notice or the user agreement is not consent; cross-references between sections (9, 15) and to separate cookie notice and processor agreement; exact consent wording quoted in the annexes; contact emails repeated in sections 1, 15 and annexes.
