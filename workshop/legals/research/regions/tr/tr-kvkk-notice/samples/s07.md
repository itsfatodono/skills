## Document
- Shape: one web page; company and notice title, controller line, two-paragraph intro, then three bold parts: the processing part split into 12 numbered activity blocks ("N- … sırasında;"), and a combined storage/rights/application part with three sub-headings; ends with a link to download the application form.
- Register: formal legal Turkish, "we" (Shaya Kahve/Starbucks/Şirket) to "you", very long sentences; legal bases quoted word for word from KVKK Art. 5 with paragraph and sub-paragraph letters; purposes taken from the VERBİS purpose list.
- Recurring elements: defined short names in the controller line ("Shaya Kahve", "Şirket"); no date or version shown; each activity block repeats the same three-step formula (data categories with example fields → purposes → quoted Art. 5 basis), followed by an Art. 8 domestic-transfer paragraph or a stock Art. 9 standard-contract paragraph for foreign IT providers; pointers to separate cookie and commercial-message notices; postal address, KEP and e-mail in the application part.
