## Document
- Shape: one web page inside the shop layout. Title, a one-sentence intro naming the controller, then 5 short headings. The core is one four-column table (processing context / purposes / data categories / legal basis). It is grouped into five contexts: shopping as a member or guest, corporate operations, legal obligations, FLO Plus membership, and e-commerce messages and marketing. Each basis cell spans several purpose rows. Two marketing rows are long "detaylı bilgi" explanation cells inside the table. Prose sections cover transfers, collection method and rights; the notice has no separate retention section.

Legal bases are given as statutory Art. 5 wording, and the Art. 9(4)(c) standard-contract basis is quoted in full. The marketing explanations move to plainer first-person sentences ("satmıyoruz, vermiyoruz").

Explicit consent is used for three things: publishing reviews under your name, commercial e-messages, and profiling-based marketing. Children's size data is covered in a footnote. A link points to the cookie notice for tracking-based marketing. Two foreign processors are named (Microsoft Ireland, Google LLC). Requests go only through a linked application form, answered within 30 days, free unless costly. The rights list leaves out the first Art. 11 right (to learn whether data is processed).
