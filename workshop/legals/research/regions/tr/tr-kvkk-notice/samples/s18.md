## Document
- Shape: one static page on the driving school's website; title, "Son güncelleme" date, then 9 numbered sections; section 3 has 6 numbered sub-sections, one per processing activity, each built from labelled paragraphs (data and collection method, purpose, legal basis) followed by a short practical note. Scope is the website only (contact form, exam-result lookup, cookies); the school's mobile apps are explicitly left to their own notices.
- Register: formal but readable Turkish, "we" (şirketimiz) to "you"; bases cited by exact article, paragraph and letter (Art. 5/2 (c), (ç), (e), (f); Art. 5/1 for explicit consent); many careful disclaimers about what a statement does not mean (reading the notice is not consent, a cookie click does not satisfy Art. 9, a TCKN lookup does not verify identity).
- Recurring elements: last-updated date at the top; controller block with address and e-mail at the start, reused by reference in the application section; third-party providers named (Google Analytics/Tag Manager, YouTube, Google Maps, hCaptcha/Intuition Machines, WhatsApp); pointers to the Cookie Policy and Privacy Agreement; no Art. 9 tool named, only a statement that Art. 9 conditions apply separately.
