## Document
- Shape: stand-alone page; ten numbered "MADDE" articles with decimal sub-clauses (2.1, 3.4), then an annex withdrawal form (Ek-1). Seller, consumer, order and invoice details are empty label-only field blocks, so the page is the template the checkout fills in.
- Register: formal legal Turkish, third person ("Tüketici", "SATICI" in caps as defined terms); a few clauses slip into first person plural ("Müşteri Hizmetlerimiz", "adresimiz") and one into "you" (2.3).
- Recurring elements: cites 6502 sayılı Kanun and the Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği by name in the purpose line; the withdrawal right, how to exercise it, refunds and the exception list appear twice (Madde 3–5, then again inside Madde 10), with small drift between the copies (payment methods, fax numbers, list lettering a–c vs d–f); no date or version line; hard-coded contact details (phone, e-mail, postal address, fax) sit in the withdrawal clauses while the formal contact blocks stay blank.
