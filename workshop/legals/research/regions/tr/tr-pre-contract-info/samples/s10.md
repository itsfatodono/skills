## Document
- Shape: one numbered text on the page titled "Satış Sözleşmesi" (Sales Contract), but its own subject clause calls it the "Ön Bilgilendirme Formu"; no separate pre-contract block and no separate contract follow, so the whole text is the block. Hybrid: form-style information clauses (parties, subject, price validity, withdrawal, complaints) mixed with contract-style general terms (liability limits, IP, order cancellation rights).
- Register: formal legal Turkish, third person ("ALICI", "SATICI", capitalised defined terms), long sentences, many clauses copied from the Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği wording.
- Recurring elements: "MADDE n" headings with decimal sub-clauses (6.1, 7.1); buyer fields left blank for the checkout to fill; carrier named (UPS Kargo); cross-references to Madde 1 for addresses; numbering skips Madde 11; closing e-acceptance statement covering both the form and the distance sales contract. No date, no version, no signature block.
- Product line split: separate return addresses for home/furniture goods and for clothing/accessories.
