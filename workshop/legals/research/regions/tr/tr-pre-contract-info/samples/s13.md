## Document
- Shape: stand-alone page under the brand's "About ASUS" section; title, preamble, then numbered all-caps sections (1–5, 7–9; there is no section 6), each split into lettered sub-clauses (a., b., c., ç. …); a signature line (Tarih / İmza) and then a model withdrawal form appended at the end. About 2,400 words.
- Register: formal; mixes third person with capitalised party labels (ALICI, SATICI) and direct address to the buyer ("siz müşterimiz", "dikkatinizi çekmek isteriz"); cites the consumer law and the regulation by number and gazette date.
- Recurring elements: seller details given filled in twice (section 1 and the withdrawal form); buyer and order fields left as empty "Label :" lines filled at checkout; the reseller status is stated in the opening sentence; hotline number and email repeated in several clauses; written for physical goods (cargo, inspection on delivery, named return carrier).
