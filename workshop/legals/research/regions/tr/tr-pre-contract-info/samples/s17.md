## Document
- Shape: stand-alone page; all-caps title, then 10 numbered articles ("MADDE n-") with decimal sub-clauses, a signature block (seller, buyer, date) and an appended withdrawal form ("EK) CAYMA FORMU"). Medium length (about 1,450 words with the annex).
- Register: formal legal Turkish, third person throughout; the seller calls itself "Aracı Hizmet Sağlayıcı" (intermediary service provider) instead of "Satıcı"; "işbu" drafting, "kabul ve beyan eder" acknowledgements; cites Law 6502 and the Distance Contracts Regulation with Official Gazette date and number, and in one clause the repealed Law 4077.
- Recurring elements: seller block filled in (trade name, address, phone, email, MERSIS); buyer block and order lines (product, quantity, unit price, shipping, extra fee, total) left as blank labels for the checkout to fill; named carriers with fixed fees and a free-shipping threshold; bank name and IBAN for transfers; refers to the form as "Sözleşme" in places; KEP address given only in the annex, with a different address from the seller block.
