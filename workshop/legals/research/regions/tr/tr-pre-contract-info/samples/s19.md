## Document
- Shape: stand-alone page; numbered "MADDE" articles with decimal sub-clauses (MADDE 5 is skipped and MADDE 6 appears twice), seller and consumer details as empty label-only field blocks, then an annex withdrawal form (EK1) and a closing acceptance line with SATICI / ALICI signature labels. Follows the same article order and much of the wording of the Karaca form (T2) but shorter, without the repeated withdrawal block.
- Register: formal legal Turkish, third person with capitalised defined terms ("SATICI", "TÜKETİCİ"); slips into "you" (2.3) and first person plural ("Müşteri Hizmetlerimiz", "adresimiz").
- Recurring elements: cites 6502 sayılı Kanun and the Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği in the purpose line and KVKK in the data clause; unfilled bracketed placeholders for call-centre number, customer e-mail, head-office address, fax and partner carriers; seller legal name given inline in the definitions but the seller field block left blank; no date or version line; the closing line calls the form "sözleşme".
