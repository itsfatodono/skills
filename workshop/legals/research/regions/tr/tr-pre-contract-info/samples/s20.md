## Document
- Shape: stand-alone page; six numbered "MADDE" articles, Madde 3–5 with decimal sub-clauses (3.1, 4.14), then two closing lines (electronic confirmation, 6502 as fallback law). The seller block is filled in with real data; the buyer, order line and delivery fields are dotted or empty placeholders for the checkout. No annex withdrawal form.
- Register: formal contract Turkish, third person with capitalised party names ("SATICI", "ALICI"); buyer duties phrased with the stock "kabul, beyan ve taahhüt eder"; one aside slips into "you" ("ulaşabilirsiniz").
- Recurring elements: names 6502 sayılı Kanun and the Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği in the subject article and cites the regulation's article 15(e) for the exception; seller contact details given twice (Madde 1 and a withdrawal-notice block in Madde 5); carriers named, with a flat shipping fee in TL; e-book delivery handled inside the physical-goods clauses; no date or version line.
