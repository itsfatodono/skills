UK view: only content that applies under UK law is tabled. Landing page:
`https://www.derbyshire.gov.uk/council/gdpr/record-of-data-processing-activities/` (links this
extract plus registers of contracts, sharing agreements and impact assessments).

## Document
- Shape: workbook titled "GDPR Information Audit", one sheet per department; each row a system
  or record type with a purpose, a legal basis and a run of yes / no / WIP compliance checks
  (sharing agreement, privacy notice updated, retention stated, location known, rights
  requests possible, security in place, supplier contract updated).
- Status: published extract of the internal audit. Activities: about 1,200 rows.
- Absent (UK): ICO registration number, representative, Schedule 1 conditions, transfers.
