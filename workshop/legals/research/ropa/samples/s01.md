## Document
- Shape: a landing web page (title, Art. 30 statement, DPIA paragraph, review note) links one
  2-page A3 PDF exported from Excel. The PDF is a two-column table, "Department / Area" and
  "Summary of Processing Activities", under a two-paragraph introduction. It calls itself a
  "simplified, public-facing version" of the full internal RoPA; the full record is not published.
- Activities: 24 rows. The first row ("Trust-Wide (All Areas)") is an organisation-wide roll-up;
  the other 23 are departments or services.
- Grouping: by department / service area (corporate, clinical, workforce, governance teams mixed,
  no sub-grouping or ordering by type).
- Unit of one row: a department, not a single processing activity or a data asset; each row
  bundles several activities.
- Scope: controller processing only; nothing on processing as a processor.
- Record fields: the Art. 30 elements are not columns but run-on clauses inside the summary cell,
  in a fixed order: activities/purposes, recipients ("Shares with"), security ("secured via"),
  lawful bases ("lawful basis/bases:"). Data categories and retention appear only in the
  Trust-wide row.
- Absent: data-subject categories as a field (only implied by "patient"/"staff"), third-country
  transfers, processors named, systems, information asset owner, DPIA flag, source of data,
  separate special-category condition, controller contact, DPO contact, glossary, version number
  or date on the PDF itself (only file metadata), contact for questions.
- Register: plain, third person ("the Trust"), telegraphic clauses separated by semicolons; NHS
  acronyms left unexpanded (CHC, MDT, MASH, MARAC, DoLS, PSIRF, LFPSE, TUPE, OH, DBS).
- Size: about 1,030 words in the PDF (48 cells) plus about 130 words on the landing page.
