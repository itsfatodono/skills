## Document
- Shape: one spreadsheet, two visible sheets ("ROPA - on-course students", 41 rows; "ROPA -
  student applicants", 12 rows). Each sheet opens with a one-line title "Oxford Colleges GDPR
  Project - Student Data" (or "- prospective students and applicants"), then a header row and one
  row per activity. No introduction, no landing text.
- Status: a published extract of the internal record ("for publication" in the file name),
  limited to student data; audit columns (created/modified by and date) were left in.
- Activities: 53 rows (41 + 12).
- Grouping: by data-subject group (one sheet each); within a sheet, rows follow the student life
  cycle loosely (academic, health, accommodation, finance, admissions, welfare, alumni).
- Unit of one row: a category of personal data held for a stated purpose ("Records of ...",
  "Information about your ..."), not a named process or system.
- Scope: controller only.
- Record fields: ID; Category of personal data; Source of the data; Why we process it; How long
  we keep it; Our lawful basis for processing; Details relating to lawful basis; Special category
  grounds; Special category details of public interest; Criminal conviction/allegation grounds;
  Criminal conviction grounds (further information); Created by; Created date; Modified by;
  Modified date; Staff member/dept.
- Absent: recipients, transfers abroad, security measures, controller and DPO contact, data
  subject category as a field (the sheet name carries it), systems, DPIA flag, version or review
  line.
- Register: written to the student ("your", "we"), full sentences, the wording of a privacy
  notice; the record doubles as the notice's source table.
- Recurring elements: retention periods tied to "the end of the academic year you cease to be a
  registered student"; lawful-basis cells repeat the Art. 6 sentences verbatim.
- Size: about 11,000 words; 53 rows by 11–16 columns.
