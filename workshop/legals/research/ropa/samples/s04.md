## Document
- Shape: a register web page (intro, list of the record contents the law requires, then 122
  record PDFs grouped under seven headings: Communication, Corporate, Human medicine, IT,
  Regulatory, Studies, Veterinary medicine), each record a 2–6 page PDF with the same numbered
  13-item form. Read: the register page and three records (Early Notification System, the first
  listed; Selection and Recruitment, staff; European Pharmacovigilance Issues Tracking Tool, core
  work). The three use the same form; they differ only in length (800–1,600 words) and in how
  many processors they name.
- Status: the public part of each record ("Records of data processing activity (public)").
- Activities: 122 records.
- Grouping: by business area on the register page; each record has its own reference number.
- Unit of one row: one processing operation (a system or a procedure).
- Scope: controller records only; processors are listed inside each record.
- Record fields (per record): Last update, version number; Reference number; Name and contact
  details of controller; of DPO; of joint controller; of processor; Purpose of the processing;
  Description of categories of persons and list of data categories; Time limit for keeping the
  data; Recipients of the data; Transfers to third countries or international organisations and
  safeguards; General description of security measures; For more information (privacy statement).
- Absent: legal basis as a field (the staff record cites its regulations inside the purpose),
  data source, DPIA flag, systems inventory beyond the record's own system.
- Register: formal third person ("the Agency", "EMA"), but the persons field slips into "you";
  footnotes link adequacy decisions, SCCs and data protection notices.
- Recurring elements: document number and date at the top of each PDF; page footer with the
  agency's address; "Classified as internal/staff & contractors" marking.
- Size: register page about 500 words; records 776, 799 and 1,581 words.
