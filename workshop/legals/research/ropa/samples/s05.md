## Document
- Shape: two layers. A web page summarises the record (legal duty, controller and DPO block,
  then bulleted lists of data-subject categories, data categories, purposes and recipient
  categories), and links "Schedule 1", a one-sheet spreadsheet with the full record.
- The spreadsheet is the ICO controller documentation template filled in: the same header
  block (controller, DPO, representative) and the same column set and order.
- Status: the full record, published.
- Activities: 21 rows.
- Grouping: by business function (alphabetical: Academic Development, Admissions, ... ,
  Sustainability).
- Unit of one row: a business function with one or two stated purposes.
- Scope: controller only.
- Record fields: Business function; Purpose of processing; Joint controller; Categories of
  individuals; Categories of personal data; Categories of recipients; Link to contract with
  processor; Names of third countries; Safeguards for exceptional transfers; DPA 2018 Schedule 1
  condition; GDPR Article 6 lawful basis; Link to retention and erasure policy; Retained and
  erased per policy?; Reasons for not adhering to policy.
- Absent: security measures, data source, systems, DPIA flag, processor names (the link column
  is empty), version or review date.
- Register: labels only in the spreadsheet; the web page is plain third person ("the
  University").
- Recurring elements: the same retention-schedule pointer and "Yes" / "N/A" on every row;
  ICO registration number on the web page.
- Size: web page about 700 words; spreadsheet about 3,200 words, 21 rows by 14 columns.
