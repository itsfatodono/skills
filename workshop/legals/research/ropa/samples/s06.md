## Document
- Shape: a 4-page "Data Audit Report" PDF in seven numbered parts: overview, data inventory
  table, processors table, security measures, data subject rights, next steps, supporting
  documents; approval block at the end.
- Status: the council's full record, adopted by a committee.
- Activities: 13 rows in the inventory, 13 rows in the processors table.
- Grouping: none; rows mix governance, residents' services, staff and finance.
- Unit of one row: a data category tied to one activity ("Allotment Hire Records", "Staff
  Records", "CCTV").
- Scope: controller record, plus a separate table of processors and other recipients.
- Record fields: Data Category; What Personal Data?; Who It Relates To; Purpose; Lawful Basis;
  How It's Collected; How It's Stored; Retention Period; Shared With.
- Absent: special-category condition, transfers abroad, DPO, controller address (only the name
  in the title), DPIA per row.
- Register: plain, short fragments in cells; prose only in the overview, rights and the
  processor notes.
- Recurring elements: header block with completion date, responsible officer and reviewing
  committee; footer block with adoption and next review date; checklist ticks.
- Size: about 1,100 words.
