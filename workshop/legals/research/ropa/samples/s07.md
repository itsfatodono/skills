## Document
- Shape: two layers. A web page summarises the record (legal duty, list of the functions where
  processing happens, then bulleted lists of data-subject categories, data categories,
  purposes, recipient categories, a transfers paragraph, a retention paragraph, and a closing
  block with DPO, controller address and ICO registration number); it links the full record as
  an Excel file. The spreadsheet is the ICO controller documentation template filled in
  (same header block and the same 14 columns in the same order).
- Note: Leeds Arts University's page (s05) uses the same web-page layout and many of the same
  list items; the spreadsheets differ in content. Not the same text (rule 4).
- Status: the full record, published.
- Activities: 30 rows.
- Grouping: by business function (Student Administration, Student Support and Welfare, Teaching
  and Learning, Research, Human Resources, Finance, ... ), several purposes per function.
- Unit of one row: one purpose within a business function ("Student Recruitment",
  "Pensions Management").
- Scope: controller only.
- Record fields: Business function; Purpose of processing; Joint controller; Categories of
  individuals; Categories of personal data; Categories of recipients; Link to contract with
  processor; Names of third countries; Safeguards for exceptional transfers; DPA 2018
  Schedule 1 condition; GDPR Article 6 lawful basis; Link to retention and erasure policy;
  Retained per policy?; Reasons for not adhering.
- Absent: security measures, data source, systems, processor names, DPIA flag, version or
  review date.
- Register: labels and semicolon lists in the spreadsheet; plain third person on the web page.
- Recurring elements: identical data-category vocabulary across rows; same retention pointer on
  every row.
- Size: web page about 800 words; spreadsheet about 3,000 words, 30 rows by 14 columns.
