## Document
- Shape: a register page (two-sentence legal duty, the Art. 31(1) minimum contents as bullets,
  then about 178 record PDFs, newest first, each titled "Record of Processing Activity - <name>").
  Read: the register page and three records (IMM Meeting, the first listed, came back as an HTML
  error page from Wayback; read instead: performance management of staff, DPR-2018-015, and the
  Roma Survey 2024, DPR-2024-216). Both use the same 10-part form, "Record of processing activity
  according to Article 31 Regulation 2018/1725 – Notification to the Data Protection Officer",
  with the form's instructions and tick boxes left in the published copy.
- Status: "Part 1 (Publicly available)" of each record; an internal part is implied but not
  published.
- Activities: about 178 records (many are one-off events and meetings).
- Grouping: none; reverse chronological list.
- Unit of one row: one processing operation, often one event or one survey.
- Scope: controller records; processors named inside ("Who is actually conducting the
  processing?").
- Record fields: header (name of operation, reference number, creation date, last update,
  version); 1 Controller(s); 2 Who is actually conducting the processing; 3 Purpose; 4 Categories
  of data subjects; 5 Categories of personal data (general / sensitive / criminal, tick boxes);
  6 Recipients; 7 Transfers to third countries (tick boxes for the legal ground); 8 Retention
  time; 9 Technical and organisational security measures (storage tick boxes); 10 Exercising the
  rights of the data subject.
- Absent: legal basis as its own field (asked for inside the purpose instructions), DPIA flag,
  data source.
- Register: formal; each field carries a question in the form ("Whose personal data are being
  processed?") and the article it implements; answers in full sentences, third person ("the
  Agency"); footnotes define personal data and processing.
- Recurring elements: reference number DPR-YYYY-NNN; article citation after every field
  heading; tick-box lists.
- Size: register page about 250 words of text; records about 2,350 and 2,550 words.
