## Document
- Shape: a 6-page FOI release: the FOI decision letter and schedule of records (not part of
  the record), then two RoPAs of different shape. (A) The FOI and Data Protection unit's RoPA: a
  wide table whose 17 column headings each cite the Art. 30(1) point they implement. (B) The
  Housing section's RoPA: a database export of record sets with technical column names
  (`retentionPeriod`, `modifiedBy`, "NULL" in empty cells), which the letter says is being
  revised against the national retention policy.
- Status: internal records released under FOI.
- Activities: (A) 10 rows; (B) about 160 rows.
- Grouping: (A) one unit, rows by request or case type; (B) by function, then service area,
  then record set.
- Unit of one row: (A) a processing activity (AIE requests, data breaches, FOI requests);
  (B) a record set, i.e. a file series with a file count ("Tenant Correspondence (13 files)").
- Scope: controller; the last columns of (A) ask whether the council is controller, joint
  controller or processor.
- Record fields: (A) Name of Department/Unit; Purpose [30(1)(b)]; Categories of data subjects
  [30(1)(c)]; Categories of personal data [30(1)(c)]; Categories of special category data;
  Source of personal data; Security measures [30(1)(g), Art. 32]; Retention periods; Recipients
  [30(1)(d)]; Third countries [30(1)(e)]; Safeguards [30(1)(f)]; Lawful basis [Art. 6] or Law
  Enforcement Purposes; Underlying law for processing; Legal basis for special category data
  [Art. 9]; Role (controller / joint controller / processor) [30(1)(a)]; Details of joint
  controllers; DPO contact details [30(1)(a)]. (B) Function Name; Service; Recordset Name;
  Personal Data Types; Contains Personal Data; Contains Sensitive Data; Sensitive Data Types;
  Record Format; retentionPeriod; postRetentionPeriod; Basis for Processing; location; audit
  columns.
- Absent: DPIA flag, systems by name (B gives storage locations).
- Register: (A) short phrases and long retention instructions ("Retain ... then destroy");
  (B) raw data, flags as 0/1, years as numbers (99 = permanent).
- Recurring elements: article citation in brackets in every (A) heading.
- Size: about 8,500 words including the letter (about 600 words).
