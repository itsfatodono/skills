## Document
- Shape: one PDF holding the whole register: a title page, a one-page "Disclaimer" (legal duty,
  validation, contacts, reproduction notice, version line), a two-page index of 74 numbered
  records with page numbers, then the 74 records back to back, each 2–5 pages on the same
  18-field numbered form "RECORD OF PROCESSING ACTIVITIES". The live landing page links only this
  file (version of April 2021). Read: the opening pages and three records (No 01 Deposit of IT
  Equipment for Home Use and Use of Pulse Secure, the first; No 4 Engagement of Temporary Agents,
  staff; No 73 CPVO Online Application System, core work: applications for plant variety
  rights). All three use the same form and the same controller and DPO pattern. They differ in:
  field 6 (No 4 splits it into "Internal processor" and "External processor" and names a
  recruitment-platform vendor and "its sub-processors"; Nos 01 and 73 give only internal units);
  field 8 (No 73 has two Art. 5 bases, a and b; the others only a); field 11 heading (No 73
  drops the "special categories ... origin of data" clause); field 12 heading (No 73 says "to
  block" instead of "to restrict"); field 14 (No 73 cross-refers to Records No 16 and No 11);
  field 16 (No 73 uses the transfer field for public publication in the Gazette; No 4 says
  "There are proposed transfers" with no safeguard given, apparently a slip). Elsewhere in the
  file one record labels field 6 "Name and contact details of processor".
- Status: full record text as published by the body (no public/internal split marked).
- Activities: 74 records.
- Grouping: none; numbered 01–74 in the index, HR, IT, legal and core-work records mixed.
- Unit of one row: one processing operation (a procedure, a system or a tool).
- Scope: controller records only; internal and external processors named inside field 6.
- Record fields: 1 Name of processing; 2 * Last update of this record; 3 Reference Number;
  4 * Name and contact details of the Controller; 5 * Name and contact details of DPO;
  6 Service responsible for processing personal data; 7 Description of the processing
  operation; 8 * Purpose(s) of the processing and legal basis; 9 * Description of the
  category(ies) of data subject(s); 10 When and how were data subjects informed; 11 *
  Description of the data or categories of data (including, if applicable, special categories
  of data (Article 10) and/or origin of data); 12 Procedures to grant data subjects rights;
  13 Storage media of data; 14 The recipients or categories of recipients to whom the data
  might be disclosed; 15 * Period of retention for the data; 16 * Proposed transfers of data to
  third countries or international organizations and safeguards in place if such is the case;
  17 * Measures to ensure security of processing; 18 * For more information ... see the privacy
  statement.
- Absent: DPIA flag or risk level, joint controller field, a link or URL in field 18 (the
  heading points to the privacy statement but no address follows), named recipients' countries,
  per-record version number (only a last-update date).
- Register: formal third person ("the Office", "the CPVO", "the data subject"); process
  descriptions run as stepwise bullet lists; legal sources listed under "Legal instruments"
  then "Legal basis" inside field 8.
- Recurring elements: asterisk marks mandatory fields, explained in an endnote ("i") on every
  record; a closing controller declaration on every record; the same DPO name and mailbox and
  one shared coordinator mailbox for every controller; controllers named by role (Head of Unit).
- Size: about 105,500 words in all; opening pages about 640 words; records 1,550, 1,420 and
  1,340 words.
