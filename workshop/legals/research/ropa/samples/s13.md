## Document
- Shape: two layers. A short web page (title, legal duty, controller and DPO block, role of the
  information governance team) links six spreadsheets, one per directorate (adults and health;
  children's services; corporate governance; customer, transformation and resources; environment
  and neighbourhoods; housing, regeneration and planning). Each workbook has one grid sheet
  ("ROPA") and a lookup sheet listing DPA 2018 Schedule 1 conditions that feeds the drop-downs.
  Four workbooks add a free-text "Legitimate Interests Assessment" block under the grid.
- The six workbooks share one questionnaire-style column set; small drift: one workbook drops
  "Service Area", one adds "Comments" and "LGA guidelines", one header has a typo ("Do we
  used"). File names carry a version (v6 to v16); no date or version inside the sheets.
- Status: full internal record, published (the working register, with "To be developed" and
  "??" left in cells).
- Activities: about 262 rows (273 non-empty rows across six grids, less 11 LIA note rows).
- Grouping: by directorate (one file), then service area, business unit and team; no ordering
  within a team.
- Unit of one row: a function (processing activity) of a team.
- Scope: controller record only.
- Record fields: Service Area; Information Asset Owner; Business Unit; Team; Function; Does
  delivering this function involve processing personal information?; What is the justification
  (legal basis) for us processing this info?; If there is a legal or contractual basis for this
  processing, specify legislation or provide contract details; If relying on consent, provide
  details of how that consent is obtained; Who processes the information (e.g. external
  supplier); Where and how is the information stored?; Security measures; Who do you hold
  information about?; Nature/Categories of personal data*; Does it contain special category
  information?*; Schedule 1 basis for processing special category information; Do you have a
  Special Category Assurance Statement in respect of the special category information?; Does it
  contain convictions information?; Schedule 1 basis for processing convictions information; Do
  we use the information to make automated decisions or conduct profiling of the information
  subject?; Provide details of automated decision making or profiling; Source of information; Do
  you have a Privacy Notice to inform the subject how you will use his/her personal information?;
  Who are the recipients of the information?*; If you share information routinely with third
  parties, do you have an information sharing protocol in place?; Is the information transferred
  out of the EU?; How long is it kept?; (Comments; LGA guidelines — one workbook only); Are there
  processes in place to ensure that the information is not retained beyond the retention period?
- Absent: a separate purpose column (the Function cell carries it), DPIA flag, joint controllers,
  transfer safeguards, review date inside the record, glossary; the asterisks on headers have no
  footnote.
- Register: column labels are questions to the service ("Who do you hold information about?");
  answers are fragments, mostly from staff, uneven in case and spelling; the web page is plain
  "we/us".
- Recurring elements: Art. 6 wording pasted verbatim, often with a leading asterisk; "N/A",
  "Not required", "To be developed" as fillers; named information asset owner per row; ICO
  registration number on the web page.
- Size: web page about 300 words of content; workbooks about 59,000 words, 273 rows by 27 to 30
  columns in total.
