## Document
- Shape: two parts. A landing page (https://www.mla.ac.uk/record-of-processing-activity-ropa/,
  linked from the site footer) with a legal-duty statement, a bulleted list of the record's
  contents, college-wide purpose and recipient lists, and a DPO and controller block; and a
  2-page A3 landscape PDF that holds one table with an empty title row and no other text.
- Status: public summary of the internal record. The page says the full ROPA "is maintained in a
  secure register" and further details are available on request; the PDF table is the published
  extract.
- Activities: 13 rows.
- Grouping: none stated; rows run students (admissions, records, support, marketing, alumni),
  then staff (recruitment, employment, payroll), then IT accounts, CCTV, complaints and
  information management.
- Unit of one row: a processing activity ("Student admissions", "Payroll & benefits"); one
  activity ("IT user accounts & logs") is split into two rows by data subject.
- Scope: controller only.
- Record fields: No.; Processing Activity; Purpose; Data Subjects; Personal Data; Special
  Category?; Lawful Basis; Recipients; Storage Location; Retention; Security Measures.
- Absent: transfers abroad (the page lists them as documented, the table has no column),
  special-category or Art. 10 condition, data source, DPIA reference, owner per row, version or
  review date in the PDF (only the "2026" in the file name and "reviewed regularly" on the page).
- Register: terse cell fragments; data-category cells reuse a fixed category vocabulary appended
  after specific items, often run together without separators; prose only on the landing page.
- Recurring elements: vendors and systems named in cells (Salesforce, Turnitin, Xero, Unit 4,
  Microsoft Teams); a group or parent payroll provider repeated as recipient across rows.
- Size: about 1,260 words (PDF about 690, landing page about 570); 13 rows by 11 columns.
