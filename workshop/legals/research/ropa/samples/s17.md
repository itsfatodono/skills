## Document
- Shape: a short register web page (one sentence on Art. 31, then 18 record titles that each
  link to a PDF, then a note that the page is updated and how to reach the DPO). Each record is a
  3–7 page PDF with the same 17-row, three-column grid (Nr. / Item / Description). Read: the
  register page and three records: Requests for access to documents (EDPB-0001, the first
  listed), the EDPB-EDPS Secondment programme (EDPB-0010, staff exchange; the register has no
  pure HR record), and the Art. 60 Final Decisions register (EDPB-0003, core cooperation work).
  All three use the same form. They differ in these ways: the first record's banner still reads
  "proposed structure"; the secondment record has a joint controller (EDPS), adds the Art. 28
  contact-point arrangement under the DPO, describes the procedure step by step with a timeline,
  and points by footnote to the Commission's and EDPS's own records for shared tools; the Art. 60
  record adds the publication-specific steps (redaction, the right to object, re-review of
  publication every three years). Length runs from about 1,150 to 1,950 words.
- Status: the published record itself; no public/internal split is marked.
- Activities: 18 records.
- Grouping: none on the page (roughly by record number, then date added); each record has a
  number EDPB-00NN.
- Unit of one row: one processing operation (a procedure, a register, a channel or an event
  type).
- Scope: controller records only; processors and joint controllers are named inside each
  record.
- Record fields: 1 Title of the processing operation; 2 Number of record; 3 Last updated;
  4 Data controller; 5 Joint controllers; 6 Processor(s); 7 Data Protection Officer (DPO);
  8 Description of the processing operation; 9 Purpose of processing operation; 10 Legal basis;
  11 Description of categories of data subjects; 12 Description of processed personal data /
  categories; 13 Description of procedure to ensure data subjects' rights; 14 Description of
  recipients of personal data; 15 Description of transfers; 16 Description of envisaged time
  limits for retention for each processing operation; 17 General description of technical and
  organisational security measures referred to in Article 33.
- Absent: data source as a field, DPIA flag, version number, owning unit or internal contact
  person, systems inventory, a link to the privacy statement (named but not linked).
- Register: formal third person ("the EDPB"); legal basis written out with article numbers and
  quoted article text; bullets for subjects, data and recipients; footnotes cite regulations and
  other bodies' records.
- Recurring elements: banner and legal-duty sentence at the top of each PDF; footnote 1 giving
  the regulation's short title; page numbers; the same IT and organisational security paragraphs
  reused across records; a reserve clause in legal basis and retention for audits, access
  requests and disputes.
- Size: register page about 230 words of text; records 1,148, 1,972 and 1,253 words.
