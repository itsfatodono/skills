## Document
- Shape: a 3-page landscape PDF "Data Audit": one wide table with a two-level header repeated
  on each page (six numbered bands: 1 What Personal Data Do We Hold?; 2 Lawful basis for
  holding personal data; 3 Consent; 4 Sharing Personal Data; 5 Our internal processes; 6 Action
  Needed), rows grouped under data-subject headings, then a "Council Profile" box with the
  council's size and the adoption, review and next review dates.
- Status: the council's full record, adopted by the council.
- Activities: about 40 rows.
- Grouping: by data-subject group (Staff, Councillors, Contractors/Suppliers, Members of the
  public, Community Organisations, Invitation Lists, Planning & Licensing, General Contacts).
- Unit of one row: a data item or record type held about that group ("Timesheets", "Bank
  details", "Declarations of Interest").
- Scope: controller only.
- Record fields: To whom or what does it relate?; What Data is it?; Including Sensitive Data?;
  What is it for?; Why do we have it?; Are we legally obliged to hold this data?; Lawful basis
  for processing; If consent, has consent been freely given and obtained?; With whom do we share
  this data? (LIST THEM ALL); Who is responsible for keeping it?; How often is it checked?; How
  long kept; Where held; How protected; Action needed.
- Absent: transfers abroad, processors as a field (payroll and bookkeeper appear as
  recipients), controller and DPO contact, data source, DPIA flag.
- Register: column labels are plain questions; cells are short fragments; the action column
  is a to-do list ("revise nomination form to include consent request").
- Recurring elements: "doc retention policy" as the retention answer on many rows; the same
  storage and protection phrases repeated.
- Size: about 2,400 words; about 40 rows by 15 columns.
