## Document
- Shape: the register is a section ("Register of the European Labour Authority") at the end of
  the agency's privacy-policy web page, after the rights and contact sections: two sentences on
  Art. 31, a link to a consolidated PDF ("ELA Central Register", 219 pages, revision September
  2026), then a five-column table of 99 records grouped by category. Each record is its own PDF
  on a shared, numbered template ("Template for record structure Ares reference(2022)1489054"),
  marked "PART 1: PUBLIC - RECORD". The consolidated PDF has a legal notice, a short list by
  year, and every record again in a shorter two-column form that adds legal basis and an
  automated-decision field. Read: the register section, the consolidated PDF's notice, short list
  and first entries, and three records: DPR-ELA-2022-0009 EURES for jobseekers and employers (the
  first listed), DPR-ELA-2022-0010 Selection and recruitment of staff (HR), and DPR-ELA-2025-0006
  ELA Good Practices Database (core work, the candidate URL). The three use the same template.
  They differ in these ways: the HR record has no Joint controllers row and is the only one that
  ticks a special category (health) with its Art. 10(2) grounds and the criminal-records box; the
  EURES record ticks a transfer (EEA states, Switzerland under an adequacy decision) and fills
  the transfer legal-base checklist, the HR record shows a shortened transfer block; EURES and HR
  cross-reference other ELA and European Commission records by number; the Good Practices record
  is still footed "Status: Draft" while the other two read "Status: Published". Length runs from
  about 2,200 to 4,100 words.
- Status: the public part of each record (Part 1); the consolidated PDF is a public summary
  version of all records.
- Activities: 99 records on the web table (the consolidated short list has about 96).
- Grouping: by category reflecting the agency's fields of activity (EURES, Operations, Human
  resources, Legal and regulatory affairs, Data protection, Corporate services, AI solutions,
  Independent mandated functions), then by reference number; the consolidated PDF groups by year.
- Unit of one row: one processing operation (a network, a procedure, a system or an app).
- Scope: controller records only; processors are named inside each record.
- Record fields (per-record PDF): Record reference; Title of the processing operation;
  Controller entity; Joint controllers; Processor(s) (Internal organisation(s)/entity(ies),
  External organisation(s)/entity(ies)); Data Protection Officer; Corporate Record; Language of
  the record; 1.2.1 Purpose; 1.2.2 Processing for further purposes; 1.2.3 Modes of processing;
  1.2.4 Storage medium; 1.2.5 Comments on the processing of the data; 1.3.1 Data subjects'
  categories; 1.3.2 Data categories/fields; 1.3.2.1 Special categories of personal data; 1.3.2.2
  Data related to 'criminal convictions and offences'; 1.4 Retention period; 1.5 Recipients;
  1.6 International data transfers; 1.7 Information to data subjects on their rights; 1.7.1
  Privacy statement; 1.8 Security measures. Consolidated PDF entry: Reference number; Title of the
  processing activity; Controller organisational entity; Controller contact details; Name and
  contact details of processor(s); Name and contact details of DPO; Description; Purpose of the
  processing; Legal basis for the processing; Does this processing activity involve automated
  decision-making, including profiling?; Data subjects' categories; Data categories; Special data
  categories / criminal-offence data; Retention period; Categories of recipients; Are there any
  transfers ...?; General description of technical and organisational measures; For more
  information ... see the privacy statement.
- Absent: legal basis as a field in the per-record PDFs (only the consolidated PDF has it; the
  records cite the establishing regulation inside Purpose); a last-updated date or version on the
  records (only a status footer); DPIA flag; data source as a field (told in Comments or
  Modes); the record's internal part (Part 2 is not published).
- Register: checkbox form with ☒/☐ marks for most fields and short free-text "Description"
  boxes; formal third person ("the European Labour Authority", "ELA"), with "your personal
  information" slipping in; article numbers of Reg. (EU) 2018/1725 printed in the field labels.
- Recurring elements: template reference in every page header; "Status: Published/Draft
  Page N" footer; footnote 1 on Art. 31; the same Art. 17–24 rights list and the same security
  paragraphs in every record; cross-references to other records by DPR number; the consolidated
  PDF's "Latest revision" footer.
- Size: register section about 2,400 words (mostly the table); consolidated PDF 83,000 words
  (sampled); records 4,111 (EURES), 4,055 (HR) and 2,239 (Good Practices) words.
