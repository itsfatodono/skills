## Document
- Shape: a register web page (legal duty sentence, the Art. 31(1) minimum contents as bullets,
  then an alphabetical list of 30 linked record PDFs). Each record is a 2-page PDF with a
  13-row numbered table under a two-line title. Read: the register page and three records
  (Access to the BLO, the first; Selection of personnel, staff; Registration to Events, core
  work). Same form in all three; they differ in the processor field (none; another EU body; a
  US email-marketing vendor) and, in the events record, a transfer to the US.
- Status: the public record per operation.
- Activities: 30 records.
- Grouping: alphabetical by record title; no areas.
- Unit of one row: one processing operation.
- Scope: controller records; processors named inside.
- Record fields: 1 Last update; 2 Reference number; 3 Controller (with responsible department or
  role and a contact form); 4 DPO contact; 5 Joint controller; 6 Processor; 7 Description and
  purpose of the processing; 8 Categories of persons and list of data categories; 9 Time limit
  for keeping the data; 10 Recipients; 11 Transfers to third countries and safeguards; 12 General
  description of security measures; 13 For more information ... see the privacy statement.
- Absent: legal basis as a field (listed inside field 7), data source, DPIA flag,
  special-category field.
- Register: formal third person ("the EUISS"); dashed lists inside cells.
- Recurring elements: reference codes ADM-n; contact form URL instead of a DPO mailbox; a
  placeholder "(link to privacy statement)" left in field 13 of the staff record.
- Size: register page about 300 words of content; records 656, 731 and 747 words.
