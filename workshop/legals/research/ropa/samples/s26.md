## Document
- Shape: a register landing page (legal duty sentence, the Art. 31(1) contents as bullets, a
  link to the list, DPO contact line) and a separate paginated list page (4 pages) of linked
  record PDFs titled "Record No n - <operation>". Each record is a 2-3 page PDF: a title block
  ("Record n of processing operation ...") over a two-column label/value table of 12 rows. Read:
  landing page, list pages, and three records (No 2 CIRAS - Cyber Incident Report and Analysis
  System, the first listed and also core work; No 12 Selection and Recruitment of TA, CA, SNE
  and Trainees, staff; No 33 CSIRTs Network Secretariat, core work). Same form in all three;
  they differ in length and specificity: No 2 is short with one named contractor and a legal
  basis citing directives inside the purpose; No 12 uses bullet lists, three named vendors, a
  cross-reference to other records, retention split by applicant group, and a Chapter V
  transfer sentence; No 33 names joint controllers (the national CSIRTs), four processors with
  their countries, numbered purposes with the NIS Directive article, data categories split into
  administrative and operational data, per-service retention, and a US transfer for one optional
  tool. No 12 carries an agency letterhead and a "TLP - CLEAR" marking; Nos 2 and 33 do not.
- Status: the public record per operation.
- Activities: 65 records (numbered up to 96; gaps are withdrawn records, which the list page
  says).
- Grouping: by internal record number; no areas.
- Unit of one row: one processing operation.
- Scope: controller records; joint controllers and processors named inside.
- Record fields: Date of last update; Name and contact details of controller; Name and contact
  details of DPO; Name and contact details of Joint Controller; Name and contact details of
  processor; Purpose of the processing; Description of data subjects; Description of data
  categories; Time limits (for the erasure of data); Data recipients; Transfers to third
  countries; Security measures - General description; Privacy statement.
- Absent: legal basis as a field (cited inside the purpose in two of three records, missing in
  the staff record), data source, DPIA flag, special-category field, reference code beyond the
  record number.
- Register: formal third person ("ENISA is mandated to ..."), one record addresses the reader
  ("Access to your data"); bullet or numbered lists inside cells; obfuscated mailboxes
  ("name [at] enisa.europa.eu").
- Recurring elements: record number in file name and title; responsible unit and a functional
  mailbox in the controller field; the same DPO mailbox; a stock OLAF/internal audit sentence in
  recipients; a stock "General security policy and technical/organisational measures" line.
- Size: landing page about 120 words of content, list page intro about 50 words plus titles;
  records 477, 950 and 1022 words.
