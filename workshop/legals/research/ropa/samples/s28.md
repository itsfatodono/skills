## Document
- Shape: two layers. A download page "ROPA, Records of Processing Activities" (under Document
  downloads › Councillors and leadership) links one file only, the councillors' record; it has
  no introductory text. The record is a one-page Word file: title line, one two-column table
  (label left, answer right, eleven rows), one closing sentence below the table; council logo in
  the header, page number in the footer.
- Not a set: the landing page links no per-service records. The same downloads section links a
  separate "Councillor data protection policy". The council's service-level privacy notices
  (about 90, under the main privacy notice's "Find a privacy notice") are notices, not records.
  An older registrars' "processing activities" PDF exists elsewhere on the site but is not linked
  from this page and was not read.
- Status: full record for one controller group (the elected members as individual controllers
  for ward casework), published; template wording left in place ("(include date of any
  subsequent reviews0"). File created and dated November 2018, never revised.
- Activities: one (constituent casework).
- Grouping: none; a single-activity form.
- Unit of one row: one Art. 30 field; the whole table is one processing activity.
- Scope: controller record only.
- Record fields: Name and contact details of the Controller:; Date compiled: (include date of
  any subsequent reviews); Name and Contact Details of the Data Protection Officer:; Purposes of
  the Processing:; Description of the categories of data subjects:; Categories of personal
  data:; Categories of recipients to whom personal data have been or will be disclosed;
  Transfers of personal data to a third country & safeguards:; Time limits for erasure:;
  Technical and organisational security measures:
- Absent: legal basis and Art. 9 / Sch. 1 conditions, data sources, systems or storage
  locations, processors, retention schedule pointer, DPIA flag, automated decisions, joint
  controllers, version number, contact for the record, glossary, any landing-page introduction.
- Register: field labels copy the Art. 30(1) list nearly word for word; answers in first person
  singular ("I process ...", "I share information with") as if written by one councillor, but
  the transfers answer switches to "we"/"your" privacy-notice wording.
- Recurring elements: answers open with a lead-in sentence then a bulleted category list; stock
  categories that match the ICO's registration-fee vocabulary for elected members; GDPR and EEA
  terms, no UK GDPR or DPA 2018 references.
- Size: record about 500 words, one table of 10 rows by 2 columns; landing page under 20 words of
  content.
