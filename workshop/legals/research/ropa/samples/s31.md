## Document
- Shape: a spreadsheet with three sheets: Template (blank), Example (filled with a fictional
  payroll, HR and recruitment record), Notes (instructions). A header grid for controller, DPO
  and representative sits above one wide table of 30 columns, grouped under merged super-headers:
  "Article 30 Record of Processing Activities", "Privacy Notices", "Consent", "Access Requests",
  "Data Protection Impact Assessments", "Personal Data Breaches", and "Data Protection Act 2018 -
  Special Category or Criminal Conviction and Offence data". A separate processor template
  exists.
- Status: blank regulator model with a worked example.
- Activities: example has about 30 rows.
- Grouping: by business function, then purpose; the instructions ask for one row per data
  category within an activity ("as granular as possible").
- Unit of one row: a data category within one purpose (Finance / Payroll / Bank details).
- Scope: controller (processor template separate).
- Record fields: as in the table below. Colour marks required (Art. 30, DPA 2018 Sch. 1) vs
  optional columns.
- Absent: nothing of Art. 30; it adds optional accountability columns.
- Register: column labels with "(if applicable)" / "(if possible)" qualifiers; example values
  are short labels ("Article 6(1)(c) - legal obligation", "Encrypted storage and transfer").
- Recurring elements: Notes sheet with four numbered fill-in steps and a pointer to the guide.
- Size: about 2,700 words, 30 columns.
