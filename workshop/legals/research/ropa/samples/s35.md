## Document
- Shape: regulator model inside a 40-page infokit (PDP Essentials, Jan 2026). Only one page is
  the sample: "Illustration on Data Inventory Map" (p. 12), which holds a one-paragraph
  introduction and a "Sample Data Inventory Map" grid. The grid sits under the infokit's
  "STEP 2 DOCUMENT DATA FLOWS" (p. 11), which is not the sample.
- No blank form: only a filled worked example, stamped "[For illustration purposes only]". The
  grid has a left "Steps" column with four numbered life-cycle stages; each stage carries three
  labelled cells (label on top, example value below), so the record reads as a per-activity
  form, not a multi-row register.
- Status: blank model (illustrative worked example, no real record).
- Activities: 1 example activity (recruitment for one role).
- Grouping: fields grouped by four life-cycle steps (source; collection and use; disclosure;
  retention and disposal).
- Unit of one row: one processing activity (a single process or system), not a department.
- Scope: controller only; no processor record.
- Record fields: Steps 1 Source, Location and Type of Personal Data: Data Owner; Data Subject;
  Types of Personal Data. Step 2 Collection and Use of Personal Data: Purposes of Collection
  and Use; Legal Basis; Time and Manner of Collection. Step 3 Disclosure of Personal Data:
  Other Recipients; Purpose of Disclosure; Time and Manner of Disclosure. Step 4 Retention and
  Disposal of Personal Data: Retention Period; Anonymisation of Data; Department Retaining
  Anonymised Data.
- Absent: overseas transfer fields, security measures, storage location or systems (despite
  "Location" in the step 1 label), external processors as a field (the intro mentions
  third-party organisations, the example names only internal staff), DPIA flag,
  controller/DPO block, version or review date, fill-in instructions per field.
- Register: intro is advisory third person ("can guide organisations"); the grid holds labels
  and short noun-phrase entries.
- Recurring elements: "[For illustration purposes only]" stamp; same three-cell layout per
  step.
- Size: about 70 words of intro plus a 4-step by 3-field grid (about 140 words).
