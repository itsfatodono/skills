# shareholders-agreement: merged section list

Row-level mapping: `section-map.md`. Formats per `build.md`: `prose`, `list`, `table`, plus the
layout marks `pointer` (points to a schedule or another document that is not in the sample) and
`embedded` (folded into another section).

## Section ids

An id is one kind of fact the agreement settles among the owners. Meanings were drafted while
mapping the first batch and fixed before judging s17 onward; later changes are listed under
*Changes*. A remedy or completion step written inside one right maps to that right; a general
clause maps to its own id.

| Id | Meaning |
|---|---|
| parties | Who signs and in what capacity (founders, holders, the company, guarantors), the date, recitals and purpose. |
| definitions | Defined terms and rules of interpretation. |
| capitalisation | Who holds what: share split among founders, share classes held, the holders' schedule, outstanding options or warrants, the price founders pay. |
| share-rights | Economic rights of share classes or holders: preferred dividends, how sale or liquidation proceeds are shared (liquidation preference, a founder's guaranteed share), conversion, anti-dilution, redemption. |
| option-pool | The employee share or option pool: size, who it dilutes, how options vest. |
| business | The company's business and how it is run: purpose, business plan and budget, financial year, operating and commercial covenants among the owners, use of owners' names or brands. |
| founder-roles | Each founder's role, responsibilities, time commitment, employment or service terms and pay. |
| ip | Intellectual property the founders (or staff) owe the company: assignment of existing and future IP. |
| funding | Money into the company: capital contributions, use of proceeds, further funding duties, shareholder loans. |
| dividends | When and how profits are distributed (policy, not class preferences). |
| board | Board size and composition, who appoints and removes directors, vacancies, observers, directors' term and pay. |
| management | Who appoints and removes officers or managing directors, their powers and pay. |
| decision-making | How the board and the shareholders take decisions: meetings, notice, quorum, majorities, casting vote, written resolutions. |
| reserved-matters | Decisions that need a special majority or a named holder's consent (veto list), including new share issues. |
| voting | Holders' undertaking to vote their shares to give effect to the agreement or to elect nominees; proxies and their irrevocability. |
| deadlock | What counts as a deadlock and how it is escalated and broken (mediation, chair, buy-sell, winding up). |
| vesting | Schedule over which founder shares stop being subject to buy-back (reverse vesting, cliff, acceleration). |
| compulsory-transfers | Events that force a holder to offer or sell its shares (leaving the company, good and bad leaver, death, incapacity, bankruptcy, divorce, breach, change of control) and the terms. |
| valuation | How the price is fixed and paid when shares change hands under the agreement (agreed price, fair value, formula, independent valuer, instalments). |
| transfer-restrictions | General limits on transferring or encumbering shares: consent, lock-in, indirect transfers, void transfers, certificate legends. |
| permitted-transfers | Transfers allowed free of the restrictions (family, trusts, holding companies, affiliates) and their conditions. |
| pre-emption | Holders' right to take up new shares the company issues, and its exceptions. |
| rofr | Right of first refusal or first offer when a holder wants to sell to an outsider. |
| tag-along | Right of other holders to join a sale on the same terms (co-sale). |
| drag-along | Right of a majority or a named holder to force all holders into a sale of the company. |
| exit | How the owners plan to exit: sale or IPO intention, cooperation in an IPO (lock-up), put and call options or a buy-sell (shotgun) between owners outside a deadlock. |
| information-rights | What information holders receive and may inspect, and limits for conflicted holders. |
| company-undertakings | What the company itself promises the holders (compliance, insurance, books, key-person cover, giving effect). |
| restrictive-covenants | Non-compete, non-solicitation and non-disparagement owed by founders or holders. |
| confidentiality | Confidentiality of company and agreement information; announcements. |
| warranties | Representations each holder gives (authority, title, no conflicting commitments). |
| joinder | Who else becomes bound and for which shares: transferees and new holders signing a joinder or deed of adherence, spouses' consent, shares acquired later, splits. |
| articles | How the agreement relates to the articles, bylaws or charter: precedence, duty to amend them to match. |
| term | When the agreement starts and ends, a holder's release on ceasing to hold shares, survival. |
| remedies | General remedies for breach: specific performance, penalties, default events, costs. |
| amendment | How the agreement is amended or rights waived. |
| notices | How notices are given and when they count as received. |
| disputes | How disputes are resolved: negotiation, mediation, arbitration, courts, jury waiver, process agent. |
| governing-law | Which law governs the agreement. |
| general | Closing clauses: entire agreement, severability, assignment, counterparts, headings, costs, no partnership, third-party rights, further assurance, independent advice, language. |

Not sections: the execution block and witness lines, cover pages, tables of contents and a form's
licence footer (`doc` rows: document level).

## Folds (made at merge, after judging)

None changes a judgement: every folded id was in the list from the first batch, and its samples
count for the target id with the same formats.

- `share-rights` (5/29: s08 s11 s15 s23 s34) and `option-pool` (5/29: s08 s10 s32 s33 s35) into
  `capitalisation`: what shares exist and what they carry. Both are investor-stage add-ons that
  most samples leave to the charter.
- `management` (6/29) into `board`: who appoints the officers or managing directors. All six
  samples have `board` too.
- `decision-making` (10/29) and `reserved-matters` (12/29) into one id, `decisions`: how
  decisions are taken and which need special consent. Founders read them as one subject, and the
  samples mix them (quorum rules inside reserved-matter clauses, s08 s15 s18).
- `valuation` (11/29) into `compulsory-transfers`: in 10 of 11 samples the price method serves a
  leaver, death or default buy-out (s06 also uses it for its first-refusal price).
- `dividends` (6/29) into `funding`: money in and out among owners.
- `business` (8/29) into `parties`: the company's purpose sits in the recitals in most samples.
- `warranties` (5/29) and `remedies` (16/29) into `general`: closing clauses, as in the terms of
  service; contractual penalties tied to one covenant stay with that covenant.

Meaning changes before judging (made while mapping the first batch, before s17):
`share-rights` was widened to holders' economic rights (s11's founder exit top-up); `business` to
the financial year and commercial covenants among owners (s09); `valuation` to payment terms
(s12); `exit` to a shotgun outside a deadlock (s12).

## Document level

- Length: median about 8,300 words (490 to 23,600). Owner-level samples median about 6,100,
  investor-stage about 13,000. By size: large about 10,300, mid 7,400, small 5,800; standard forms
  about 10,800 (Seedcamp's pre-incorporation form is 490).
- Recitals or an introduction: 28/29. Definitions block: 23/29 (a list or block 18, in a schedule
  or at the end 17 of those, interpretation rules 12). Table of contents: 8/29.
- Numbered articles or sections: 29/29. Schedules or exhibits: 22/29 (holders' list, joinder or
  deed of adherence form, reserved-matter lists, model articles).
- Signature block for every party: 29/29 (counted as document level, not a section); executed as
  a deed: s07 s31 s35 and the adherence deeds of s19 s23.
- Register: formal contract drafting, third person with defined party labels ("the Founders",
  "the Investors", "the Company"); long sentences; numbers in words and figures in the US texts.
  Plainer drafting only in s32 (one page) and parts of s33.
- Companion documents: 22/29 rely on others (articles or charter 16, investors' rights or
  subscription agreement, employment or service agreements, IP assignment, option plan). US
  investor-stage texts split the shareholders' agreement into a voting agreement (s02 s20 s34), a
  right of first refusal and co-sale agreement and an investors' rights agreement.
- Missing as a whole: founder vesting (26/29 have none), founders' roles and time (25/29),
  deadlock (26/29), founders' IP (20/29 name none).

## Copies and templates (rule 4)

No two samples share a whole text. Shared layouts kept as samples: the US voting-agreement layout
(board seats, drag-along, proxy, legend) in s02, s20 and the NVCA model s34, with different
wording; Delaware buy-sell drafting in s03 and s21 (s21 a cut-down template with broken
cross-references); the Chinese red-chip investor package in s19 and s23 (investor protections,
founder put). s05 and s12 were drafted by the company's own counsel, who say so in the text.
