# r8-founders: EDGAR founder-stage phrases (route R8)

Method: EDGAR full-text search (efts) for seven phrases ("founders agreement", "founders' agreement", "founder agreement", "co-founder agreement", "cofounder agreement", "founders shareholders agreement", "founders stockholders agreement"), forms S-1, S-1/A, 10-12G, 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, F-1, 20-F, SB-2, filed 2005-01-01 to 2026-11-01, all result pages. 265 hits scanned; 28 were exhibits of type EX-10*, EX-4* or EX-9* from 12 filers; each opened for the Locate check. 2 rows kept.
Column mapping: Company = filer; App = exhibit title as written; Store id = CIK, accession number; Genre = filer's industry; Ratings or downloads = form type and filing date; Document URL = exhibit on sec.gov; Developer site = the parties.
Skipped (10 filers), one line per kind:
- Securities purchase, Series A purchase, placement agency, subscription (incl. "Founders Subscription Agreement" form): Rackable SPA (the founder agreement itself is not an exhibit hit beyond the amendment kept), WorldSpace, ATAI/FSV7, Gamer Pakistan, Avenue Therapeutics (Fortress, placement agency).
- Employment, consulting, option or services contracts with a founder: Bayhill Therapeutics, InspireMD, Fresh Grapes.
- Advisory services / assignment agreement named "Founder Agreement" (SPAC sponsor): Perimeter Solutions.
- Registration rights or LP agreement of a listed company: Bloomin' Brands, Bumble.
- Repurchase rights agreement among key holders of a public shell after a merger (not a private company's owners): Ascend Acquisition (Andover Games).
