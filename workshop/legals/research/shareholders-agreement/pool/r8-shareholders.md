# r8-shareholders: EDGAR pool slice for shareholders-agreement

Slice R8 (shareholders' and stockholders' agreements of private companies, including joint-venture companies), collected for `shareholders-agreement` (language en). Method: EDGAR full-text search (efts) for the phrases "shareholders agreement", "shareholders' agreement", "stockholders agreement", "stockholders' agreement", "joint venture shareholders agreement" and "shareholders agreement" + "deadlock", date range 2005-01-01 to 2026-11-01, first 300 hits per phrase and pass, exhibit hits only (EX-10*, EX-4*, EX-9*). Pass (a) forms F-1, F-1/A, 20-F, 6-K (foreign filers); pass (b) forms S-1, S-1/A, 10-12G, 8-K, 10-K, 10-Q (mostly US filers). About 2,240 hits scanned, 389 distinct filers opened (first screen of each exhibit). Rows are in order of CIK within each pass.

Column mapping: Company = filer name; App = exhibit title as written (first match on the exhibit's first screen; generic where none found); Store id = CIK and accession number; Genre = filer's industry and home country (estimated); Ratings or downloads = form type and filing date of the hit (earliest qualifying hit seen); Document URL = exhibit URL on sec.gov; Developer site = parties and stage.

Kept: 60 filers, one row each, earliest qualifying exhibit seen. The stage in column 7 comes from the first screen only; exhibits that are amended and restated or in form state that on the title.

Skipped (about 329 filers), one line per kind:
- amendments, joinders, adhesion and termination agreements to a shareholders' agreement (not the agreement itself);
- agreements of a listed company with its sponsor or holders that start at or after the IPO (director nomination, governance, "form of" agreements effective at the IPO, e.g. Cellu Tissue, Education Management, Union Drilling, CI&T, StoneCo, HeadHunter, Birkenstock, Fidelis, Coty, Ternium, Seanergy, Solarfun);
- registration or investors' rights agreements, M&A support or voting agreements, option, warrant and subscription agreements, joint-venture formation or licence agreements that are not among owners of one company, employment and management-incentive plans;
- exhibits that mention the phrase only in passing, did not load or whose first screen showed another document;
- later filings by a filer already in the rows.
