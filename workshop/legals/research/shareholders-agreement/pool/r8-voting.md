# r8-voting: EDGAR voting agreements among a private company's holders (R8)

Method: EDGAR full-text search (`efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index`), phrases "voting agreement" + "key holders", "amended and restated voting agreement", "voting agreement" + "drag-along", "voting and drag-along agreement", "shareholders voting agreement". Forms S-1, S-1/A, F-1, F-1/A, 10-12G; 2005-01-01 to 2026-11-01, searched in three-year windows (two result pages each). Kept exhibit hits only (EX-10*, EX-4*, EX-9*): 399 exhibits from 247 filers; opened the first screen of every exhibit and kept a filer when the title and recitals show a voting agreement among a private company's holders about electing the board, voting shares or drag-along.

Columns: Company = filer; App = exhibit title as written; Store id = CIK and accession number; Genre = industry, home country; Ratings or downloads = form and filing date; Document URL = exhibit on sec.gov; Developer site = parties and stage. One row per filer, earliest qualifying exhibit; the exhibit date can precede the filing date (older agreements filed with the IPO).

Skipped (one line per kind): amendments only (Codexis, Data Domain, Homeowners Choice, Proto Labs); merger or acquirer voting agreements (Brookdale, Motient/MSV, Clearwire-Intel, MultiSensor AI); sponsor or proxy voting agreements of a holder group at listing (Caesars omnibus, Facebook holder proxy, Spotify, Cloudastructure); filers whose agreement is a stockholders' agreement (Arsanis; left to r8-shareholders); investors' rights, registration rights and other EX-4/EX-10 hits that are not voting agreements (about 350 exhibits). Rows kept: 27; BioFuel (form of restatement, listed company) skipped.
