## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (type EX1A-3 "HLDRS RTS", described as "Form of Shareholders Agreement", Exhibit 3 to the filer's 2018 Regulation A offering statement on Form 1-A), so filed as an instrument defining holders' rights, the Reg A counterpart of an EX-4. Title, a one-sentence preamble, two WHEREAS recitals and a NOW, THEREFORE line, then six numbered sections with decimal sub-sections (definitions, transfer restrictions, right of first offer with involuntary transfers and drag-along, after-acquired securities, board composition, miscellaneous), a signature page and Exhibit A listing the founders. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: US closely-held-company drafting ("shall", "hereto", "provided, however", "notwithstanding"), defined terms in quotes at first use, numbers written in words then digits, two all-caps certificate legends; founders and initial director designees named in the text.
- Recurring elements: the carve-out for Permitted Transferees (immediate family and Affiliates) across transfer, assignment and further-assurance clauses; the condition that the founders and their Permitted Transferees together keep majority voting power, which switches the right of first offer and the drag-along on and off; "all, but not less than all"; drafting slips left in (the preamble's defined terms said to be defined in the recitals, "Offered Securities" alongside "Offered Shares", "Requisite Parties" alongside "Requisite Holders", "Aggregation Principals").
- Stage: founder (two founders who formed the company, plus any later holder who signs a counterpart); matches the candidate row. Borderline with early: the board clause already gives a seat to the holders of a separate class of common stock, a holder group other than the founders, but no investor class or preferred stock appears.
- Parties: the company, two founders listed on Exhibit A, and "Shareholders" defined as anyone who later acquires shares and signs a counterpart (open-ended joinder by counterpart, no separate joinder form).
- Status: executed form with blanks: dated, with the deal's names, board designees and company address filled in, but the signature lines are unsigned and the shareholder counterpart block is blank (name, title, email, address).
- Companion documents named: the Certificate of Incorporation (defines the classes of common stock; governs director elections where a designation right lapses); a promissory note and pledge as the form for deferred purchase prices; the AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules (Expedited Procedures). No stock purchase, vesting, IP assignment or employment agreement is named.
- Absent: founder share vesting, leaver and good/bad leaver terms, death or disability buyout (transfers to family on death are exempt from the involuntary-transfer offer); IP assignment, confidentiality and non-compete; founder roles and time commitment; reserved matters or shareholder decision rules beyond board composition; deadlock; tag-along; pre-emption on new issues; information rights; dividend policy; spousal consent.
- Size: 5,843 words (about 5,790 in the body; the signature page and Exhibit A are about 50).
