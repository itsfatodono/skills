## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-4.3 to the filer's 2014 registration statement) of an agreement signed in 2012, when the company was private. A cover page (title, company, party groups, date), a numbered list of six parties each followed by a "Hereinafter referred to as" definition, a one-line operative formula ("THE PARTIES HAVE AGREED AS FOLLOWS"), fourteen numbered articles with headings in capitals ("Article N: HEADING"), some with decimal sub-sections and roman-numeral lists, a "Done in [place], on [date]" execution clause, conformed signatures, and a list of two appendices whose contents are not filed. No recitals. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: Continental (Belgian) contract English translated or drafted by non-native drafters: "shall mean" definitions, Dutch legal terms in parentheses after their English label (bring along right, pre-emption right), "the present Agreement", typos and repeated text (one term defined twice, a mistyped year). Short articles that mostly point to the Articles of association or the Belgian Company Law rather than setting their own mechanism.
- Recurring elements: deferral to the Articles (transfers, bring-along, pre-emption, founder stock rights) and to the Company Law (pre-emption, class rights, control); share classes as the unit of rights (Class A for the founders, Class B for warrant holders, Class C for the investors); a reference price per share carved out three times (bring-along floor, survival on termination, precedence over the Articles); "such approval not to be unreasonably withheld" for company consent.
- Stage: early. Founders (two individuals and their holding company) with a Belgian private equity investment company and a small group of private investment companies and an individual; the investors hold a separate share class with a price protection but the text never calls it preferred stock, and the company was about two decades old at signing, so the sample sits between `early` and `vc`. Kept as in the candidate row. Rule 6: a contract among the shareholders of a then-private company, with the company as a party; it runs for a fixed term from signing and names an IPO only as one possible exit route, so it neither starts at nor depends on an IPO.
- Parties: two founders (individuals, also defined as the company's Key Managers) and the founders' holding company, together the Class A holders; one private equity investment company and three investors acting through one attorney, the Class C holders; and the company. Class B warrant holders are defined but are not parties.
- Status: signed exhibit (conformed /s/ signatures); Appendices 1 and 2 are listed but not included in the filing.
- Companion documents named: the Articles of association (transfer restrictions, bring-along, pre-emption, founder stock voting and dividend rights; they prevail over the agreement except for the reference price); the Belgian Company Law (pre-emption on cash capital increases, modification of class rights, definition of control); Appendix 1 (shareholding and outstanding warrants); Appendix 2 (agenda, resolutions and coordinated Articles for an extraordinary general meeting).
- Absent: founder share vesting and leaver buy-back; IP assignment; founder roles and time commitment (beyond the non-compete's link to the founders' positions); board composition, shareholder decision rules and reserved matters; deadlock; tag-along; right of first refusal mechanics (left to the Articles); joinder or deed of adherence for new holders; dispute resolution other than a court forum.
- Size: 2,704 words (about 2,590 in the body, the rest the execution clause, signatures and appendix list).
