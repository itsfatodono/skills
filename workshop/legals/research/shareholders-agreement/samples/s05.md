## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-4.1 to the filer's 2005 Form 10 registration, filed when the bank holding company was formed) of an agreement dated 1999 among the organisers of a de novo bank, before the bank opened and while it was private. Title, a one-sentence preamble listing the individual shareholders, two WHEREAS recitals, then eighteen numbered sections with run-in headings (one transfer mechanism, then boilerplate), and a signature page. No articles, no definitions section, no schedules. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: plain small-firm US drafting ("shall", "hereto", "WITNESSETH"), short and largely self-contained; defined terms in quotes at first use, three terms defined inline inside the right of first refusal; male pronoun for the selling shareholder; one all-caps legend quoted in full.
- Recurring elements: "shares of Stock" throughout; every number written in words with figures in brackets; the Bank (not the shareholders' holding company) is the company referred to throughout.
- Stage: founder (individual incorporators and first directors of a start-up bank, at formation, no investors as a class); matches the candidate row.
- Parties: individual shareholders only, each an incorporator or director of the bank; the bank itself is not a party and does not sign.
- Status: signed exhibit (conformed /s/ signatures with signing dates about five months after the effective date); one more person signs than the preamble lists.
- Companion documents named: stock option agreements between shareholders and the bank (expressly not superseded); otherwise none, not the charter or bylaws.
- Absent: founder share vesting and leaver or buy-back terms; death, disability or departure buy-out and valuation; IP assignment, confidentiality and non-compete; founder roles or time commitment; board composition, reserved matters and decision rules (it disclaims any voting arrangement); deadlock; tag-along, drag-along and pre-emption; exit or sale process; dispute resolution beyond a court action.
- Size: 2,498 words (about 2,290 in the body, the rest signature blocks).
