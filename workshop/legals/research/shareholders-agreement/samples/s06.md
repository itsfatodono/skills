## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-9.1 to the filer's 2013 Form S-1) of an agreement dated 2011 among the first shareholders of a company being set up in Delaware, while it was private. Title page, a preamble listing each shareholder with address and phone, then the company; an unheaded paragraph on when it binds; RECITALS (purpose, formation and capital, a per-holder cap table); then two headed blocks: SPECIAL PROVISIONS (Sections 1 to 7, the substance) and GENERAL PROVISIONS (lettered clauses (a) to (q), the last one the signature page). Three exhibits: A, a joinder consent; B, a two-sentence "standard bylaws" stub; C, an arbitration clause. No definitions article; terms defined inline. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: small-firm drafting by non-specialists. Classic US share-restriction wording (right of first refusal, death buy-out, lawful-purchase limits) runs next to plain, lightly edited additions (resale caps, forced sale, directors) that use capitals for SHAREHOLDER and COMPANY. Typos and inconsistencies: "EXHEBIT", "Security Exchange Commissioner (SEC)", "United State Treasury Prime Interest Rate", one corporate holder called LLC in one place and Inc. in another, and male pronouns with an occasional "him/her".
- Recurring elements: "Shares", "Purchase Price" and the Board of Directors' supermajority vote recur across sections; deadlines are written in words with figures in brackets; "page X of 20" footers.
- Stage: founder. The candidate row says `early`; corrected, because the recitals set up the company and every holder takes the same single class at the same founding price per share, with no investor class or later round. Individual holders plus one corporate holder; some hold large founder-sized blocks and some hold small ones.
- Parties: individual shareholders and one corporate shareholder, around ten in all, plus the company as a named party; the company does not sign the signature page.
- Status: signed exhibit with conformed /s/ signatures; the agreement's date is left blank, and the consents in Exhibit A are dated and signed by the same founding holders (one consent leaves the company's name blank, and a name sits in the title field).
- Companion documents named: bylaws (Exhibit B, only a stub on how to amend them); Exhibit C arbitration; no charter, stock purchase, vesting, IP or employment agreement is named.
- Absent: founder share vesting and good- or bad-leaver buy-back on leaving (only buy-outs on death, incompetence or breach); IP assignment and confidentiality (Exhibit C's injunction carve-out refers to confidentiality and IP provisions that the agreement does not contain); non-compete; roles and time commitment; deadlock; reserved matters beyond the board supermajority for new shares and price waivers; pre-emption, tag-along and drag-along; exit or sale process; any term or termination clause (no end date, no IPO trigger; it only says the SEC rules apply to sales and director nominations once the company is public).
- Size: 4,981 words (about 3,600 up to the signature page, the rest signatures and exhibits).
