## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-4.7 to the listed buyer's parent's 2013 registration statement) of an "Execution Copy": cover page with a confidential-treatment legend, a table of contents with page numbers and lists of ten exhibits and two schedules, a one-sentence preamble naming all parties, eleven lettered recitals, "The Parties therefore hereby agree as follows", seven articles ("ARTICLE N" over a capitalised heading) with decimal sections and lettered and roman-numeral sub-clauses, about thirty conformed signature pages, then the exhibits and schedules. Redactions marked with bracketed asterisks and "###". Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: US (New York) M&A drafting applied to a Dutch company: dense "is permitted to" / "shall not" style, very long sentences, "such Party" repetition, capitalised defined terms for every actor and many points back to the SPA; Dutch elements bolted on (articles of association, a civil-law notary, Dutch-language legal terms in a deed). A few drafting slips (a cross-reference to Section 7.3 inside 7.4, shareholder votes tied to matters "voted on by the Board", a defined parent IPO term used nowhere).
- Recurring elements: mirror clauses for the two sides ("[parent]-Designated" and "[minority side]-Designated" directors and officers, each removable only by its appointer); "commercially reasonable efforts"; "directly or indirectly" and look-through to every layer of the minority chain; shareholders' undertaking to vote and act as directors to carry out each clause; fixed day counts for every step (notice, ROFR, put, arbitration).
- Stage: jv. Two corporate owners of one private Dutch B.V. (the listed buyer's subsidiary with a majority stake and the founders' holding company with the minority), plus the buyer's listed parent, a sister company of the minority holder and two layers of the minority holder's indirect owners (personal holding companies, trust-office foundations and individuals, the company's founders and staff among them). Kept as in the candidate row. Rule 6: a contract among the shareholders of a private company, with the company as a party, on governance, transfers and exit; it takes effect at the closing of the share purchase that creates the joint ownership, not at an IPO, and the parent's IPO is only a defined term never used; the filer is the majority side's parent, which is listed, but the company governed is private.
- Parties: two corporate shareholders (majority buyer and minority founders' holding), the company, the buyer's parent, the minority holder's sister company, the minority holder's direct owners and ultimate owners by exhibit, later direct holders by joinder, and the company's directors for one board-delegation clause only.
- Status: signed exhibit (conformed /s/ signatures); exhibits included, the notarial deed as an unsigned draft with blanks; a Schedule 4.5 deed form referenced in the ROFR clause is not attached under that number; confidential portions redacted.
- Companion documents named: the stock purchase agreement (definitions, closing, expenses, entire agreement), the option agreement and the earlier joint-venture agreement (superseded), the regional venture's operating agreement (put valuation), the parent's stockholder agreement with the minority side (its share transfers, non-competes), the company's articles of association (transfer-approval procedure, creative-matters delegation; the agreement prevails), the put option purchase agreement and brand purchase agreement to be signed later, a joinder agreement in a form the board accepts, financing documents.
- Absent: founder share vesting and leaver buy-back (the minority's exit is a put, not a leaver mechanism); IP assignment to the company (IP appears only inside confidential information and the brand list); founders' roles and time commitment beyond officer appointments; tag-along and drag-along; pre-emption on new issues of the company (only a ban on issues up the minority chain); a deadlock clause beyond the budget fallback and arbitration; a term or termination clause; spousal consent.
- Size: 23,627 words in the stripped text (about 19,800 in the body from preamble to signature line, about 1,000 in signature pages, about 2,400 in exhibits and schedules, the rest cover and contents).
