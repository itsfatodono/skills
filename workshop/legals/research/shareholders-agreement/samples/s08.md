## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-10.11, accession 0001193125-16-434605, the filer's own document served from sec.gov) of a Dutch BV's Series C shareholders' agreement drafted by a Dutch law firm: cover page with date and party groups, contents list of twelve clauses and eight schedules, numbered party list, six lettered recitals, twelve numbered clauses with decimal sub-clauses and run-in sub-headings (definitions; articles and ownership; capital and share rights; use of proceeds and dividends; issues and pre-emption; transfers; management board; supervisory board; general meeting; registration rights; ESOP and other matters; various), a notices address block, a two-column signature page, then Schedule 1.1 (definitions table) and seven schedule title pages without content. Version taken: the exhibit as filed.
- Register: continental English of a Dutch firm ("the Parties undertake", "in first instance", Dutch terms in brackets such as besloten vennootschap, ontbinding, stichting, notarial deed) grafted onto US venture term-sheet language (piggyback, Form S-3, market stand-off, pay to play, full ratchet, FCPA); long sentences, defined terms in quotes and brackets, numbers in words then digits, deal values stated "for the avoidance of doubt"; worked "for example" cases and one italic "Note" explaining a clause.
- Recurring elements: Preferred Majority and Preferred Supermajority consents throughout (dividends, issues, transfers through the articles' approval right, committees, director pay, amendments); funding the second tranche as a condition of rights (penalty conversion, loss of anti-dilution, board seat and supermajority vote); summaries of the New Articles "meaning in summary" with the articles controlling share rights; holders' duty to vote and to amend the articles so the agreement takes effect; drafting slips (survival clause and Schedule 1.1 cite wrong clause numbers, two items lettered "(b)", a defined term not used, a cited schedule missing).
- Stage: vc (four share classes, three of them preferred, held by venture funds and corporate venture arms in a later round); matches the candidate row. The founder takes part as a holder through his personal holding company and in person, and as CEO.
- Parties: the company, twenty shareholders (US, European and offshore funds, pharma corporate venture arms, Dutch funds and a Dutch corporate, the founder's personal holding company, and the depositary-receipt foundation holding ESOP shares) and the founder in person; the founder's holding and the founder are together "Founders".
- Status: signed exhibit (conformed /s/ signatures for every party); schedules other than the definitions are filed as titles only.
- Companion documents named: the New Articles (share rights, transfer approval, board rules; this agreement prevails between the parties), the Subscription Agreement (tranches, commitments, investor majority, pay-to-play conversion), the replaced earlier shareholders' and subscription agreements, the Business Plan, the ESOP and option overview, a future registration rights agreement and lock-up agreements, a drag-along agreement the founders and key managers must sign, employment contracts with restrictive covenants, a Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement for staff, a management rights letter, an FCPA certification form, and a corporate investor's right-of-first-negotiation letter agreement.
- Absent: founder share vesting or reverse vesting, good and bad leaver pricing (the founder's exit only moves his votes to the foundation, he keeps the economics); founder roles and time commitment beyond naming the CEO; IP assignment and non-compete owed by the founders as holders (left to staff agreements); death or disability; a valuation method for compulsory transfers; shareholder deadlock beyond the board tie-referral chain; spousal consent; a joinder form or deed of adherence (accession is a clause duty only).
- Size: 21,336 words in the stripped text, including the notices address block, signature page and Schedule 1.1.
- Qualification (rule 6): it terminates only on a Liquidation Event or a Qualifying IPO (12.5), so it is a pre-IPO agreement among a private company's owners and passes rule 6.
