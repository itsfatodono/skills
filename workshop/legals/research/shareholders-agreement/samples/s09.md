## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-10.14 to the filer's 2008 filing) of an agreement dated 2007 between two corporate owners who formed an equally owned joint company, the company also a party. Title, one-paragraph preamble with each party's state of incorporation and address, four WHEREAS recitals (each owner's business, the prior memorandum of understanding, a completed prototype study), then eleven numbered articles with run-in all-caps headings and decimal sub-sections (article 4 uses lettered paragraphs), a signature page, an appendix index and one patent-list appendix. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document (the filer is one of the two owners).
- Register: plain small-company commercial drafting, "shall" and "will" mixed, owners referred to by defined acronyms; the commercial deal (roles, payments, manufacturing, funding schedule) sits in the same text as the shareholder terms, with exact amounts and dates written in. Loose drafting: typos, a missing word in a definition, a list "(i) … (ii)" joined without "or", a dangling "(to be paid … by the Company,)".
- Recurring elements: "the prior written consent of the Shareholders" as the decision rule for anything outside the ordinary (new business, new equity); cross-references to the confidentiality article from the information clauses; the cost-plus pricing basis repeated from the roles article into the manufacturing article; "in connection with the Joint Venture" as the scope limiter.
- Stage: jv (two corporate owners of a newly formed joint company, each contributing technology or funding); matches the candidate row.
- Parties: two corporate owners (a laser manufacturer and a dental-technology commercialiser) and the joint company.
- Status: signed exhibit with names and titles typed, no /s/ marks; the company's signatory name is blank; Appendices B (the second owner's IP) and C (manufacturer's standard terms) are listed but not included in the filing.
- Companion documents named: the earlier memorandum of understanding between the owners (basis of the joint venture, the prototype order and the contributions); the company's Certificate of Incorporation and By-Laws (the agreement prevails and they are to be amended to match); the manufacturer's standard terms and conditions (Appendix C) for supply; "any of the agreements with the Company" whose breach is a default.
- Absent: definitions article (only "Affiliate" and a few inline terms); founder-style vesting and leaver terms (replaced by an event-of-default buy-out); non-compete and non-solicit; deadlock mechanism (two-director board, one vote each, with no casting vote or escalation); quorum and meeting rules; reserved-matters list (only two consent items and joint sales decisions); right of first refusal, pre-emption, tag-along and drag-along; exit or IPO provisions; dispute resolution forum or arbitration; dividend policy.
- Size: 3,152 words in the body to the end of the boilerplate; about 3,340 with the signature page and appendices.
