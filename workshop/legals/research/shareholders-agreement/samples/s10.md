## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-10.1, accession 0001193125-13-407296, the filer's own document served from sec.gov) of an Israeli company's amended and restated shareholders agreement: title, a one-sentence preamble naming three party groups by schedule, two "Whereas" recitals, seven numbered articles with decimal sub-sections and run-in headings (definitions; registration rights; information rights and certain covenants; covenants on conversion; incorporation of provisions of the Articles; insurance; miscellaneous), a "Remainder of Page Intentionally Left Blank" line, signature pages grouped by party class, then Schedule I(a) and Schedule I(b) (holder name and address tables). Page numbers survive in the stripped text. Version taken: the exhibit as filed.
- Register: US investors'-rights-agreement wording (demand and piggyback registration, market stand-off, cross-indemnification, Rule 144 reporting) adapted to a foreign private issuer (Forms F-1 and F-3, WKSI, shelf supplements) by Israeli drafting ("Bring Along", "Right of No-Sale", "registered share capital", "without derogating from the aforesaid", em-dash glosses); long sentences; numbers in digits with the words in brackets; typos left in ("Perefrred", "PREFRRED", "underwriten", "Articles or Association", "adopted on July 28" without a year in 5.1).
- Recurring elements: Majority Preferred consent (defined differently before and after the IPO); Permitted Transferees aggregated with the holder; the Articles of Association adopted on a stated date as the source of share rights and transfer terms; an "on an as converted basis" count; each article saying whether it ends at the IPO.
- Stage: vc (six series of preferred shares held by US and European venture funds); matches the candidate row. Founders take part only as Ordinary Shareholders (some also hold preferred); the agreement gives them no class of their own.
- Parties: the company; the Ordinary Shareholders in Schedule I(a) (the CEO and other founders, an Israeli venture fund pair); the Preferred Shareholders in Schedule I(b) (US venture funds and their parallel vehicles, Luxembourg funds, individual holders, one private company). Ordinary and Preferred Shareholders together are the "Party Shareholders".
- Status: signed exhibit (conformed /s/ signatures; a few holder signature lines left blank, which 7.4 allows: it binds all listed holders once signed by the company and the Majority Preferred); individual holders' addresses redacted as "[###]".
- Companion documents named: the Articles of Association adopted at a stated date (preemptive rights, no-sale, right of first refusal, co-sale, bring-along, major-decision restrictions and election of directors, pulled in by reference; the Articles prevail on conflict); the Series D and Series E share purchase agreements and two founders share sale agreements (used only to define registrable securities); the replaced former shareholders agreement; a Board-approved Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement for staff; employee share purchase and option agreements; underwriting and lock-up agreements.
- Absent: founder share vesting, reverse vesting and leaver terms (vesting is required only of future employees); founder roles, time commitment, founder IP assignment and non-compete as owners (IP is left to staff PIIAs); deadlock; death or disability; a valuation method; spousal consent; a joinder or deed of adherence. Transfer restrictions, exit (bring-along), decision rules and board appointment are not written out: one clause lists the Articles that carry them.
- Size: 12,394 words in the stripped text, including the EDGAR header lines, signature pages and both schedules.
- Qualification (rule 6): a pre-IPO restatement among the holders of a private Israeli company. Information rights and covenants end at the closing of an IPO except confidentiality (3.6), the incorporated transfer and governance Articles end at a Qualified IPO and the co-sale right at an IPO (5.5), and D&O insurance at an IPO (6); only the registration rights and market stand-off of Article 2 run after the IPO, to a set anniversary or Rule 144 eligibility (2.12). Governance and transfer terms end at the IPO, so it is in. By bulk, though, it is mostly a registration rights agreement: Article 2 is well over half the text, and the shareholder terms the skeleton wants are only pointed to in the Articles.
