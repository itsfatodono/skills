## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-10.10, filed in July 2014 ahead of the filer's IPO) of an agreement signed while the company was private. A centred title with the date, a one-paragraph preamble naming the parties with defined labels, three "WHEREAS" recitals, an operative formula ("NOW THEREFORE IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED AS FOLLOWS"), four numbered sections with headings (decimal sub-sections, two of them inserted later as "2.2A" and "2.2B"), a "[left blank - Signature pages to follow]" line, two signature pages with blank "By:/Title:" blocks, and Schedule A listing the shareholders. Two formulas are images inside the text (fetched and read); each formula and threshold is followed by a worked numeric "For example". Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: Israeli contract English; the founder is named by first name as a defined label throughout; long single-sentence sub-sections with "provided however" and "For the avoidance of doubt"; small slips (a mistyped year in the recitals, missing spaces). Narrow scope: the whole agreement is a set of financial assurances to the founder from the company and the other shareholders, not a general shareholders' agreement.
- Recurring elements: almost every defined term borrowed from the Articles of Association; "the Company or the Shareholders, as the case may be, shall cause to be paid"; worked examples with round sample numbers after each formula; the exit right and the IPO right each opening with "Unless [founder] had earlier exercised the right set forth in Section N".

Rule 6: a contract among the shareholders of a then-private company with the company as a party; it has no term clause, but its rights fall due at an exit event or immediately before an IPO and are alternative, so it works up to and at the IPO and does not start at one. In.
