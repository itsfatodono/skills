## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-10.3 to a 2026 filing by the holding company VME Companies, Inc.) of a 2003 agreement of its operating company, a private Texas corporation. Two-line title, a one-sentence preamble, an "Article 1" purpose statement in place of recitals, fourteen numbered and capitalised articles with decimal sections and lettered sub-clauses, a page footer "Shareholder Agreement Page N of 24" on every page, a company signature page, and Exhibit A (holders and holdings). Exhibits 3.2 (adoption agreement), 8.1 (valuation method), 8.2 (promissory note) and 8.3 (stock pledge) and the holders' joinder pages are cited but not filed. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: Texas small-company lawyer's drafting; long sentences, "he" for every holder, heavy cross-referencing to the formula price of Section 8.1 and the two payment profiles of 8.2; a few slips ("Section 3.73.7", a notice sentence pointing at 7.4(c), stray parentheses, "Internal Federal Code"); bracketed text kept from the drafter's form (the IPO sunset on pre-emption, the deleted Article 9).
- Recurring elements: "at the purchase price per Share determined pursuant to Section 8.1 to be payable in accordance with the Conservative (or Aggressive) Payment Terms" closes every buy-out trigger; company first, then the remaining holders pro rata "or as such Remaining Shareholders may otherwise agree among themselves"; successors take unbought shares only after signing the adoption agreement of 3.2; "Solely as among the parties to this Agreement" before each binding-notice rule.
- Stage: early. Kept from the candidate row with a caveat: the text defines one founder holder, says no venture capital holder exists, and treats every other holder as an employee holder; Exhibit A lists the founder with a majority and one other holder, so it is a founder with one working minority holder rather than founder-and-investor. Rule 6: a contract among the shareholders of a private company with the company and the spouses as parties, setting transfer, buy-out and exit rules; 13.3(c) ends it at the company's IPO, and 3.8 says the same of pre-emption, so it is pre-IPO and works up to the IPO. In.
- Parties: the company, the holders listed on Exhibit A (individuals: one founder, one other), their spouses as parties in their own right, and later holders and their spouses through the adoption agreement.
- Status: signed exhibit in part: the company's signature page carries a conformed /s/ signature by its president (the founder); the holders' and spouses' joinder pages are not in the filing.
- Companion documents named: the articles of incorporation (only for the common stock definition); employment or consulting agreements of employee holders (their end triggers the leaver option); the company's credit and loan agreements (transfers that would default them are barred); the four exhibit forms above (adoption agreement, valuation method, promissory note, stock pledge and purchase money security agreement). No investors' rights or stock purchase agreement.
- Absent: founder share vesting (the leaver and passive-holder call options stand in for it, with one formula price for all leavers); IP assignment; founder roles and time commitment beyond the part-time test; a non-compete as such (competing triggers a call option instead); board composition, decision rules, reserved matters and voting (Article 9 deleted); deadlock as such (the push/pull clause is open to any two holders after a waiting period); information rights.
- Size: 12,658 words (about 12,430 in the body, the rest the company signature page and Exhibit A).
