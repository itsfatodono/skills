## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-10.43, a plain-text filing described as the stockholders agreement "as amended", filed in 2006 while the filer prepared its IPO) of an agreement signed while the company was private. A centred title, a one-sentence preamble naming the parties with defined labels, three "WHEREAS" recitals and a "NOW, THEREFORE" formula, four articles ("ARTICLE I DEFINITIONS" to "ARTICLE IV MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS") of numbered "Section N.N" clauses with run-in headings and lettered sub-clauses, a "[SIGNATURE PAGE FOLLOWS]" line, signature pages, Exhibit A (joinder form), then three amendments filed after it, each with its own preamble, recitals, numbered sections that restate amended clauses in full, and signature pages. Most numbers are written in words with the figure in parentheses.

- Stage: vc (corrected from `early` in the candidate row).
