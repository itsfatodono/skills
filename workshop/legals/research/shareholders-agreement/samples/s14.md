## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-10.23, accession 0000950137-06-010763, the filer's own document served from sec.gov) of a 2005 stockholders agreement of a private Delaware holding company, made when a private-equity sponsor and the management holders contributed their shares in the operating company (the name the filer carries) to the holding company under a share-exchange agreement. Title, one-sentence preamble naming the company and three holder groups defined later, three WHEREAS recitals (holders own common stock or options; the exchange agreement; this agreement as a condition precedent to the exchange), consideration line, then six numbered SECTIONs with capitalised headings over decimal sub-sections with run-in headings and lettered paragraphs, "[Signature Pages Follow]", signature pages per holder, and three one-paragraph joinder forms (Exhibits A–C). Page numbers survive in the strip. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov.
- Register: standard US private-equity drafting: "shall" throughout, long single-sentence clauses, defined capitalised terms, majority-in-interest thresholds computed on an as-converted basis (Section 1.2). Rights run asymmetrically: the sponsor's investors are free to transfer and hold the ROFR, board majority, drag and veto; one named management holder (the former controlling owner, inferred) gets his own drag after a waiting period, a board seat above an ownership threshold, pre-emption and amendment vetoes; the other management holders only bear restrictions. Drafting slips: the IPO definition uses "the Act" before "Securities Act" is defined; 4.5 refers to an undefined "Purchase Agreement" (the recitals name an "Exchange Agreement"); the certificate legend calls it a "Restricted Stockholders Agreement"; "Transferring Shareholder" for "Transferring Stockholder"; "binding upon and notice to the benefit" in 6.10.
- Recurring elements: "majority-in-interest of the Investors" as the consent and decision threshold; the as-converted share count of Section 1.2 reused for every pro rata fraction; the three-part transfer condition (Securities Act exemption, no Exchange Act reporting trigger, signed joinder) repeated in 2.1(a), 2.1(b) and 2.7; an irrevocable proxy or power of attorney backing each voting and drag undertaking; "deemed to lapse … in violation" for sales not closed in time.
- Stage: vc (a private-equity sponsor holding Series A convertible participating preferred stock, with management common holders); the candidate row said `early`, corrected from the text. Not a founder-formation agreement: it follows a sponsor recapitalisation of an existing insurance business.
- Parties: the company; one investor entity (a sponsor-managed LLC) under the "Investors" group; nine management common holders (individuals, their revocable trusts and one LLC); an "Additional Common Stockholders" group with no signatories yet, joined later by joinder.
- Status: signed exhibit (conformed /s/ signatures with holders' home addresses); the Additional Common Stockholders signature page is empty; joinder forms blank.
- Companion documents named: the stock contribution (exchange) agreement, which this agreement is a condition of and from which "Material Adverse Effect" is taken; the Charter (liquidation preference, makewhole amount, conversion price and dividend rate, "Public Offering" definition) and Bylaws (director exculpation and indemnification); any stock restriction agreement carrying repurchase rights on restricted shares, which transferees must also sign; option and stock plans; an undefined "Purchase Agreement" whose sections govern charter-amendment votes.
- Absent: founder-style vesting and leaver terms (repurchase on departure left to separate stock restriction agreements); roles, time commitment and employment terms; IP assignment and confidentiality; non-compete and non-solicit (only a ban on selling shares to competitors); deadlock mechanism; reserved matters or a decision list beyond board composition and meeting frequency; information rights; dividend policy; registration rights (only an IPO market stand-off); dispute resolution by arbitration.
- Size: 8,988 words from the preamble to "[Signature Pages Follow]"; 9,645 in the stripped text with signature pages and the three joinders.
