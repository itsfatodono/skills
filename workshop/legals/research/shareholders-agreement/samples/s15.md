## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-10.31 to the filer's 10-Q, accession 0001580695-21-000020, the filer's own document served from sec.gov) of a 2021 founding shareholders' agreement for a Swiss stock corporation (AG) still in formation, set up by a Nasdaq-listed US investor (the filer) and an individual Swiss founder to buy an operating software business abroad and roll up others in its sector. The governed company is private; the agreement ends on the first trading day after a qualified IPO. The text is itself "Appendix E" of a Founding Investment and Subscription Agreement that is not in the exhibit. Layout: cover page with title, date and a numbered party block (investor, founder, "Other Shareholders" described as future key people, two individual "Investment Directors", company); a table of contents with page numbers; a lettered preamble (A–F); eleven numbered sections with decimal sub-sections and run-in headings, down to four levels in Section 7 (7.4.1 …); "[Signature page to follow]"; signature page; list of appendices; Appendix E (defined terms, alphabetical); Appendix 2.a) (articles, "see attached", not filed); Appendix 4 (reserved matters, Parts A and B); Appendix 6.2 (anti-dilution formula, the equations held as an image, read and transcribed into the raw text). Page numbers survive in the strip. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov.
- Register: Swiss continental drafting in English: "shall" throughout, defined terms collected in an appendix, Swiss Code of Obligations articles cited by number, German legal terms in parentheses after the English ones. Mixes a formal shareholders' agreement with commercial deal terms (founder put schedule at an EBITDA multiple, payment in the investor's listed shares, a named valuation expert). Drafting slips: the party block defines only company and investor as "Parties" while the definitions add the founder; 7.5.1 and 7.6.1 refer to themselves for the notice form, 7.5.1 points to 7.4.3 for the tag-along period, 3.4 calls itself "Section 3.2"; 3.3's paragraphs are lettered (c)–(d); 10.8 says "including this Section 10.9"; the definitions point "Tag-Along Exercise Notice" to a non-existent 7.5.5 and "Confidential Information" to 10.2, which defines none; "Manager" is left as "[specify]"; "Issue Price" and "Investment Agreement" come undefined from the parent agreement; Section 8 deems a breach of 10.3 (assignment) material, maybe meaning 10.2.
- Recurring elements: "Founder Directors" and "Investor Directors" as the two voting blocks whose majorities decide approvals, the put schedule and the IPO effects; the company telling each shareholder within a fixed number of days when an exercise window ends; a "bona fide purchase offer" as the trigger and price setter for ROFR, tag and drag; "the procedure … shall be repeated" once a sale window lapses; "mutatis mutandis" pointers to the Investment Agreement (confidentiality, notices, reps and warranties, cost reimbursement, liquidation waterfall); Swiss German terms in parentheses (Einzelzeichnungsberechtigung, Kollektivzeichnungsberechtigung zu Zweien, Bezugsrecht, verwirkt, Konventionalstrafe, wichtiger Grund, einfache Gesellschaft, Verwaltungsratspräsident, Stammaktien).
- Stage: jv (one corporate investor holding the majority and one individual founder, with key people of the acquired business to join as "Other Shareholders", in a company at formation); the candidate row said `founder`, corrected from the text, since the parties are not founders only: a listed corporate investor holds the majority, controls reserved matters and gives the founder a put. Base: the governed company is Swiss (Zurich), as the row says.
- Parties: the company (in formation); one corporate investor (the filer); one individual founder; "Other Shareholders" as a class of future key people, executives and directors, joining by accession; two individuals named as the investor's directors.
- Status: signed exhibit: the founder's conformed /s/ signature and the investor's CEO's signature as an image; no signature lines for the company, the Investment Directors or Other Shareholders; Appendix C (cap table) is listed but not filed, and Appendix 2.a) (articles) reads "(see attached)" with nothing attached.
- Companion documents named: the Founding Investment and Subscription Agreement and the "Investment Agreement" (Closing Date, Issue Price, Subscription Amount, reps, warranties and indemnities applied to drag sales, confidentiality, notices, cost reimbursement, and the liquidation waterfall in its Appendix 1.6, used to split drag and liquidation proceeds); the articles of incorporation (Appendix 2.a); a cap table (Appendix C); each shareholder's employment agreement with the company (leaver triggers); an ESOP; board regulations (CEO appointment).
- Absent: founder vesting schedule (the leaver call option, priced by good or bad leaver status, stands in); IP assignment to the company (IP appears only as a reserved matter); non-compete and non-solicit; roles and time commitment; a deadlock mechanism beyond a re-convened quorum and a chairman's casting vote after founder dilution; the confidentiality and notice texts themselves (pointers to the Investment Agreement); arbitration (state courts).
