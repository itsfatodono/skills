## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-4.2, marked "Execution Version", filed with the holding company's 2013 registration statement) of an agreement signed in 2011 while the company was private. A centred title, a one-paragraph preamble naming the parties with defined labels, a "RECITALS" heading over three "WHEREAS" recitals and a "NOW, THEREFORE" formula, fifteen numbered sections with bold run-in headings and lettered sub-clauses (a., b., ...; board composition as numbered items (1)-(7)), a "[Remainder of page intentionally left blank; signature page to follow]" line, four signature pages, and Schedule 1 (the seller holders with addresses). No article level, no table of contents; the definitions come near the end (Section 14), not at the start. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: US private-equity deal English: long sentences, defined terms in quotation marks in parentheses mid-sentence, "his, her or its" throughout, "provided, that" and "provided further" chains, capitalised certificate legends. Numbers mostly as figures, once in words with the figure in parentheses. Drafting slips: "Adam's" for the holder's possessive; "Section 5(b)" where the rest of the text writes "5.b"; an undefined "such Seller" in 7.e; the "Independent Third Party" definition stops without a closing period; the governing-law clause repeats the specific-performance sentence of 15.f; the agreement legend leaves the day of the date blank.
- Recurring elements: the same sunset, "the first to occur of (i) an Initial Public Offering or (ii) a Sale of the Company", closes Sections 1, 3 and 4; the seller holder's rights (board seat, post-IPO nomination, amendment veto) all hang on keeping a fixed ownership percentage; the sponsor's holding vehicle decides alone or with the board (board size, drag-along, amendments); every non-public transferee signs a counterpart; "Initial Public Offering" and "Public Sale" are carved out of the transfer rules.
- Stage: jv (corrected from `early` in the candidate row). The holders are a private-equity sponsor's holding vehicle, which controls the company and its board, and the owner-managers of an acquired trucking business (two individuals and one family trust) who took the company's common stock as part of the merger price; there are no founders of the company, no angel or seed holders and no preferred stock, so the sample is a few-owner agreement over one joint holding company, borderline with `early`. Rule 6: a contract among the shareholders of a then-private company with the company as a party, setting board composition, tag-along, drag-along, transfer limits and exit; it was signed at the closing of the merger and starts before any IPO. Sections 1, 3 and 4 end at an IPO or sale of the company; Section 2 (a post-IPO nomination) and the piggyback registration block (Sections 5-11, ending a fixed period after the IPO) run past it. The registration block sits inside a shareholders' agreement, not as a standalone registration rights agreement, and the holders do not undertake to vote for the merger, so it is not an M&A support agreement. In.
- Parties: the company (a Minnesota holding corporation); the sponsor's holding vehicle (a Delaware LLC); the seller holders listed on Schedule 1 (two individuals, one also signing as trustee of a family trust); the merger subsidiary signs solely for the fee ban in 1.c. The company's CEO who signs for it is a principal of the sponsor and also signs for the sponsor vehicle.
- Status: signed exhibit: conformed "/s/" signatures for every party.
- Companion documents named: the merger agreement (all capitalised undefined terms take its meaning; it holds the forfeiture or cancellation of the sellers' shares that the second legend points to, and the period after which that legend comes off); the articles of incorporation and bylaws (limit on contribution in 10.d); underwriting agreements and questionnaires for registered sales (Section 11). No investors' rights or registration rights agreement: those rights are inside this text.
- Absent: founder share vesting and leaver buy-back; founder roles and time commitment; IP assignment and confidentiality; non-compete or non-solicit; reserved matters or other decision rules beyond board composition and the sponsor's approvals; deadlock; right of first refusal and pre-emptive rights on new issues; information rights; spousal consent.
- Size: 8,291 words in the stripped exhibit (about 3,290 of them in the registration block, Sections 5-11; 230 in the signature pages; 110 in Schedule 1).
