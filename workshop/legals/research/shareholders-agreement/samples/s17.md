## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-4.3, "Form of Shareholders Agreement", filed with the French company's 2015 US registration) of an agreement among all shareholders of a then-private French société anonyme, signed around 2008-2009. Underlined centred title; a "BETWEEN:" block whose shareholder list is replaced by an "OMISSIS" note; two lettered WHEREAS recitals and "IT IS HEREBY AGREED AS FOLLOWS"; four upper-case Titles (I Purpose and definitions, II transfers, III management, IV application) over seventeen Articles numbered 1 to 16 (12 used twice) with short headings ("Article 4 - Tag along"); only Article 8 has numbered sub-clauses (8.1, 8.2), Articles 8.2 and 10 have lettered lists. Ends with an "OMISSIS" note in place of the signature list and a three-column table for a new shareholder's name, place and date, and signature. Page footers "Advanced Accelerator Applications n of 7". No table of contents, no schedules besides the signature table. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: plain English drafted by a French (and partly Italian) team: short articles, civil-law habits (Titles over Articles, "registered letter return receipt requested", "deed of adherence", waiver of a Civil Code notice, community-property clause, a named commercial court), defined terms bold-italic in quotation marks in parentheses. Numbers as figures, once in words with the figure in parentheses. Drafting slips: two Articles numbered 12; "other that", "more then", "Infra Group" for intra-group, "shaeholder", "and and", "shall been signed"; Article 6 exempts intra-group transfers from "paragraph 4 above" (the tag-along) only; Title I is headed "Purpose – Definitions" but puts definitions first; the community-property sentence speaks of "each manager" although the parties are shareholders; the deed of adherence the text calls "attached" is only a signature table.
- Recurring elements: every transfer right is built on top of the pre-emption right in a numbered paragraph of the company's by-laws (its notice and exercise period are reused for the tag-along and drag-along); third-party transferees must sign the deed of adherence; the named founder holds the management post, can trigger the drag-along alone, and countersigns every shareholder's copy under a power of attorney.
- Stage: early (corrected from `vc` in the candidate row). The text shows one named founder as chief executive and director, and a body of shareholders large and scattered enough that they sign separately by post and the agreement only takes effect when holders of a fixed share of the capital have signed; it names no preferred shares, no investor class, no investor board seats and no investor consent rights, and gives information rights to every shareholder alike. Borderline: the omitted shareholder list may include funds. Rule 6: a contract among the shareholders of a private company (the company is not a party) about transfers, management and non-compete; Article 11 ends it automatically on listing, so it is a pre-IPO agreement that ends at the IPO. In.
- Parties: the company's shareholders only (list omitted in the filed copy), individuals and possibly companies (the intra-group clause); the company is described in the recitals but is not a party, although Article 10 puts reporting duties on it.
- Status: form as filed: the operative terms, dates and thresholds are filled in, the shareholder list and the signatures are removed ("OMISSIS").
- Companion documents named: the company's by-laws (the pre-emption right, paragraph 10.3, which governs the transfer procedure and its notice period; the managing director's powers); "any previous shareholders agreement", superseded on entry into force; a power of attorney each shareholder gives the founder for signing.
- Absent: founder share vesting and leaver buy-back; founder roles and time commitment beyond the one management appointment; IP assignment and confidentiality; non-solicit; reserved matters or other decision rules; deadlock; pre-emption on new issues; dispute resolution beyond amicable talks and a court; spousal consent (only a best-efforts community-property clause); exit beyond the drag-along.
- Size: 2,430 words in the stripped exhibit (no schedules; the signature table is a few words).
