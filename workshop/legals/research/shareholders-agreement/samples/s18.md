## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-10.2 to an SB-2 registration filed in 2006) of a 2002 shareholders agreement of a then-private company. Upper-case title, "made as of" date, party block in the Canadian "OF THE FIRST PART" style, one WHEREAS recital and a "NOW THEREFORE THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH" formula; eleven upper-case Articles with two-level numbered Sections ("3.1 Business Management and Corporate Action") and lettered and roman sub-clauses; the reserved-matters list carries italic group subheadings. Two small tables (cap table, officers). Page numbers "Page n" in the text; "///" filler lines before the signature page. No table of contents (although 1.3 mentions one), no schedules or exhibits. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: Canadian solicitor's precedent adapted for a Nevada corporation run from California: "attorn", "enure", "amalgamation", "cheque", "Vendor/Purchaser", "Time of Closing", "save and except", Canadian Income Tax Act s. 116 clearance; long defined-term sentences, numbers as figures. Many drafting slips: "Income Tax Act" defined as the US Internal Revenue Code yet the sale article cites the Canadian Act; undefined or mismatched terms ("Offer Period", "Offered Shares", "Option Period" reused, "Offer to Purchase", "Auditor" beside "Accountants"); one shareholder named differently in the cap table; "the later of" with a single date; typos ("statue", "bourne", "often days", "and arbitrator"); the party block names the holding company, while the definitions make the operating company the "Corporation".
- Recurring elements: Extraordinary Resolution (board and shareholder supermajority) as the gate for most decisions; a Capitalization Date (repayment of the lender-shareholder's loan) that switches board control and the investor veto; one Fair Market Value article and one common sale-closing article serve every buy-out mechanism; each holding-company shareholder is tied to its individual "Principal", whose death, incapacity or departure counts as the shareholder's.
- Stage: early. Three operator-shareholders (two through holding companies), a minority shareholder who is president, and one investor-lender holding the largest stake, at or near the company's start (the company "has been formed" for the business; an asset purchase, a loan agreement and option agreements are signed the same day). Common shares only; no preferred stock or VC terms. Base: US kept (Nevada corporation, offices and governing law in California), but most shareholders sit in Canada and the drafting is Canadian. Rule 6: a contract among all shareholders of a private company, with the company as a party, on governance, financing, transfers and exit; it ends only when one holder owns all shares, with no IPO link. In.
- Parties: five shareholders (three companies, two individuals) and the company.
- Status: signed exhibit (conformed "/s/" signatures, witness lines blank, the shareholders' notice addresses and phone numbers left in).
- Companion documents named: the company's articles and by-laws (to be kept consistent, with the agreement prevailing); the Loan Agreement with the investor-shareholder (loan terms and covenants left to it); the asset purchase agreement with a vendor loan to a founder; Option Agreements between shareholders (transfers under them pre-cleared); a founder's employment agreement (incorporated by reference for the company guarantee); employment generally (the leaver triggers turn on it).
