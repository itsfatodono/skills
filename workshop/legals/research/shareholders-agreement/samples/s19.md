## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-10.7, filed with an amendment (F-1/A) to the issuer's 2019 registration statement) of an agreement signed shortly before the IPO. A cover page (title, "by and among", the parties, the date), a two-level table of contents with page numbers, a numbered party list as preamble, lettered recitals with a "NOW, THEREFORE" formula, eighteen numbered sections in capitals ("SECTION 2 / ISSUANCE OF EQUITY SECURITIES") with decimal sub-clauses carrying bold run-in headings (2.1 Pre-Emptive Right) and lettered and roman items below, a "[the remainder of this page is intentionally left blank]" line, signature pages, Schedule A (holders, holdings and votes) and Exhibit A (form of deed of adherence). Definitions first (Section 1). Nearly every pre-IPO section closes with its own "Termination" sub-clause. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: Hong Kong and PRC cross-border deal English in the English-law style: "Disposal" and "Dispose" rather than transfer, "procure", "best endeavours", "Deed of Adherence", "save as", British spelling mixed with US ("favourable" and "favorable"); long sentences with defined terms in quotation marks in parentheses; numbers in words with the figure in parentheses; a US-style registration rights block (Sections 9-13) bolted on in US register. Drafting slips: numbering skips 7.3 and 17.8 is missing from the table of contents; 3.8 opens a parenthesis it never closes; 4.2(e) breaks off at "the ultimate beneficial"; 9.2 says "no limit ... under this Section 9.1"; 13.1(c) reuses (a)-(c) inside item (c); one address line holds an e-mail address; the deed's recital dates the agreement differently from the cover.
- Recurring elements: "Termination ... upon completion of an IPO" closes Sections 2-8; "Prior to an IPO" opens the minority protections in Section 6; the two holder groups (the controlling holding companies and the co-investors) get mirror-image but unequal transfer clauses; pro rata allocation in rounds with a stated cap per acceptor recurs in pre-emption and both first-refusal clauses, each with a board power to round fractions; fixed periods in business days, Hong Kong and PRC bank days; regulatory-approval extensions; new holders sign the deed of adherence; the co-investors' entry price per share is the floor for two protections (4.4 and 6.3).
- Stage: vc (corrected from `jv` in the candidate row). The holders are two holding companies of one corporate group, which own the high-vote class and control the company, and nine financial investors (venture and private-equity funds and investment vehicles) holding a minority of ordinary low-vote shares after swapping their stake in an upstream company; there is no joint venture between independent operating owners and no founder. The investors hold ordinary, not preferred, shares, so the sample is borderline between `vc` and `jv`; its content (pre-emption, first refusal, tag-along, information and audit rights, observer seat, buy-back and down-round consents, registration rights) is an investor-protection package. Rule 6: a contract among the shareholders of a then-private Hong Kong company, with the company as a party, setting how they issue, transfer and exit their shares; it is effective on signing before the IPO, and Sections 2-8 end at the IPO, while the registration rights (Sections 9-13) start after it. Registration rights inside a shareholders' agreement, not a standalone registration rights agreement; no undertaking to vote for a merger. In.
- Parties: the company (a Hong Kong limited company, the IPO issuer); two holding companies of its parent group (one Hong Kong, one Swiss); nine co-investors listed on Schedule A (fund partnerships and investment companies, several in one fund family).
- Status: signed exhibit: conformed "/s/" signatures for every party; the Exhibit A deed is a blank form with bracketed blanks.
- Companion documents named: the share exchange agreement (completion, the swap; the agreement is its condition precedent); the company's amended and restated articles (share classes; this agreement prevails, and the articles must be conformed); the earlier subscription and shareholders' agreement over the upstream company (named articles terminated by 14.3, rights waived by 3.7) and that company's articles; a cooperation agreement term sheet with the parent group (carved out of the related-party clause); the equity incentive plan; underwriting agreements (lock-up length, indemnity priority); the deed of adherence (Exhibit A).
- Absent: founder share vesting and good/bad leaver buy-back (only a compulsory sale offer on an investor's bankruptcy or financial failure); founder roles and time commitment; IP assignment; non-compete and non-solicit; board composition and appointment rights (only one investor's observer seat); voting covenant; reserved matters beyond buy-back and down-round consents; deadlock; put or call options; spousal consent.
- Size: 19,565 words in the stripped exhibit (about 570 of them in the cover and table of contents; about 5,460 in the registration block, Sections 9-13; 450 in notices and addresses; 270 in the signature pages; 190 in Schedule A; 480 in Exhibit A).
