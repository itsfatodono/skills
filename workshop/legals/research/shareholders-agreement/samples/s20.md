## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-10.9, filed with the company's 2006 US registration) of a second amended and restated voting agreement of a then-private California corporation, signed in 2005, with a later amendment appended in the same exhibit. Bold centred title; a one-paragraph preamble naming the company, each preferred series and the common holders, with defined terms in quotation marks in parentheses; four "WHEREAS" recitals under "WITNESSETH:" and a "NOW, THEREFORE" line; 23 numbered sections with short run-in headings ("6. Bring Along Right."), lettered sub-clauses only in Sections 3, 6 and 13; no definitions article (terms are defined where first used). Then the signature block over many "SIGNATURE PAGE TO ..." pages, Schedule A (investors with notice addresses), Schedule B (common holders), Exhibit A (Adoption Agreement), and "Amendment Number One" with its own recitals, restated sections and signature pages. EDGAR "QuickLinks" navigation at the top and a link list at the end. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: standard US venture-financing boilerplate of the NVCA era: long sentences, "hereby", "hereto", "provided, however", numbers in words with the figure in parentheses. Drafting slips: Section 11 speaks of "Investor Stock" and "Common Holder Stock" where the defined terms are "Investor Shares" and "Common Holder Shares", and points to Section 5 for the copy-on-request duty it itself sets; Section 23 uses "New Investors" and "Existing Investors", which are never defined; the preamble defines one series as "Series D Preferred" and the next clause calls it "Series D Stock"; the arbitration clause only covers claims "relating to the termination of this Agreement" and runs two sentences together with a comma; Exhibit A cites the agreement without "Second" and with a blank day; the drag-along article carries a no-voting-trust covenant as its sub-clause (e); several signature pages repeat a blank investor block from the template.
- Recurring elements: every voting duty is tied to the board seats the company's articles create, with the agreement fixing who nominates each seat; voting runs "at a regular or special meeting of shareholders (or by written consent)" in every operative clause; the agreement binds transferees through a certificate legend plus a signed Adoption Agreement; amendments need the company, a majority of all holders and a majority of one lead series.
- Stage: vc (as in the candidate row). The parties are the company, holders of several series of preferred stock (venture funds, a corporate investor, individuals) and a small group of common holders; the recitals tie it to a new preferred round. Rule 6: a contract among the shareholders of a private company, with the company as a party, about how they vote their shares for the board and on a sale; Section 14 ends it on the company's first registered public offering (or a liquidation event, or a fixed date), so it is a pre-IPO agreement that ends at the IPO. The drag-along is a standing clause, not support for one named merger. In.
- Parties: the company, the preferred holders listed on Schedule A ("Investors"), and the common holders listed on Schedule B ("Common Holders"), three individuals one of whom signs for the company as chief executive; the text never calls them founders.
- Status: signed exhibit (conformed "/s/" signatures; some signature pages left blank).
- Companion documents named: the company's amended and restated articles of incorporation (which create the board seats and define the liquidation event and the order in which sale proceeds are distributed); the bylaws (director removal); the series F preferred stock purchase agreement it is signed to induce; the prior amended and restated voting agreement it supersedes; the Adoption Agreement in Exhibit A; "any other agreement" imposing transfer restrictions.
- Absent: founder share vesting and leaver buy-back; founder roles and time commitment; IP assignment and confidentiality; non-compete and non-solicit; deadlock; reserved matters or other decision rules beyond board composition; transfer restrictions (no right of first refusal, tag-along or lock-up; only the transferee joinder); pre-emption on new issues; exit beyond the drag-along; spousal consent.
- Size: 7,448 words in the stripped exhibit; about 3,600 for the agreement up to the signature block, about 190 for Exhibit A, about 620 for the amendment; the rest is signature pages and schedules.
