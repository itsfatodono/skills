## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-10.19, filed with the company's 2019 US registration) of a signed shareholders agreement of a then-private Cayman holding company with a PRC operating group held through a VIE structure. Cover page listing the parties in English with Chinese names in brackets, a contents table, a one-paragraph preamble, four lettered recitals and a "NOW, THEREFORE" line; 11 numbered articles in capitals with decimal sub-sections and run-in headings ("4.3 Right of First Refusal."), lettered and roman sub-clauses; definitions moved to Schedule B. Then signature pages by party group, Schedule A (heading only), Schedule B (definitions), Schedule C (registration rights, about 6,700 words with its own definitions), and Exhibit A (form deed of adherence); Exhibit B (notices) is listed but not filed. Page numbers survive as bare lines in the stripped text. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: US venture-financing boilerplate (NVCA pre-emption, ROFR and co-sale, registration rights) adapted to a Cayman/PRC "red-chip" deal and written by non-native drafters: long sentences, numbers in words with the figure in parentheses, "Group Companies" throughout, and two PRC-practice clauses (a profit-target compensation clause and a founder put) drafted as raw formulas with the deal's figures in the text. Drafting slips: "PREFRENCE" in the contents; "the PRC generally acceptable accounting principles"; Section 5.1(a) says "in either case" with only one case; Section 9.4 "Series A Principle Put Price"; Section 9 uses redemption wording ("out of funds legally available therefor including capital") for a purchase by the founders; Section 11.11 ranks the agreement against an undefined "Framework Agreement"; Schedule B points "Compensation Committee" to Section 1.4, which never uses it, and never defines "Group Companies"; Section 11.5 and 11.6 both exclude third-party rights.
- Recurring elements: nearly every investor right ends "upon the Qualified Initial Public Offering", a term the agreement leaves to the memorandum and articles; board-level carve-outs need a fixed supermajority "including the affirmative vote of the Series A Director"; the founders' obligations are joint and several and reach their holdcos (look-through on holdco transfers, founder guarantee of holdco performance); every transfer or assignment needs the Exhibit A deed of adherence; values and periods are fixed in the text, not left to the board.
- Stage: vc (candidate row said early). The investors hold Series A preferred shares (one is a venture capital company) bought from a founder and a founder vehicle under two share purchase agreements, and the agreement gives them a preferred-round package (pre-emption, ROFR and co-sale, reserved matters, a board seat, a liquidation preference, a put and registration rights). Rule 6: a contract among the shareholders of one private company (founders, their holdcos and the two investors) with the company and its subsidiaries as parties, on how shares are held, governed, transferred and exited; information, participation, transfer, protective and liquidation provisions each end at the qualified IPO, and the put runs only if there is no IPO by a deadline, so it is a pre-IPO agreement that ends at the IPO. Schedule C's registration rights live inside it and run after the IPO, but they are one part of a wider shareholders agreement, not a standalone registration rights agreement. In.
- Parties: the Cayman company, its BVI, Hong Kong and PRC (WFOE) subsidiaries, two PRC domestic operating or holding companies, two individual founders, the founders' wholly owned holdcos, a founder-held BVI investment vehicle, and two PRC investors (a limited partnership and a venture capital company); about twelve signatories.
- Status: signed exhibit (conformed "/s/" signatures; founders' identification numbers left as blank brackets; Schedule A and Exhibit B not filed).
- Companion documents named: the two amended and restated share sale and purchase agreements (which define undefined terms and the "Transaction Documents"); the amended and restated memorandum and articles (board size, class meetings, the qualified IPO definition, which the agreement overrides between shareholders); the amended and restated VIE agreements; separate NDAs and non-competes the founders must have key staff sign; an underwriting agreement (prevails on registration indemnities); the ESOP; an undefined "Framework Agreement".
- Absent: founder share vesting and leaver or bad-leaver buy-back (only a carve-out for company repurchases on termination of employment); founder roles, time commitment and service terms; IP assignment to the company and confidentiality of company information (Section 6 covers only the deal terms); deadlock; drag-along; decision rules among the founders themselves; spousal consent; founder exit other than the investor put. Has a founder non-compete and non-solicit.
- Size: 19,489 words in the stripped exhibit; about 10,500 for the agreement up to the signature block, about 440 for the signature pages, about 1,300 for Schedule B, about 6,700 for Schedule C, about 480 for Exhibit A.
