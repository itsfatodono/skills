## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-4.2, plain-text filing to the filer's 2005 registration statement) of an agreement dated 2003, when the company was private. A one-paragraph preamble, three lettered recitals under "WITNESSETH", five articles in capitals (stock certificates; transfer rights and restrictions; corporate governance; term and amendment; miscellaneous) with two-level decimal sections (1.01, 2.05) and lettered sub-clauses, a company signature page, then nine one-page stockholder signature pages, each a short acknowledgement of being bound. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: US small-company venture drafting ("shall", "hereto", "NOW, THEREFORE"), defined terms in quotes and parentheses at first use, numbers written in words with numerals in parentheses; several drafting slips left in ("to the pursuant to", "are are", "all not apply", "if there is a vacancy occurs") and loose defined terms (the text switches between "Offered Stock", "Offered Shares", "Stock" and "Shares" for the same thing; 2.03 calls the right of first refusal "this Section 2.04").
- Recurring elements: "Capital Stock" defined broadly in the recitals (present and later-acquired shares and convertible securities) and used in every operative clause; references to Exhibit "A" with the letter in quotes; Article II carve-outs that point to each other (2.02 excludes the Compelled Sale, 2.04 excludes 2.05).
- Stage: vc (Series A convertible preferred holders, individuals, family trusts, LLCs and a corporation, with the company); matches the candidate row. The candidate's *Document* cell names common holders as parties; the text binds only the company and the Series A holders, and reaches later holders through 1.03 and 5.02.
- Parties: the company and the Series A preferred holders listed on Exhibit A (individuals, trusts, an investment LLC, a corporation); no founder is named as such.
- Status: signed exhibit (conformed /s/ signatures on the company page and each stockholder page); Exhibit A (the holder list) is not included in the filing.
- Companion documents named: the Series A Convertible Preferred Stock Purchase Agreement (records holdings; the agreement is entered into under it); the Certificate of Designations of the Series A Preferred Stock (Series A voting rights and class vote on Series A director vacancies); the Bylaws (appointment of two common-stock director seats); the instruments granting options (whether options accelerate in a drag-along); Exhibit A (holder list and notice addresses).
