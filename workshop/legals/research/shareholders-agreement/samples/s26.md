## Document
- Shape: EDGAR exhibit (EX-4.3, filed with the company's 2012 US registration) of a signed amended and restated stockholders agreement of a then-private Delaware parent with an Ontario operating subsidiary held through exchangeable shares and a voting trust (the agreement names the company Eloqua Limited, not the filer's name Eloqua, Inc.). Title under "Exhibit 4.3" and "Execution Copy", a one-paragraph preamble, three WHEREAS recitals and a "NOW, THEREFORE" line; seven articles with Roman numerals and capital headings, decimal sections with run-in headings ("3.6 Drag-Along Rights."), lettered and roman sub-clauses; no contents table. Then signature and counterpart pages, Annex I (instrument of adherence form), Schedule I (purchasers and existing holders), Schedule III (securities-law exchange certificate), and a later amendment with its own signature pages, filed in the same exhibit. Schedule II is never referred to or filed. A running footer "Stockholders Agreement — Page N" survives as bare lines in the stripped text. Version taken: the exhibit as filed on sec.gov, the filer's own document.
- Register: US venture-financing boilerplate (voting agreement for board seats, ROFR and co-sale, drag-along, lock-up) on a Canadian founders' base: Canadian spellings ("endeavour"), Ontario-practice clauses (statutory class votes on exchangeable shares, a family-law call event, an oppression-remedy waiver, independent legal advice, time of the essence, no partnership). Long sentences; every majority spelt out with the voting-trust vote count. Drafting slips: two clause (iii)s in "Offeree"; 6.1 non-solicit items hang from an unfinished lead-in; 7.2(b) points to the legend "in Section 7.3" (it is 7.4); 7.7 adds new parties "to Annex I", which is the adherence form; "United Stated dollars"; Schedule III calls the subsidiary's exchangeable shares shares "in the capital of US Co."; the s. 240 OBCA cite for the oppression remedy; in the amendment the new 2.2(f) is quoted as "(e)" and item 4 is missing.
- Recurring elements: the voting trustee runs through every vote ("or direct the Voting Trustee to vote"); thresholds are fixed share counts or class majorities in the text; obligations fall on "Existing Stockholders" (founders, employees, small holders) and benefits on the "Purchasers" and "Offerees" (lead funds plus the named founders); the Instrument of Adherence for every transferee and new holder; ROFR and co-sale each end at a registered public offering, the whole agreement at a qualified IPO or cash sale.
- Stage: vc (as the candidate row says). Holders of three series of preferred stock (venture funds) with one board seat each, a preferred-round package (ROFR, co-sale, drag-along, lock-up) and preferred-majority waivers. Rule 6: a contract among the stockholders of one private company (founders, other existing holders, the purchasers and a voting trustee) with the company and its subsidiary as parties, on how shares are voted, transferred, called and sold; it ends just before a qualified IPO or a cash or freely-tradable merger (only the lock-up survives), so it is a pre-IPO agreement that ends at the IPO. In.
- Parties: the Delaware company, its Ontario subsidiary, a corporate voting trustee, the venture fund purchasers (three fund families, two entities each), four named Major Holders, and several dozen other existing holders on Schedule I; the amendment is signed by the company, funds, two Major Holders, a few existing holders, the subsidiary and the trustee.
- Status: signed exhibit (conformed "/s/" signatures; one Major Holder's line unsigned; Annex I and Schedule III are blank forms; only the signers' pages are filed, not one per Schedule I holder).
- Companion documents named: the prior stockholders agreement (ended by 7.20); the Series C preferred stock purchase agreement (notice addresses); the certificate of incorporation (board election rights of preferred, the "Deemed Liquidation Event" used as "Sale Event", liquidation preferences); the voting trust agreement (how exchangeable holders vote through the trustee); the subsidiary's own shareholders agreement and its exchangeable share provisions; a separate underwriters' lock-up agreement. Vesting, IP and employment terms are left to other documents, none named.
- Absent: founder share vesting schedule and good or bad leaver pricing (the Article V call takes all shares at fair value on leaving or joining a competitor, founders exempt from the leaving trigger); founder roles, time commitment and service terms; IP assignment to the company; deadlock; decision rules among the founders and reserved matters (left to the charter); dispute resolution and forum; spousal consent. Has transfer restrictions (ROFR, co-sale, drag-along, permitted transfers), a non-compete and non-solicit, a one-sentence confidentiality clause, a call right and an exit clause.
- Size: 13,488 words in the stripped exhibit; about 10,150 for the agreement to 7.20, about 260 for the signature pages, about 150 for Annex I, about 350 for Schedule I, about 1,500 for Schedule III, about 1,070 for the amendment with its signatures.
