## Document
- Shape: cover page, a one-page explanatory note on the assumptions (Singapore private company, founders the only holders of ordinary shares, separate employment or services and IP assignment agreements, executed as a deed, a fuller shareholders' agreement once investors join), a table of contents, then 13 numbered clauses, two schedules and the signature pages. Nineteen drafting notes sit in footnotes, each telling the drafter what to decide or where to get advice. Version taken: "(Revised) (clean)" docx served from SVCA's domain; SVCA's live *Model legal documents* page lists "Founders' Agreement (NEW)" under Pre-Series A documents, without a link on that entry.
- Register: formal English drafting ("shall", "save as", "inter se"), capitalised defined terms, long single-sentence clauses; deal values left as `[●]` blanks, with suggested defaults in square brackets ("[fourteen (14)] days").
- Recurring elements: square-bracket blanks and bracketed default numbers; `[OR]` / `[OPTION 1]`, `[OPTION 2]` alternatives; cross-references to parts of Schedule 1, where each founder's deal terms go; "Drafting Note:" footnotes pointing to legal, tax or financial advice and to other VIMA models (Series A Shareholders' Agreement, Deed of Assignment of IP).
- Stage: model (written for the founder stage, before any outside financing).
- Parties: the founders (listed in Schedule 1 Part A, three signature blocks given) and the company.
- Status: blank model.
- Companion documents named: the Constitution (the agreement prevails between the founders and they must amend the Constitution to match); each founder's employment or services agreement (role, remuneration, IP and confidentiality terms); a separate IP assignment agreement for existing IP; the VIMA Series A Shareholders' Agreement for when investors join (pre-emption, tag-along, drag-along, reserved matters are left to it); the deed of ratification and accession (Schedule 2).
- Absent: a vesting schedule (only a drafting note proposing reverse vesting with a cliff); the leaver purchase terms themselves (Clauses 6.4 and 6.5 are `[●]` placeholders); a list of reserved matters or shareholder decision thresholds (left to Schedule 1 Part C); pre-emption on new shares, right of first refusal, tag-along and drag-along; an exit or sale process; a lock-in period (suggested in a note only); founder warranties; information rights; dividend policy; funding obligations beyond the purchase price; death or incapacity of a founder.
- Size: 10,325 words (about 9,500 in the body, about 830 in the drafting notes).
