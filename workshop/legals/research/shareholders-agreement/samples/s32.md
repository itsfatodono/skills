## Document
- Shape: a title, one recital paragraph, four numbered paragraphs, two unnumbered closing paragraphs (a warranty and governing law), a dated signing line with no signature blocks, and a Creative Commons licence footer. Two pages.
- Version taken: the live `.doc` and `.pdf` links on Seedcamp's v2.0 page both return 404 (checked 2026-11-12). The Wayback `.doc` capture (2019-07-17) is listed in the CDX index but does not replay, so the text is the latest capture of the sibling `.pdf` linked from the same page (2025-12-15). All PDF captures from 2019 to 2025 have the same digest. The PDF metadata says Sept 2011 and the date line reads "201[1]". The text matches word for word Seedcamp's own "Founder Collaboration Agreement" (version 7) on Docracy (capture of 2017-12-10). The v2.0 page announces a vesting-schedule template and a broad non-compete. The text has only a blank vesting-schedule slot in paragraph 3 and no non-compete. The non-compete language credited to Phil Weiss appears only in his Docracy derivative, "The Revised Collaboration Agreement", which is not this sample. The `.doc` may have held a fuller v2.0 that could not be recovered.
- Purpose (from the linking page, seedcamp.com/views/seedhack-founders-collaboration-agreement-version-2-0, 13 Nov 2012): a tool to turn an informal team, typically formed at a hackathon, into a formal company. It is meant as a starting point for discussion, to be used as is or changed. The page explains reverse vesting and good and bad leavers at some length. The original was drafted by Brown Rudnick. The page lists hackathons that endorse it and disclaims liability, saying the form is not legal advice. Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.
- Register: formal English drafting ("shall", "absolutely with full title guarantee", "acts and deeds and things"), capitalised defined terms set in the recital, each numbered paragraph a single long sentence ending in ";" or "; and", so the four read as one list of agreed terms.
- Recurring elements: square-bracket blanks and alternatives (`[relating to [ ]]`, `Option 1:` / `Option 2:`, `[two, three, four]`, `[English]`); no drafting notes, no definitions clause, no clause headings.
- Stage: model (pre-incorporation: written for collaborators before the company exists, to fix what happens when it is formed).
- Parties: the individual collaborators only ("the undersigned"), any number; the future company is named as the beneficiary of the commitments, not as a party.
- Status: blank model.
- Companion documents named: none. The agreement presumes the future company will issue shares and may reserve a share option pool, but names no articles, subscription, vesting or IP assignment document. The assignment in paragraph 1 is an undertaking to do it later.
- Absent: roles, time commitment and duties; confidentiality; non-compete or non-solicit; decision rules and reserved matters; deadlock; board composition; transfer restrictions, pre-emption, right of first refusal and tag-along (only a drag-along); good and bad leaver classes and the price for returned shares; a vesting cliff or acceleration; what happens if the company is never formed or a collaborator leaves before formation; termination of the agreement; dispute resolution; notices and boilerplate; signature blocks.
- Size: 490 words (about 456 in the agreement, 34 in the licence footer).
