## Document
- Shape: cover page (title, update date, release notes, contributors, disclaimer), a table of contents, parties and a three-line introduction, 22 numbered clauses with numbered sub-clauses, a signature page with a personal accession undertaking under it, and three schedules (parties, cap table, adherence agreement). One illustrative table inside Clause 8 sets vested and unvested shares against good and bad leavers. Version taken: the "without investors" variant dated 9 March 2021 from the seriesseedfi GitHub repository that `seriesseedfi.github.io` links; the same repo holds two sister variants ("only small investors", "with vc lead investor") and the term sheets.
- What the form is for (linking page and repo README): SeriesSeed.fi is a free set of standardised Finnish startup documents (term sheet, shareholders' agreement, investment agreement), CC BY 3.0, written with input from both founders and investors and meant to be used as-is so that terms need little negotiation; version 3.0 (Nov 2020) was drafted with Bird & Bird and merged with the Nordic StartupTools template. The page itself still describes version 2.0 and says to seek independent advice.
- Register: formal English contract drafting ("shall", "mutatis mutandis", "save for"), capitalised defined terms, long sentences; Finnish legal terms glossed in brackets ("Fi. ositus", "Fi. yrityssaneeraus"). The 42 drafting notes are Word comments (none in footnotes or endnotes) in a very plain, joking second-person voice that restates each clause ("Simply put: Shut up!").
- Recurring elements: square-bracket blanks for names and deal values, and bracketed default numbers the drafter may change (Founder Majority above [75]%, a [2/3] financing vote, [four] board meetings, a [six]-year founder lock-in, a [50]% breach discount, [30] days to pay); many other numbers fixed in the text (vesting 25% cliff then 36 months, 14/30/45/90-day windows, 20% valuation gap, 90% amendment majority); "Founder Majority Consent" as the recurring approval and escape valve (board deviations from vesting, non-compete exceptions); cross-references to the 7.2 first-refusal and 9.2 redemption procedures reused mutatis mutandis. The notes tell the drafter what to choose in a few places only: keep the Business definition wide enough but not broad, treat the board size as a suggestion, negotiate the reserved-matters list, allow dedication exceptions for side projects; inline `[Note: …]` lines in the schedules say who goes in Part 1 or Part 2 and when the personal accession block is used; a bracketed choice between digital signing and paper originals.
- Stage: model (written for founders and key persons before any investor; sister variants cover investors).
- Parties: the company, the founders (Schedule 1 Part 1, possibly as holding companies), key persons (Schedule 1 Part 2, key employees and founders in person), and optional blocks for other individual or corporate shareholders; the individuals behind holding companies accede personally to the core obligations.
- Status: blank model.
- Companion documents named: the Articles (their first-refusal, post-sale purchase and consent clauses are switched off for compliant transfers; the agreement prevails); the Term Sheet on seriesseed.fi (explains the terms); a possible minority shareholders' agreement for later small holders; new shareholders', investment or subscription agreements on a later financing round (which holders must sign); the employee stock option scheme (its terms prevail over vesting); key persons' employment or service agreements (their termination triggers leaver status); a customary share purchase agreement for leaver buy-backs; the Adherence Agreement (Schedule 3).
- Absent: a deadlock mechanism; founder roles, titles or pay (only a full-time dedication duty); founder warranties; information rights; a separate IP assignment document or a list of pre-existing IP (the assignment sits in Clause 12); any funding duty (expressly excluded); a dispute step before arbitration.
- Size: 10,837 words (about 9,300 in the body, about 1,500 in the drafting notes).
