The notes tell the drafter what to choose: tailor the board seats to the deal and the charter, weigh how a founder seat can be held at the founder's pleasure (seat tied to employment or to share holdings), whether the board may fill vacancies under DGCL 223, which constituencies must approve a drag (and the fiduciary risk of adding the Board), to keep express appraisal and fiduciary-claim waivers, whether to keep a qualified-IPO termination trigger (advised against), who counts as a Key Holder, amend-and-restate versus terminate the prior agreement, JAMS or AAA arbitration versus courts, and a California judicial-reference backstop to the jury waiver.
- Stage: model (blank form for a preferred-stock financing; the vc stage).
- Parties: the company (a Delaware corporation), the Investors (Schedule A, preferred holders), the Key Holders (Schedule B; per the notes, founders and large common or option holders), and other common holders who join (a Schedule C the text names but the file does not include).
- Status: blank model.
- Companion documents named: the Restated Certificate (class and series board votes, protective provisions, Deemed Liquidation Event and Qualified Direct Listing definitions; the agreement must not contradict it); the Series Preferred Stock Purchase Agreement (closing condition; source of "Transaction Agreements" and "Side Letters"); a prior Voting Agreement it amends and restates; in the notes, the Investors' Rights Agreement and the Right of First Refusal and Co-Sale Agreement (termination should match them), the term sheet, the bylaws, and employee or consultant equity award agreements (should carry a matching drag and appraisal waiver or a joinder).
