## Document
- Shape: cover page and index, parties, four lettered recitals, then 30 numbered clauses (governance and investor rights first: board, information, consent matters, undertakings, exit; then founder covenants, confidentiality and the closing clauses) and five schedules (parties, two-tier consent matters, undertakings, a US PFIC statement, the adherence agreement), then alternative signature blocks. Thirty-two short drafting notes sit in Word footnotes, most saying "insert details" or "amend as appropriate"; the substantive ones tell the drafter to choose between restating a prior agreement and a fresh one (with a deed terminating the old one), to merge EIS and VCT manager definitions when one manager runs both, to consider a long-form registration rights agreement, to include the consideration clause only if signed under hand, to keep consent matters in the schedule, to include the US tax covenants and Schedule 4 only for US-taxed investors, to drop the ITEPA power of attorney unless the agreement is a deed, and to include the ESG undertakings as appropriate. Several notes say "see drafting notes", a separate BVCA guidance document not in this file; no endnotes or comments. Version taken: the February 2023 docx on bvca.co.uk ("02-23 - BVCA - Shareholders' Agreement"), read from the Wayback capture of 11 March 2023 (the URL above), because the current edition (February 2025) on the UK Private Capital site needs a member login.
- Register: formal English drafting ("save with", "procure", "if and in so far as it lies within their power"), capitalised defined terms with many borrowed from the Articles; deal values as `[***]` blanks, with bracketed defaults for periods ("[21] days", "[four] months", "[five] years") and bracketed alternatives joined by "/" or `OR`.
- Recurring elements: "[Major] Investors" brackets letting the drafter limit rights to larger holders; two consent levels (Investor Majority Consent, Investor Director Consent) used throughout; "No claim … without Investor Majority Consent" closing clauses 6, 7 and 10; cross-references to numbered Articles; bracketed optional modules for EIS/VCT, US tax and ESG; footnotes beginning "[Note:".
- Stage: vc (Series A model for a UK company with institutional investors, founders and optional existing holders).
- Parties: investors, founders, optional existing shareholders (each a list in Schedule 1) and the company.
- Status: blank model.
- Companion documents named: the Articles (director appointment, share rights, transfers and permitted transferees, leaver provisions, drag-along, lock-up, new holding company; the agreement prevails between shareholders); the Subscription Agreement (the investment, completion, option-plan undertakings, termination that unwinds this agreement); the Prior Agreement being amended and restated; the founders' Service Agreements; the Share Option Plan; a separate IP transfer agreement; the Adherence Agreement (Schedule 5); a BVCA model registration rights agreement; separate BVCA drafting notes.
- Absent: founder share vesting and leaver terms (left to the Articles; the agreement holds only a founder's waiver of claims and an option-vesting default in the consent matters); pre-emption, right of first refusal, tag-along and drag-along terms (left to the Articles); a deadlock mechanism; founder roles and time commitment (left to the Service Agreements); founder warranties (in the Subscription Agreement); death or incapacity of a founder; dividend policy beyond the consent right; funding obligations.
- Size: 16,616 words (16,272 in the body, 344 in the footnotes).
