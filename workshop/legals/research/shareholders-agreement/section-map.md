ids counts for both. `doc` marks a whole-document row (execution block, form licence footer,
cover page); `none` marks a row with no content (a deleted article). `reg-*` ids are regional content (`merge.md` *Regional content*). A remedy or a
completion step written inside one right maps to that right's id; a general clause maps to its own.
