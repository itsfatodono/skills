# subscription-terms: merged section list

Formats: `prose`, `list`, `table` (the only formats that can make a sample new), and the
layout marks `pointer` (links to another page) and `embedded` (folded into another section).
A sample's subscription part is a whole separate page or the subscription section(s) of its
ToS (`sources.md` rule 6); only that part is mapped.

## Section ids

Meanings fixed before judging (build step 3). Change only with a note below.

| Id | Title | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| scope | Scope and relation to other terms | what the part covers (products, plans, purchase types), the provider, incorporation into or precedence over the ToS, eligibility to buy, acceptance, non-transferability, definitions |
| plans | Plans and what they include | the paid tiers and billing periods offered, what each includes (features, perks, add-ons, lifetime plans), where prices are shown |
| payment | Payment and billing | payment methods, charge authorisation, when charges happen, currency, pre-authorisation holds, updating the payment method, card-updater services, how a purchase is concluded |
| auto-renewal | Automatic renewal | that the plan renews until cancelled, the renewal period and price, the cancel-before-renewal cut-off, renewal reminders |
| free-trial | Free trials | trial availability and eligibility, payment details at sign-up, conversion to paid, cancelling during the trial |
| promotions | Promotional and introductory offers | discounts, introductory prices, vouchers and referral credits; eligibility; the step-up to the full price; no stacking; no price protection |
| changes | Changes to prices, plans and these terms | the right to change prices, plans or the terms; notice period and channel; the user's remedy (cancel); deemed acceptance |
| cancellation | Cancellation | how and where to cancel, the deadline, when it takes effect, access until the period ends, deleting the app or account does not cancel, what happens to the account and data afterwards |
| refunds | Refunds | the refund rule (usually none), partial periods, discretionary refunds and credits, exceptions required by law (stated generally), where store refunds are requested |
| third-party-billing | Purchases through app stores and other third parties | purchases billed by an app store, a partner bundle or an employer/organisation: whose terms govern, who handles billing, cancellation and refunds, data shared with the store on refunds |
| taxes | Taxes | whether prices include tax, the user's tax duty, tax collected by the store |
| failed-payments | Failed and late payments | declined charges, retries and alternative methods, suspension or downgrade, liability for uncollected amounts, collection costs, late interest |
| billing-disputes | Billing errors and chargebacks | deadline to dispute a charge, deemed acceptance, raising issues before a chargeback, consequences of a chargeback |
| company-termination | Suspension and termination by the company | the company ending or suspending a subscription for breach or by discontinuing the offering, and any refund in that case |
| service-changes | Changes to included features | the right to add, change or remove paid features or content; no guarantee of availability; no reliance on future features |
| service-terms | Paid-feature terms of use | terms of use specific to the paid features: licence, usage limits (devices, streams, household), feature-specific disclaimers, content stored by the paid tier |
| shared-plans | Family and shared plans | family, household or group plans, extra members, sharing a subscription, roles of the paying holder |
| gifts | Gift subscriptions and gift cards | buying, redeeming and cancelling gift subscriptions, gift cards and giveaways |
| plan-changes | Upgrades, downgrades and plan switches | changing plan mid-term or at renewal, proration and credits |
| one-time-purchases | One-time and consumable purchases | in-app items, virtual currency, licensed content and other non-recurring purchases sold under the same terms |
| contact | Contact | where to ask about subscriptions or billing |
| legal-boilerplate | Notices and general clauses | electronic notices, severability, entire agreement, translations, conflict of laws, inside a separate subscription page |
| pause | Pausing a subscription | pausing instead of cancelling: maximum length, how often, access during the pause, reactivation and its charge (added at s25) |
| regional | Regional consumer notices | statutory withdrawal or cooling-off rights, state or country rules; goes to *Regional content*, not the root |

## Document level

- Placement: 19/30 part of the ToS (s01 s02 s03 s04 s05 s07 s08 s09 s12 s14 s16 s18 s20 s21
  s23 s24 s26 s27 s29); 11/30 a separate page (s06 s10 s11 s13 s15 s17 s19 s22 s25 s28 s30).
  By size, separate pages: large 6/10, mid 2/10, small 3/10. Two separate pages were reached
  through a pointer: s25 (the ToS section is one sentence linking it), s11 (a store
  "Cancellation policy" stub linking it).
- In the ToS: 13/19 put the part in the first third, right after accounts or registration; 8/19
  spread it over non-adjacent sections (s01 s04 s05 s09 s12 s20 s21 s26).
- Separate pages: 7/11 say they supplement or are incorporated into the ToS; 3/11 say they win
  on conflict.
- Length of the part: median about 1,190 words (185–23,000; s19 repeats paragraphs per plan,
  about 9,000 unique). ToS parts median 1,050; separate pages median 2,110.
- Layouts: key-points summary box at the top 2/30 (s01 s22); whole page a plain summary of the
  ToS 1/30 (s30); table of contents 2/30 (s13 s17); whole page mostly bullets 3/30 (s06 s17
  s30).
- Billing channels: store and direct 19/30; store only 5/30 (s03 s06 s09 s24 s30); direct only
  6/30 (s07 s17 s19 s20 s26 s29). Apple or Google named 20/30.
- Register: 13/30 plain consumer style, 17/30 formal contract style; 30/30 address the reader as
  "you"; 17/30 defined terms in quotes; 11/30 at least one all-caps sentence.
