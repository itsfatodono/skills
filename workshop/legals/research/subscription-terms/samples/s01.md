## Document
- Version taken: `/terms/us/en/` redirects to `/terms/intl/en/`; no separate US English version exists, so the intl English page (effective March 5, 2026) is the sample.
- Placement: part of ToS. Subscription content sits in four places: the summary box under the H1 (before section 1); section 8 "PURCHASES AND AUTOMATICALLY RENEWING SUBSCRIPTIONS" with 8a–8d (heading shown without its number), the core, roughly a third of the way in (section 8 of 22); section 21 "SPECIAL STATE TERMS"; and the Denmark/UK subsection of section 22 "SPECIAL COUNTRY TERMS". Passing mentions left out: section 1 (right to change features in subscription plans), section 9 (delete account does not cancel store billing; no refund on termination).
- Words: about 2,970 in the subscription part (summary 250, section 8 about 2,140, section 21 about 390, section 22 Denmark/UK about 190); whole page about 16,150.
- Billing channels: two named channels. "External Service" purchases (iTunes/Apple ID, Google Play, other authorised stores) are billed and cancelled through the store account and charged under the store's general terms; "Internal Purchases" are billed by the company to a card or other payment processor on the website or in the app. Apple handles Apple-ID refunds; outside the Americas internal payments may go to the Irish group entity.
- Shape: one long ToS page; H1 with effective date and PDF download; a bulleted summary box whose bullets are all subscription and refund points, plus contact and complaint addresses; numbered H2 sections with lettered H3 subsections; most sections open with a one-sentence plain summary in front of the full text (the page states the summaries are part of the agreement).
- Register: formal US consumer-contract style, "we/you", long sentences, defined terms in quotes ("External Service", "Internal Purchases", "Payment Method", "Virtual Item(s)"); all-caps for the virtual-items no-refund clause; regional rules as bold "For subscribers residing in …" labels.
- Recurring elements: postal cancellation address (P.O. Box, "Attn: Cancellations") repeated in the summary and in 8d; the statutory three-business-day cancellation wording repeated in the summary, 8d and 21; "access continues until the end of the then-current term" repeated in the intro, 8a and 8b; chargeback-may-terminate clause repeated in 8a and 8b; parallel structure between 8a (store) and 8b (direct); cross-references to sections 8d and 9.
- Coverage notes: no free-trial clause, no stated price-change notice period (renewal "at the price you agreed to", promo prices may rise after the first period), no plan upgrade/downgrade clause beyond "change or terminate" through the store; consumable in-app items (virtual items) handled inside the same part; a model withdrawal form is printed inline for Denmark/UK.
