- Register: plain consumer English, "we/you/Telegram", short-to-medium sentences; light marketing tone in the intro and feature sections; few defined terms (only "Premium Services" in quotes).

- Billing channels: payments go to Telegram via third-party payment processors for direct purchases; Apple App Store and Google Play purchases are cancelled through the store, governed further by the store's terms, and the billing relationship is with the store; giveaways also sold via Fragment and web versions, with prices that may differ by channel.
- Coverage notes: no free-trial or introductory-offer terms; no plan upgrade/downgrade terms; no regional consumer notices (no EU withdrawal right, no state auto-renewal law); refund stance is no refunds except prorated refund when Telegram discontinues subscriptions; gift subscriptions appear only inside the boosts and giveaway rules.
