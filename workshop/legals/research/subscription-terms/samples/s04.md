## Document
- Version taken: US English (provider Netflix, Inc., US arbitration agreement), from the Wayback capture of 2026-10-14. The live URL serves by fetcher location and returned the Netflix International B.V. version (Dutch law, no arbitration) with no locale switch to the US text, so the latest US-served capture was used. Both carry "Last Updated: April 10, 2026".
- Placement: part of ToS. Core is "2. Subscription Terms" (2.1–2.8), section 2 of 8 top-level sections, directly after the service description; "3. Extra Members" (3.1–3.2, paid add-on member billing and cancellation) follows it and is taken as part of it; "1.3. Offers" (promotional and introductory offers) sits inside "1. The Netflix Service" and is recorded as embedded.
- Words: 1264 for the subscription part (1.3, 2 and 3); about 8,900 for the whole Terms of Use page.
- Billing channels: Netflix bills the Payment Method on the account directly; a "Payment Method" may be the user's account with a third party, in which case billing details and cancellation (turning off auto-renew, unsubscribing) are handled through that third party. Third-party bundled subscriptions may carry different conditions disclosed at sign-up. Apple and Google are not named, and the part does not say store purchases are governed by the store's terms.
- Shape: single help-center page; H1 title, intro naming the provider, an all-caps arbitration warning, then numbered sections with decimal sub-clauses; each sub-clause opens with a bold-style run-in title ("2.6. Cancellation.") followed by a single paragraph; "Last Updated" date at the very end; no table of contents, no summary box, no tables.
- Register: plain consumer style, "we/you", medium sentences; defined terms in quotes inline ("Payment Method", "Account Owner", "Offers", "credits"); repeated pointers to the in-product "Account" page as the place to see dates, change payment, and cancel.
- Recurring elements: "Account" page as the self-service location; cross-reference by clause title ("see "Cancellation" below"); third-party-billing caveat repeated in the billing and cancellation clauses.
- Coverage notes: no free-trial clause (trials not offered); no plan list or prices on the page; no tax clause beyond taxes being charged with the fee; no state auto-renewal law notice; refunds handled as a flat no-refund rule plus discretionary credits; a separate passing line in "4. Account Access" says account holds earn no credit or discount (left out as a general section).
