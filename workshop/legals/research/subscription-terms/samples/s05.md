## Document
- Version taken: `/terms/` redirects to `/terms` and serves the Terms of Service for users outside the UK, Switzerland and EEA (US and rest of world), "Last Modified" April 9, 2026; a note at the top links a separate UK/CH/EEA version, which was not taken.
- Placement: part of ToS. The core is the H3 "Subscription and Payment Terms" (section 13 of 23 unnumbered H3 sections, just past the middle), followed much later by two short plan-type sections near the end: "Participation in Services Through Financial Wellness by YNAB Subscription" (section 20) and "Participation in Services through YNAB Together" (section 21). Passing mentions left out: the arbitration section's fee clauses and the Open Banking section.
- Words: about 1,050 in the subscription part (core section about 730, Wellness about 290, YNAB Together about 25); whole ToS about 7,000.
- Billing channels: the company bills a payment method the user provides (card, debit card or other) directly; purchases through "App Stores" (Apple App Store, Google Play named as examples) may need card details to start the trial, "different terms may apply", and must be cancelled through that store. Store terms are not named as governing; no store refund route is given. Employer-sponsored ("Partner") plans are paid by the Partner under its own agreement with the company.
- Shape: one long ToS page, H1, a residence-scope note, "Last Modified" date, then flat unnumbered H3 sections. The core subscription section is a run of 15 paragraphs, each opening with a bold run-in label ("Label: text"); some labels carry two or three paragraphs.
- Register: plain-leaning US consumer-contract style, "we/you", mostly short sentences; a few friendly touches ("While we don't want to see you go"); "sole discretion" reservations recur; defined terms in quotes only for "App Store" and, in the Wellness section, "Partner", "Group", "Wellness Plan".
- Recurring elements: support email repeated in cancellation and billing-dispute paragraphs; "continued use = acceptance" wording repeated in price changes and modification to services; reservation-of-right-to-change clauses repeated for plans, trials, prices and services; inline links to marketing/help pages (pricing, subscription sharing, free-trial page, cancellation policy, YNAB Together supplemental terms).
- Coverage notes: no stated trial length in the terms (only via the linked trial page; the Wellness section names a trial length for lapsed sponsored users); price-increase notice is "as required by law" with no period; refund clause names only monthly fees as non-refundable and is silent on annual plans; no upgrade/downgrade or proration clause; no regional consumer notices inside the subscription part (the New Jersey notice at the end concerns liability, not subscriptions); no gift-subscription terms despite a gift offer in the site navigation.
