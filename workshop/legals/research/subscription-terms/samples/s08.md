- Placement: part of ToS. Sections "4. Subscriptions and Promotional Offers" and "5. Other Payments Terms", sections 4–5 of 23 (followed by an 8-section "Global Addendum"). Subscription terms sit early, right after eligibility/account and before the licence, content and dispute sections.
- Words: 1123 for sections 4–5; about 8200 for the whole page (ToS plus Global Addendum).
- Billing channels: Calm bills directly through calm.com; for subscriptions bought "through a third-party, like an app store" the user must cancel the renewal with that third party. No statement that store purchases are governed by the store's terms, and Apple/Google are not named in the part (Apple appears only in the separate section 23 "iOS Minimum Terms for Custom EULA", which is outside the part). Promotional offers can be claimed only through calm.com. Section 5 covers all purchases (subscriptions, gift cards, other items), not only subscriptions.
- Shape: each of the two sections is an H2 with a lettered (a)–(g) / (a)–(d) list; every item opens with a bold label ("Recurring Subscriptions.", "Cancellation.") followed by one or two paragraphs. Section 4 opens with a one-sentence scope line defining the paid tier's product name.
- Register: US consumer-ToS style, "we/you", medium-length sentences, defined terms in quotes and bold ("Calm Premium", "Recurring Subscriptions", "Promotional Offer", "Offer Terms"); cross-references by section letter ("section 4(c)") rather than by name; "to the extent permitted by law" hedging is light in this part.
- Recurring elements: "Last Updated" date under the title; support email repeated (cancellation help, and elsewhere in the ToS); inline links to the web subscription-management page and a help-center cancellation article; "then-current price plus any applicable taxes" phrase repeated in renewal, promo conversion and payment method items; cancellation deadline stated twice (renewal and end of promo).
- Coverage notes: no fixed notice period for price changes (advance email only); no failed-payment suspension or retry wording beyond liability for uncollected amounts; no upgrade/downgrade terms; no gift subscriptions in the part (gift cards are pointed to a separate Gift Card Terms page from section 2); no statutory withdrawal right in this version. Subscription-related text outside the part: section 11 "Termination" lets Calm end a subscription obtained through an organization or third-party promotion when eligibility lapses, and says closing an account does not cancel subscriptions; Global Addendum section 2 gives a pro rata refund of the unused period when a service change materially disadvantages the user (stated as mandatory for Australia).
