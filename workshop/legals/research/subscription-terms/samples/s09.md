## Document
- Placement: part of ToS; three separate, non-adjacent H2 sections: "Free Trial" (section 1 of 14), "Payment & Refunds" (3 of 14) and "Cancellation" (5 of 14), with "Right to Refuse Service" and "New Features and Modifications to Terms of Service" between them. Version taken: the default English page at flighty.com/legal/terms (no locale split), "Last updated October 22, 2026".
- Words: subscription part about 185 (the three sections' bodies); whole ToS body about 3,000, excluding site navigation and footer.
- Billing channels: purchases go through the Apple App Store or "another app marketplace or payment provider made available by Flighty" (defined as "App Marketplace"); no direct billing by Flighty is described and Google Play is not named. Prices, renewals, cancellations and refunds "may also be subject to" the marketplace's terms, and where a marketplace processes the purchase it, not Flighty, handles payment and refund requests under its own policies. Cancellation is done through the marketplace's own cancellation feature.
- Shape: very short subscription content scattered across a long, generic ToS; each section is one paragraph (the trial section is one sentence); no lists, no tables, no prices, no plan names.
- Register: plain-to-formal, "you/we" with a defined-term alias ("App Marketplace"); several hedges deferring to "applicable law" and marketplace terms; the surrounding ToS is boilerplate with all-caps blocks and an odd "Administration" alias for the company.
- Recurring elements: "Last updated" date under the title; the defined term "App Marketplace" reused across Payment, Cancellation and the separate "Additional Terms for App Marketplace Users" section; repeated carve-outs for rights that cannot be excluded by law.
- Coverage notes: no auto-renewal statement, no renewal period or billing-date terms, no trial length or trial-to-paid conversion terms, no price-change notice (only a right to change rates), no plan descriptions, no upgrades/downgrades, taxes, failed payments, family sharing or gift-subscription terms (the site menu sells gift cards, but the ToS does not cover them). Left out as general ToS: "New Features and Modifications" (ties new terms' effect to the next App Store bill payment), "Additional Terms for App Marketplace Users" (standard Apple licence clauses, incl. Apple refunding the price for warranty failure), "Terminations of Terms", and "European Economic Area and United Kingdom Consumers" (a general clause noting statutory withdrawal and refund rights may also apply).
