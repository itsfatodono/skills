## Document
- Shape: one long Terms & Conditions page (Webflow), H1 title, subtitle naming the services and the company, "Last updated" month/year, then 11 numbered H2 sections with parenthesised clause numbers ((4.1), (4.2.1)); some clauses carry an inline bold sub-heading run into the text; no summary box, no table of contents, no tables. English version taken (site default `x-default`/`en`; German version at /de/terms).
- Register: German-law contract style translated into English, "KptnCook GmbH"/"you", long sentences, legal terms of art ("invitatio ad offerendum", "binding offer", "good cause", "set-off and right of retention"); defined terms in quotes and parentheses ("Subscription Access", "Trial Subscription", "Initial Term", "Web Checkout").
- Recurring elements: purchase-channel split repeated in almost every clause (Apple/Google in-app vs KptnCook website); "will be displayed to you before you purchase" used instead of stating prices, terms or periods; "Mandatory statutory rights remain unaffected" closing several clauses; cross-references by section number (Section 9, Section 4.3, Section 6); support email and postal address repeated in the withdrawal text.
- Placement: part of ToS. Sections "4. Paid subscriptions and free trial subscriptions", "6. Right of withdrawal", "7. Fee and payment terms, set-off and right of retention", "8. Gift Cards" and clause (9.2) of "9. Term and Termination" — sections 4 and 6–9 of 11; section 5 (liability) sits between them and is left out, as are (9.1) free account and (9.3) termination for good cause.
- Words: subscription part about 2,060; whole page about 5,000.
- Billing channels: subscriptions sold as in-app purchases through Apple App Store and Google Play, and through the company's own website (web checkout, payment processor named); store purchases are billed by the store, the store's terms and payment terms "additionally apply", and store refunds/withdrawals go through the store's procedures; for web purchases the company stays the contract partner and handles cancellation and withdrawal itself. Gift cards are redeemable only on the website. (1.2) in the Scope section also states that store terms additionally apply.
- Coverage notes: German consumer-law shape — full statutory withdrawal instructions and model form, German-resident consumers get indefinite continuation with monthly notice after the initial term (Fair Consumer Contracts rule) and pro-rata refund, everyone else gets plain auto-renewal; no price-change clause, no failed-payment/suspension clause, no plan upgrade/downgrade clause, no tax clause, no named prices or plan names (except "Premium or Pro" as gift-card examples).
