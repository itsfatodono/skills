## Document
- Shape: separate page; H1 title with a Print control, "Last Updated" date and a link to the previous version, then 8 H2 sections. The second H2 ("Quick Reference") is an in-page table of contents. Each later H2 is a bullet list of bolded run-in labels ("Label. text"). Subscription terms sit under one nested label ("Subscription Service") inside "Payment Methods". No summary box, no tables. The site's legal sidebar lists the page next to Terms of Service, District Terms and the other legal pages.
- Register: formal US law-firm style, "we/you/ClassDojo", long sentences with defined terms in quotes ("Premium Features", "Payment Method", "Subscription Service", "Subscription Billing Date", "Subscription Fee"); heavy reservation-of-rights wording ("at our sole discretion", "with or without prior notice"). Despite the page name, it covers all real-money purchases, not only subscriptions.
- Recurring elements: "Last Updated" date and previous-version link under the title; incorporation into the general Terms of Service, with terms not defined here taking their ToS meaning; links to the Privacy Policy, the HelpDesk and a help-centre cancellation article; one postal address for legal notices; "sole discretion" reservations repeated in the purchase and actions sections.
- Placement: separate page, "Premium Features Terms"; the whole page is the part, and it is incorporated into the ToS.
- Words: 2113 for the part (title through "Additional Terms"); the whole page, including legal-nav sidebar and footer, is about 2430.
- Billing channels: ClassDojo bills directly through its designated payment processor. The listed payment methods include cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and bank accounts. The page does not mention App Store or Google Play in-app purchases, and does not say that store purchases follow the store's terms.
- Coverage notes: no free-trial or introductory-offer terms (promotional offers appear only as a discretionary right); no plan upgrade/downgrade terms; no named plan tiers or renewal periods; refunds are covered only by a blanket "non-refundable" line and a discretionary refund after a dispute; the only regional notice is an EU waiver of the right to cancel; no US state auto-renewal law notices; cancellation steps are a pointer to a help article.
