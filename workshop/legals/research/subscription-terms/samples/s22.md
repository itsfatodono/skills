- Placement: separate page; the whole page is the part ("Pandora Subscription Terms"). It is a supplement to the Terms of Use: the ToS and Privacy Policy are incorporated by reference, and disputes, liability and arbitration are left to the ToS.
- Words: 2641 for the document text; about 2670 for the whole page including the legal-hub navigation.
- Register: formal US contract style, "we/you", long sentences; many capitalised defined terms introduced in quotes inline ("Subscription Services", "Subscription Product", "Access Period", "Fee", "Applicable Taxes", "External Service", "Eligibility Requirements"); summary box is plainer and shorter.

- Billing channels: Pandora bills directly to the payment method on file and transfers billing data to its payment processor(s); purchases through External Services (Apple, Google Play, Google, Amazon, Roku named as examples) must be managed and cancelled with that service's tools, may carry that service's additional terms, and the service's refund policy may differ; user must cancel a store subscription themselves to avoid duplicate charges when buying direct.
- Coverage notes: product types beyond subscriptions (one-off Passes, Gift Passes, Trials as single-use passes that convert); 30-day emailed notice before price or product changes; no concrete prices or periods (disclosed at purchase); no regional consumer notices (refunds only "where required by law"); no failed-payment/suspension mechanics beyond a duty to keep payment info current; issuer entity named only for the US.
