## Document
- Shape: one-page Terms of Use. H1 title, "Last updated" date, a 3-paragraph acceptance intro, then 15 flat H2 sections with no numbering, no table of contents and no summary box. The subscription part is a run of six short H2 sections near the top.
- Register: generic online-template style (TermsFeed-like headings and wording: "Billing Cycle", "Free Trial", "Fee Changes"), "we/you" mixed with the company named in full in the third person, and capitalised defined terms in quotes. Newer, plainer sentences about the app stores and lifetime purchases have been added into the template text.
- Recurring elements: defined terms introduced in parentheses ("Store", "Subscriptions", "Billing Cycle", "One-time Purchase", "Free Trial"); repeated statements that the company cannot cancel, charge or refund and that the Store handles it; "sole discretion" reservations; a support email in the closing Contact Us section only.
- Placement: part of ToS. Sections "Purchases", "Subscriptions", "One-time Purchases", "Free Trial", "Fee Changes" and "Refunds" are sections 1–6 of 15, right after the intro. General sections (termination, liability, governing law, changes) follow and are not extracted.
- Words: 612 in the subscription part; 1,571 for the whole page.
- Billing channels: app stores only (Apple App Store and Google Play, defined together as the "Store"). The Store takes payment, holds billing data, handles failed payments, cancellations and refunds. Refunds follow the Store's own refund policy, but there is no general statement that store terms govern the purchase. The company says it never sees payment details and cannot act on a purchase.
- Coverage notes: covers subscriptions and lifetime one-time purchases side by side. No named plans or prices, no plan upgrade/downgrade, no tax, no regional consumer notice (only "except when required by law"), no family/gift plans, no link to store cancellation help (a pointer is implied but not given). The footer copyright line is in Korean, which looks like a template or localisation leftover.
