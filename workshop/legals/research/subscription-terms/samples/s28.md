## Document
- Shape: single page inside the YouTube legal hub (left nav: Terms of Service, Paid Service Terms, Usage Rules, Collecting Society Notices, Copyright, Community Guidelines). H1 title, "Effective as of" date with a "view previous version" link, a language switcher (English, Turkish), a one-line welcome, then two stacked documents: the general Paid Service Terms (8 numbered sections with decimal subsections) and "Additional Terms for YouTube Premium, YouTube Music Premium and YouTube Premium Lite" (9 numbered sections, own numbering restarting at 1). No summary box, no table of contents, no tables.
- Register: plain-ish corporate, "we/you" with "Google" used interchangeably; short-to-medium sentences; defined terms in quotes ("Paid Service", "Affiliates", "Terms", "Virtual Items", "Premium Service Trials"); examples given in brackets ("e.g.", "such as").
- Recurring elements: effective date plus archived previous version; many "Learn more" links to Help Center articles after a clause; repeated pointers to the Refund Policy help page; layered-document precedence clauses (Paid Service Terms over the main ToS; Premium Terms over Paid Service Terms); cross-references by section name ("Section 4 (Cancellation of Subscriptions)").
- Placement: separate page, "YouTube Paid Service Terms of Service" plus the appended "Additional Terms for YouTube Premium, YouTube Music Premium and YouTube Premium Lite (and accepted payment methods)". Covers all paid services (rentals, purchases, channel memberships, virtual items, subscriptions), not only subscriptions; the whole page is taken as the part.
- Words: the whole page body is about 4,130 words (Paid Service Terms about 2,890, Premium Additional Terms about 1,240); the page chrome adds about 30.
- Billing channels: payments are taken by a named Irish group billing entity (with its VAT number), distinct from the US service provider; purchases made through another platform (Apple, Google Play named as examples) are charged by that platform, which also handles payment issues including refunds; promotions may vary by billing platform. No statement that store purchases are governed by the store's own terms beyond that platform responsibility.
