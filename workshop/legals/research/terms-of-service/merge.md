# terms-of-service: merged section list

All 30 samples. Row-level mapping: `section-map.md`. Formats per `build.md` step 3: `prose`,
`list`, `table`, plus the layout marks `pointer` (links to another page) and `embedded` (folded
into another section, no heading of its own).

## Section ids

Meanings and format words were fixed after mapping the first batch (s01–s15) and before judging
s16 onward. Changes made at merge time are listed under *Folds*.

| Id | Meaning |
|---|---|
| intro | Opening: who offers the service, what the terms cover, binding contract, how acceptance happens; definitions blocks and descriptions of what the product does (folded from `service-description`); contracting entity by user's region. |
| incorporated-terms | Other documents that form part of the terms (policies, supplemental or product-specific terms) and which wins on conflict. |
| eligibility | Who may use the service: minimum age, parental consent, capacity, acting for an organisation, sanctions-list or residence conditions, children's data. |
| account | Registration, accurate information, credential security, responsibility for account activity, transfers, organisation or managed accounts, usernames, identity checks. |
| privacy | Pointer to the privacy policy and any statement about data handling made in the terms. |
| licence | Licence granted to the user to use the service or app; software restrictions, updates, open-source components. |
| acceptable-use | Prohibited conduct and content, permitted use, commercial-use and competitor bans; pointers to an AUP or community guidelines. |
| user-content | Ownership of user content, licence granted to the company and other users, user warranties about content, visibility. |
| content-moderation | Monitoring and removal rights, responsibility for others' content, reporting channels, appeals, preservation and disclosure. |
| ai-features | AI inputs and outputs: definitions, accuracy, ownership of outputs, prohibited AI uses, vendors, agents acting for the user. |
| feedback | Feedback and suggestions and the company's right to use them. |
| company-ip | Company ownership of the service, trademarks, reserved rights, linking rules, usage data derived from use (folded from `usage-data`). |
| copyright-complaints | Infringement notices, counter-notices, designated contact, repeat infringers. |
| communications | Texts, calls, push and email from the company; consent, opt-out, carrier charges, electronic notices. |
| fees-payment | Fees, billing, payment methods and processors, taxes, price changes, failed payments, chargebacks. |
| subscriptions | Plans, automatic renewal, cancellation, free trials, lifetime plans, upgrades and downgrades. |
| refunds | Refund policy, discretionary refunds, app-store refunds, pro-rata refunds. |
| promotions | Referral programmes, promo codes, gift cards, contests and sweepstakes. |
| app-stores | App-store distribution: Apple and Google required clauses, store-governed billing and refunds. |
| feature-terms | Rules for one product-specific feature or business line (orders and delivery, financial products, host or creator roles, virtual items, clubs). |
| beta-features | Beta, preview or pre-release features. |
| third-party-services | Third-party links, integrations, products and ads; no endorsement or liability. |
| user-interactions | Dealings and disputes between users; release of the company. |
| export-sanctions | Export controls, sanctions, use from other countries, local-law compliance. |
| service-changes | Changing, suspending or discontinuing the service or features; downtime. |
| terms-changes | How the terms change, notice, acceptance of changes. |
| termination | Suspension and termination by the company and by the user, inactive accounts, effect of termination, survival. |
| account-closure | How the user closes or deletes the account and what happens to the content. |
| disclaimers | Warranty disclaimers, product-specific disclaimers (not advice, physical risk), data-loss disclaimers. |
| liability | Limitation of liability: excluded damages, caps, carve-outs, sole remedy. |
| indemnity | User's indemnity and defence mechanics. |
| dispute-resolution | Informal resolution, arbitration, class and jury waivers, opt-outs, small claims, limitation period, equitable relief. |
| governing-law | Governing law and courts. |
| general | Boilerplate: entire agreement, severability, waiver, assignment, relationship, third-party beneficiaries, notices, force majeure, interpretation, language. |
| contact | How to reach the company about the terms. |

## Document level

- Length: median about 6,500 words, range 166 (s15) to 51,850 (s07). By size third: large median
  about 8,200, mid about 11,400, small about 2,700.
- Numbered sections 16/30; table of contents 6/30 (s01 s06 s07 s14 s22 s25); per-section
  plain-language summaries 4/30 (s07 s10 s25 s26); a whole-document summary 1/30 (s10); a summary of
  the latest changes 2/30 (s02 s15).
- Date line ("Last updated" or "Effective") 27/30; none in s09 s18, a change log instead in s15.
  Link to the previous version 3/30 (s10 s11 s20).
- All-caps blocks (warranty, liability, arbitration) 24/30; none in s02 s09 s15 s18 s21 s25.
- Arbitration notice at the top 16/30.
- Defined terms set inline 27/30; a definitions block 3/30 (s09 s13 s29).
- Several versions or regional appendices on one page 6/30 (s08 s10 s16 s19 s25 s26).
- Register: "we/you" 28/30, often mixed with the brand name in the third person; plain or
  conversational 8/30 (s01 s02 s10 s11 s18 s19 s25 s26); US-counsel formal drafting about 15/30;
  generator-style template wording 4/30 (s03 s09 s21 s27).
- Closing contact section 17/30; 10/30 give contact details only inside other clauses (copyright
  agent, Apple clause) or not at all.
- Missing as a whole: an account-deletion procedure 12/30; arbitration 11/30; a liability cap
  4/30; a table of contents 24/30.
