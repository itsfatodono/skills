## Document
- Shape: one page inside a "Legal center" hub; metadata panel (effective date, type, audience,
  language, version-history link) and a "Download PDF" link above the text; a 13-item table of
  contents; 13 numbered sections, most split by bold run-in sub-headings ("Minimum age.",
  "Fees and billing."). Version taken: the default English page (the HTML also carries other
  language versions; only English was read). No summary box.
- Register: plain and friendly in the narrative parts ("Welcome to Anthropic!", "Our team works
  hard..."), "we/you", short sentences; switches to formal contract language for warranty,
  liability and indemnity.
- Recurring elements: effective date at the top; defined terms inline in bold with quotes
  (Terms, Services, Inputs, Outputs, Actions, Materials, Providers, Subscription, App
  Distributor) rather than a definitions block; all-caps blocks for warranty, liability and
  indemnity, introduced by a plain-language sentence explaining why caps are used; no
  arbitration notice at the top; support email repeated in-line (no contact block at the end);
  links to Acceptable Use Policy, Privacy Policy, Commercial Terms, Supported Regions Policy,
  pricing page, help-center cancellation article and T&S Support Center.
- Opening: says the terms cover the consumer apps and sites, that the contract is with the
  company (and not its Providers), that the Acceptable Use Policy is part of the terms, and that
  access means acceptance; points to the Privacy Policy, and to separate Commercial Terms for
  API/console use.
- Referenced but missing: §6 cites a model cancellation form in "Appendix 1", which appears on
  neither the web page nor the PDF.
