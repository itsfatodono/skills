## Document
- Shape: hub page "Snap Terms of Service" with a tab switcher (Summary, Snap Inc. Terms, Snap Group Limited Terms). A bulleted plain-language summary of the whole terms sits on top, followed by a two-line chooser: US residents/businesses agree to the Snap Inc. terms, everyone else to the Snap Group Limited terms. Extracted: the Snap Inc. (US) terms only, the version for the default en-US page and the company in the candidate row. 26 numbered sections; section 20 has lettered sub-headings a–m. No table of contents in the terms body.
- Register: deliberately informal, "we/you", contractions and light humour ("lifelong Snapchatter", "free of annoyances"); the intro admits some parts still read like a contract. Legal passages (disclaimers, liability, arbitration, jury and class waivers) switch to all caps.
- Recurring elements: banner naming the new effective date and linking the prior version, which stays in force until then; "Effective:" date at the top of the terms; an "In summary:" paragraph closing most sections (sections 4, 21–26 have none), with a note in the intro that summaries are for convenience only; all-caps arbitration notice at the top of the terms; defined terms introduced inline in quotes ("Services", "Terms", "Public Content", "AI Features", "Inputs", "Outputs", "Third-Party Materials", "Messages"), no definitions block; survival list by section number inside the termination section; postal address of the company at the end; many links to companion pages (Privacy Policy, Privacy Safety and Policy Hub, Support Site, Terms & Policies page, Community Guidelines, Moderation Enforcement and Appeals Explainer, Transparency Hub, brand guidelines, patents page).
- Companion version: a separate Snap Group Limited Terms of Service on the same page for users outside the US (not extracted).
