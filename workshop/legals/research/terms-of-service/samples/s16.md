## Document
- Shape: a Help Center article that holds three full versions one after another (European Users; Users outside of the EEA, UK, and Australia; Australian Users), preceded by a three-line selector that says which version applies by country of residence or establishment. Version taken: "Terms of Service for Users outside of the EEA, UK, and Australia", the version the default en-US page assigns to a US user (the page splits by EEA/Switzerland/UK, Australia and everyone else, not by US vs non-US). That version opens with a bold arbitration notice, the last-updated date, a bulleted list of incorporated policies and two defining paragraphs, then a linked table of contents grouped under three H3 labels (Guest Terms, Host Terms, General Terms); 28 numbered H2 sections, most split into numbered H3 sub-sections (1.1, 23.17); ends with Schedule 1, a set of per-country three-column tables of contracting entities.
- Register: plain-language marketplace terms, "we/you", fairly short sentences in the guest and host parts ("It's easy to create a Listing"), shifting to dense US litigation drafting in the arbitration section; capitalised defined terms set in bold inside quotes and parentheses throughout; examples introduced with "For example" and "This means".
- Recurring elements: "Last Updated" date at the top and named in the modification clause; arbitration and class-waiver notice at the top (bold) and repeated in all caps at the start of the arbitration section; bold (not all-caps) blocks for assumption of risk, warranty disclaimer, liability and indemnity; inline cross-references by section number ("see Section 15"); per-country sections for the US, China, Brazil, rest of world and Canada; postal addresses for the agent for service and the arbitration opt-out, plus an opt-out email; contact by a terms email link; many links out to Help Center policy articles, the AAA and ADR Services sites, the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, Google Maps terms and Apple's standard EULA.
- Incorporated documents: Payments Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Service Fees Policy, pricing and payment transparency policy, host-imposed contracts policy, Taxes Policy, Host Privacy Standards, Additional Terms for Service and Experience Hosts, cancellation policies (homes; services and experiences), Major Disruptive Events Policy, rebooking and refund policies, Resolution Center, Host Damage Protection Terms, Reviews Policy, Community Policies, Content Policy, Nondiscrimination Policy, Services and Experiences Standards, parties and events rules, Trademark Guidelines, Copyright Policy, SMS Terms, Standards pages, every page on the "Additional Legal Terms and Policies" hub (by reference), Google Maps/Earth Additional Terms, Apple's Licensed Application EULA.
