## Document
- Shape: one HubSpot-served HTML page titled "Reddit User Agreement" holding two full parallel versions behind a region selector at the top: one for the United States and everywhere outside the EEA, UK and Switzerland, one for the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Each version has an unheaded introduction and numbered H5 sections (18 in the US/rest-of-world version, 17 in the EEA/UK/CH version, which drops the governing-law section and merges disclaimers into a short liability clause). No table of contents inside the text; a sidebar lists past versions by date (archive back to 2020) and other Reddit policies; a language switcher offers about 30 locales. Version taken: default English, the US/rest-of-world version (word count is that version only, about 3,600 words in the EEA one); rows 44-50 record where the EEA/UK/CH version departs from it.
- Register: plain and short for a large platform; "we/you" with "Reddit" in the third person; a friendly hook ("Hello, redditors and people of the Internet!", "Reddit is for fun"); few defined terms, set in bold inside quotes; lawyerly only in the all-caps liability block.
- Recurring elements: "Effective [date]. Last Revised [date]" line at the top, above a bold one-sentence tagline; defined terms inline (Terms, Services, Content, Your Content, Third-Party Content, Promotion, Paid Services, Materials, Reddit Entities, Account, Additional Terms); all-caps warranty and liability paragraphs only; no arbitration clause and no class waiver anywhere; many links out (Privacy Policy, Reddit Rules, Moderator Code of Conduct, Help Center articles on age, personalization, moderation appeals, Public Content Policy, copyright and trademark report forms, 17 U.S.C. § 512, Brand Guidelines, help-request form); postal address blocks for the copyright agent and for the contracting entity at the end; a security-report email address.
- Incorporated documents: Privacy Policy (linked), Reddit Rules (incorporated by reference), Moderator Code of Conduct (binding on moderators), Additional Terms incorporated by reference and prevailing on conflict: Econ Terms (paid services and digital goods), Previews Terms, Advertising Platform Terms, Developer Terms, Earn Terms; Public Content Policy and Brand Guidelines linked; robots.txt named as the crawl permission.
